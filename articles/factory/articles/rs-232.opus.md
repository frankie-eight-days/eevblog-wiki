# rs 232

RS-232 is an asynchronous serial interface standard defined by its own bipolar signalling levels, carried over a UART and terminated at each end by a dedicated line driver/receiver chip.[1061][587][710] It became the default control and data path for test instruments, industrial equipment, point-of-sale hardware and personal computers, and survives in current gear long after USB and Ethernet displaced it for bulk data.[655][1023][1693] Its persistence is largely practical: an asynchronous UART link is trivially cheap to isolate, easy to decode, and needs no host-side stack, which are exactly the properties that matter in an instrument or a service port.[1032][478]

## Signalling levels

RS-232 uses bipolar levels rather than logic levels; measured at a transmitting port the idle and mark states sit at roughly plus and minus five and a half volts.[1061] A logic-level UART pin is therefore not an RS-232 port, and a TTL-only tool such as a bus pirate cannot be connected directly to one — the signal must pass through a transceiver in either direction.[485][CK5nbC_dBWk] The reverse trap is equally common: a serial header found on a PCB is usually at TTL levels and must not be wired to a PC's serial port without conversion.[977] Adapter boards that handle both cases exist, with a switch selecting RS-232 mode or TTL mode and, in TTL mode, a further choice of 5 V or 3.3 V thresholds.[680][977]

The requirement for a negative supply is a visible consequence of the standard. On equipment where nothing else needs it, a negative 12 V regulator with only a token heatsink is usually present solely to feed the RS-232 driver.[1364]

When the object is to read data rather than to conform to the standard, the level converter is a layer to be bypassed. Tapping the serial data inside an instrument ahead of its RS-232 level converter yields the same stream at logic level, avoiding the need to convert it back again.[6M-xXEn1_iI]

## Clocking and baud rate accuracy

Asynchronous framing has no clock line, so both ends must agree on bit period from their own timebases. As a rule of thumb, RS-232 comms require a frequency error of 1% or better.[232] This is the reason a microcontroller's internal RC oscillator is generally unsuitable: although some parts can be software-trimmed to 1% or better, and PIC parts have historically been factory-trimmed to that figure, trimmed accuracy does not hold up over temperature.[232][238] The usual resolution is to spend two pins on an external crystal or ceramic resonator.[232][238]

The same arithmetic explains oddball clock frequencies in older designs. 3.6864 MHz is common precisely because it divides evenly into the standard RS-232 baud rates including 9600.[334]

Rates encountered in the field span the full historical range: 300 baud in early portable machines with built-in modems,[116] 38.4 k on an 8251 UART,[1662] 9600 as the default on instruments and the rate at which a bit period is 104 µs,[1691][297] and 115200 on modern embedded debug ports, occasionally printed on the panel next to the connector.[977][1289] Instrument software that fails to connect is frequently a baud rate or interface-selection mismatch rather than a fault; on one electronic load the communications menu defaulted to RS232C and had to be switched to USBTMC before the host would identify the instrument at all.[862]

## Drivers, receivers and connectors

The transceiver is the identifying feature of an RS-232 port on a board. Parts encountered include the MAX3232 on a ten-pin header,[485] the MAX202 alongside an FTDI FT232 on a combined serial/USB board,[440] the DS14C232,[710] the ICL3221 as a 232-equivalent,[587] a Linear Technology LT1137,[639] and older Intersil silicon in point-of-sale equipment.[641] Some processors need no external UART at all — a Hitachi HD6303 with a built-in serial communications interface drives the RS-232 section directly.[791] Differential line drivers such as dual AM26LS30s appear where the interface hardware is segmented onto its own module in a large systems design.[598]

Connectors are split between the 25-pin D and the later 9-pin D, with the larger shell a reliable age marker on an instrument.[1554][955][cgCFNi8CekE] In DTE pinout, pin 2 is transmit and pin 7 is a ground reference.[1061] A straight cable between two devices that both present as DTE will produce no traffic whatever; a null modem or crossover cable is required, and mislabelled cables are a common cause of an apparently dead port.[1225]

## Isolation and safety

RS-232 over a simple UART can be isolated with an off-the-shelf protocol isolator that carries the link across a barrier into the instrument's processing, which is why floating-front-end architectures worked in the era of GPIB and RS-232 and stopped working with USB and LAN — those require processing right next to the interface because they are grounded.[1032] Isolated RS-232 is accordingly common on bench instruments: isolated cables shipped with electronic loads,[281] an isolated RS-232 output on a bench multimeter,[791] RS-232 drivers on the digital side of an isolation slot away from the ADC input so the instrument can be safely connected to a PC,[478] and optocoupled comms with the entire interface section — Ethernet, USB, logic and RS-232 — referenced to its own supply and isolated from the outputs.[511]

Handheld multimeters are the case where a galvanic serial port is simply unacceptable. Electrical continuity between a serial connector and the input jacks creates a safety hazard, which is why virtually no multimeter carries a direct RS-232 or USB connection and instead uses an optical or infrared link, with the isolation living in the adapter.[Xg_niU86bhI][171][1378] Where the interface is only a single IR LED it is transmit-only; a second LED for receive allows commands and, potentially, firmware updates back into the meter.[229][234]

The optical approach also produces a hardware distinction between otherwise identical models. Two meters sharing the same case and the same IR window can differ only in whether the serial output is populated and enabled — with the transparent holes present in both, the difference reduced to an unpopulated IR LED pad and firmware that never drives the UART pins.[5v8qX9PrjnA][912] Not all such protocols are conventional: rather than emitting a measurement as an ASCII string, one 6000-count meter protocol transmits the LCD segment digits and leaves the host to map them.[5v8qX9PrjnA]

## Instrument remote control

On test equipment RS-232 typically appears beside GPIB as the cheap alternative to it, on signal generators,[261] source measure units,[718] LCR and combiscope-class instruments,[559][1450] THD multimeters,[478] AC power sources,[1698] power meters,[1693] and bench supplies.[655][8] Full remote control over the RS-232 interface is normal even where the instrument's other functions are stripped down.[1450] The interface is sometimes hardware-fitted but licence-locked: a supply may ship with the RS-232, LAN and digital IO hardware present and inert until a software key is installed.[509][511]

Its absence on a nominally replacement instrument is conspicuous. When a bench multimeter was positioned as a successor to a model that had offered RS-232, the newer instrument shipped with LAN and USB only, despite an RS-232 header and a MAX3232 being present on its front-panel board.[485]

Consumer and professional AV equipment carries the interface for automation rather than for data: an RS-232C port for servicing and remote operation on a television, with the full command set — channel changes, power on and off — documented in the consumer manual rather than the service manual,[1154] control ports on an HD DVD player intended for studio automation,[1011] and RS-232 control alongside 3D sync on a large projector.[1546][278]

## Debug and boot ports

Equipment running an embedded operating system very commonly exposes a debug serial interface or RS-232-style terminal that emits boot information, and locating it is the standard entry point for examining or modifying such a device.[977] The port is sometimes labelled with a serial, debug, TX or RX marking and sometimes not; a four-pin header near the processor is a likely candidate.[977][CK5nbC_dBWk] Confirmation is not guaranteed — a port with a nonstandard pinout may not be a serial debug port at all,[731] and a genuine RS-232 service interface may simply produce nothing useful on boot, with no way to interrupt the sequence into a bootloader.[1154][1225] Where the digital board's ground is not tied to mains earth, probing with a mains-referenced oscilloscope is hazardous.[731]

## Decoding and triggering

An oscilloscope with deep memory has largely absorbed the traditional logic analyser role for serial protocols such as SPI, I²C and RS-232.[44] Decoding starts by measuring the period between two transitions to establish the bit rate, then configuring the bus as UART/RS-232 with a threshold, a data width, parity, stop bits and an idle polarity — almost always eight data bits, no parity and one stop bit, idling high.[977] Because the framing is generic, any signal with matching timing can be decoded by the same engine: the Canon LANC bus, with a 104 µs bit period identical to RS-232 at 9600, can be captured with a scope's UART decoder.[297]

Serial decode and trigger are near-universally sold as paid options rather than included, on entry-level and mid-range instruments alike,[704][703][1146] and vendors frequently unbundle UART/RS-232 from I²C, SPI, CAN and LIN so each must be bought separately.[CMoBGGqojqs][1501] Option pricing has reached four figures for basic RS-232 and RS-485 trigger and analysis on a mixed-domain scope.[199] Capability at the top end extends to 20 Mbaud decode.[1146] Software logic analysers handle multiple simultaneous asynchronous serial buses limited only by channel count, since decoding happens after capture.[436]

## USB adapters and host-side software

Most modern hosts reach RS-232 equipment through a USB-to-serial bridge, generally an FTDI part, cheap enough to be treated as disposable and used as the programming and comms path on countless boards.[45][230][440][1331][155] Any off-the-shelf FTDI or RS-232 lead can often be pressed into service in place of a vendor cable.[229] The significant failure mode is driver-level: a Prolific-based isolated adapter can present a Windows code 10 error because the vendor modified its drivers to render counterfeit chipsets unusable.[1225]

Host software is a recurring weak point. Vendor applications may require a VISA layer that conflicts with existing installations,[1691] and may fail to expose modes the instrument itself supports — an electronic load capable of constant-power discharge could not log or export that data through the supplied software despite a working RS-232 port and isolated cable.[774] Where the software does match the hardware, RS-232 or USB control makes automated battery discharge curves and capacity calculation routine.[393]

## Related standards and practical limits

RS-232 is single-ended and short-haul. RS-422 uses differential pairs and is preferred for long runs in professional installations; a video effects processor carries mostly RS-422 with some RS-232 alongside it.[598][738] RS-485 covers multi-drop industrial links, and combined boards offering 232, 422 and 485 together are the natural product for industrial automation.[833][8][1760] Half-duplex RS-232 has been used as a servo bus for position feedback on multi-axis robots, though a differential standard would be the better choice.[614]

The choice between them can be forced by connector allocation rather than by signalling. A hybrid solar inverter with a single RS-232 port could not simultaneously accept its Wi-Fi dongle — the only source of a network time sync for a real-time clock losing minutes per day — and feed a Raspberry Pi based monitoring system; moving the monitoring link to RS-485, which shared a physical connector with the CAN bus, freed the port and resolved the clock fault.[4vY5-obAx6w][HctGMxWPWRE]

RS-232-class data rates are low enough to be undemanding of layout. On a flex PCB carrying an RS-232-rate link, the pair should still be routed together as a pair, but controlled impedance, differential length matching and the associated detail work are unnecessary.[1262]
