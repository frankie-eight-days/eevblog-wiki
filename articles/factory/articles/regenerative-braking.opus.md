# regenerative braking

Regenerative braking is the use of an electric vehicle's traction motor as a generator during deceleration, so that the vehicle's kinetic energy is returned to the battery instead of being dissipated.[7nUgmEuyKIU] In an internal combustion vehicle the same energy is converted to heat in the friction brakes and lost entirely.[7nUgmEuyKIU][1600] The practical consequences are twofold: measurably extended range, and friction brakes that are barely used across the life of the vehicle.[7nUgmEuyKIU][5IV_oRAupeo][1339]

## Operating principle and the coasting alternative

When the accelerator is released, the motor is driven by the wheels and current flows back into the pack.[7nUgmEuyKIU] The instrument cluster represents this on a bidirectional bar graph: bars in the positive direction mean energy is leaving the battery under acceleration, and bars in the negative direction mean charge is being returned, whether from braking or from a descent.[7nUgmEuyKIU][5rZk8mNvYUc] Some implementations also report the recovered energy in range terms, for instance several hundred metres of range added at a single stop.[h9MvrP9JDrs] On the Mitsubishi iMiEV the equivalent function is selected by moving the gear lever from eco into B, which drives the econo gauge into the charge region on a descent, though on a shallow hill the change in deceleration is barely perceptible.[179]

The complement to regeneration is coasting. Turning regeneration off entirely leaves the vehicle to roll on when the accelerator is released, losing energy only to tyre rolling resistance, wheel bearings and aerodynamic drag.[7nUgmEuyKIU][1432] In a low-drag body — the IONIQ's drag coefficient is around 0.23 — there is very little left to slow the car, so it simply keeps going.[7nUgmEuyKIU] Which of the two is preferable is scenario-dependent rather than settled, which is why the comparison is worth measuring rather than assuming: regeneration against coasting, over a mountainous route and over ordinary flat suburban driving.[7nUgmEuyKIU]

## Levels, selection and driver control

Regeneration strength is a driver-selectable quantity. The Hyundai IONIQ exposes it through steering-wheel paddles: pulling the right paddle enters a fixed manual mode with discrete levels, of which level zero is regeneration off and level three is maximum.[7nUgmEuyKIU][XmBW_MV-TBU] Holding the left paddle down applies full regeneration and will pull the car to a standstill regardless of the level otherwise selected.[XmBW_MV-TBU][7nUgmEuyKIU] There is also an automatic mode that varies the level without driver input, and in eco mode with level three selected the car performs the regeneration on lift-off without any paddle use at all.[1432][1337]

Even at maximum fixed level, lift-off regeneration alone does not quite bring the car to a complete stop; the paddle hold, or a light touch of the brake pedal at the very end, closes the gap.[7nUgmEuyKIU][XmBW_MV-TBU] Modulating the accelerator to arrive at a stop line without touching the brake is a skill in its own right — released fully, the car often decelerates too early, so a small amount of pedal is held to stretch the stop out.[7nUgmEuyKIU] This is the basis of one-pedal driving, in which the brake pedal is reserved for exceptions.[7nUgmEuyKIU][h9MvrP9JDrs] In general driving Dave Jones leaves regeneration engaged permanently, on fixed level three or on auto, and rarely touches the brakes.[7nUgmEuyKIU] The engineering argument for defaulting to it is that in roughly 95% of lift-off events the driver wants to decelerate anyway, so the deceleration may as well be recovered.[1432]

Two interactions matter for safety and for measurement. Under strong regeneration the vehicle applies the brake lights even though the brake pedal has not been pressed, since a following driver needs the same warning regardless of which mechanism is decelerating the car.[7nUgmEuyKIU] Engaging cruise control disables regeneration, which rules it out during any consumption comparison.[7nUgmEuyKIU]

## Measured recovery

Over a mixed motorway and suburban route with regeneration held at full for the whole trip, average consumption came to 10.6 kWh per 100 km, with instantaneous figures ranging from the low eights to 12 or 13.[7nUgmEuyKIU] Motorway sections with a slight downhill component ran at 9.7 and then 8.8 kWh per 100 km; around 10 kWh per 100 km is a typical low figure for suburban driving.[7nUgmEuyKIU]

Recovery is at its most dramatic on sustained descents, where the energy being reclaimed is gravitational potential rather than the kinetic energy of individual stops. A descent from Blackheath in the Blue Mountains down to Penrith consumes zero net energy, the regeneration on the way down offsetting all traction energy used.[1600] Regeneration is an efficient means of recovering that energy — efficient enough that the alternative of harvesting it from the road surface under the tyres offers nothing that the vehicle is not already capturing for itself.[1600]

In fleet service the recovery fraction is substantial. An electric city bus operation reports regeneration returning on the order of 30 to 40 percent, with up to 30 percent seen on a single trip; hilly routes yield more.[1339] Close stop spacing was expected to be a problem, on the assumption that the bus would never reach enough speed between stops to regenerate meaningfully, and the outcome was the opposite of that expectation.[1339]

## Effect on friction brakes

Because the friction brakes are seldom commanded, pad wear is nearly eliminated. Even with regeneration switched off and the vehicle in coast mode, regeneration still handles the deceleration, and the discs engage only under hard braking.[5IV_oRAupeo] After twelve months of use an IONIQ front pad showed effectively no wear.[5IV_oRAupeo] Electric buses still require brake inspections, but show significantly less pad wear than their diesel equivalents, attributable to regeneration.[1339] On the iMiEV, pad life was expected in the region of 30,000 to 40,000 km, brakes and fluids being close to the whole of the remaining service schedule.[179]

## Beyond cars

The same mechanism appears on smaller vehicles. A brushless hub-motor electric bicycle kit provides regeneration through a switch in each of the supplied brake levers, with the amount of regeneration programmable in the controller so the rider can dial up as much or as little braking effect as wanted.[338]
