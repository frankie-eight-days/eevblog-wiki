# firmware update

A firmware update replaces the embedded software stored in a product's non-volatile memory, and for modern test and measurement equipment it is the primary mechanism by which defects are corrected, features are added, and hardware capabilities are unlocked after sale.[178][699][829] Because contemporary instruments implement triggering, decoding, and even sample processing in processors and FPGAs, a single update package may rewrite the instrument's entire logic configuration, not just its application code.[PcxEO3fA_Ls][369] The quality and longevity of a manufacturer's firmware support is therefore part of what a buyer pays for in a premium instrument.[1220]

## Update mechanisms

Removable SD and microSD media are a common delivery path. The 121GW multimeter updates from a `.bin` file copied to the root directory of its SD card; holding the HOLD and MEM keys while powering on, then pressing SETUP, starts the download.[96XE5Ye1q6I] A Siglent signal generator was re-initialised from a FAT32-formatted microSD card with vendor-supplied files after early pre-release firmware caused a failure.[tM2j9zuBiRQ] The Chronos high-speed camera carries a microSD slot expressly for firmware updates, and storing the entire operating system on an SD card — as in one Uni-T arbitrary waveform generator — makes both development and field updating straightforward.[947][1679]

USB flash drives dominate on oscilloscopes. The Rohde & Schwarz procedure is regarded as exemplary: the firmware file is downloadable from the website without registration, is placed on a USB stick, and the instrument displays the before and after versions.[6XpyOGw6RFM] Rigol scopes take a `.GEL` file from a stick and prompt before installing.[699] Simpler still, some instruments enumerate as a mass-storage drive: the Miniware DT71 LCR tweezers accept a dropped hex file, rename it automatically, and update on the next power cycle without any prompt.[1335]

PC-side utilities range from small dedicated updaters that display old and new versions before proceeding, as with the RØDE AI-1 interface, to full device managers such as RØDE Central and update wizards that detect outdated firmware automatically, as with the Digilent OpenScope MZ.[1405][f7jPJek3XuQ][1056] Network-based updating appears in oscilloscope web interfaces, in AV receivers — one Yamaha unit jumped from version 1.09 to 2.59 over USB or network — and in the web dashboards of open-source miners.[YkgCcYG29nk][1151][4ANGcEJbrTE] Over-the-air updating is used where a radio already exists: the CYBLE-212006-01 Bluetooth module in the BM787BT multimeter was updated from version 1.03 to 1.04 through a phone app.[XOuYJ1nxVJQ] At the lowest level, in-circuit programmers remain the fallback, with devices such as a networked radiation monitor requiring an AVR ISP and an individually programmed hex file per unit.[MWHamD1Hj58]

## Robustness, bricking, and side effects

A well-designed bootloader survives a failed update. The 121GW's bootloader could not be broken even by pulling the SD card mid-download, a design decision described as the "best decision we ever made" because it freed users from needing an in-circuit programming header and a purchased programmer.[121][e9cpKN69Avk] Similarly, an interrupted update on the µSupply can simply be run again from the start.[1264]

Where the bootloader is not robust, an update can brick the device. A Zoyi ZT-703S oscilloscope-multimeter reported "Upgrade failed" during a USB drag-and-drop update and was left unable even to power off.[1597] A MakerBot printer was bricked during a firmware update, and recovery was frustrated when the published hex file would not load into AVR Studio.[500] The Haasoscope Pro documentation explicitly warns against unplugging the unit while its flash is being erased and written.[1709] Devices built around FTDI USB interface chips inherit a particular wariness from the episode in which a vendor driver update disabled counterfeit chips.[692]

Updates can have side effects beyond the intended fix. Reflashing a Siglent generator resets its power-on startup counter, which also makes the instrument appear factory-new.[tM2j9zuBiRQ] The BM786 multimeter requires no recalibration after a firmware update, because calibration is tied to the sigma-delta converter inside the microcontroller; replacing the chip itself, by contrast, can shift calibration.[0Oo2J66FLZA] The Rohde & Schwarz MXO4 retains the user's complete setup across an update, booting back into the exact prior configuration.[1529]

## Bug fixes and feature additions

Firmware updates are the standard remedy for instrument defects found after release. Agilent investigated a reported U1272A multimeter fault, confirmed it was a firmware issue, and published a downloadable fix within three weeks, additionally supplying free USB cables so owners could apply the update.[178] Rigol corrected a jitter problem in the DS1054Z by changing PLL coefficients in firmware version 4.02.04.07, having acknowledged the cause.[699] Keysight added a capacitance function to its bench multimeter purely through a firmware update.[829] The BM787BT's 1.04 module firmware fixed a long-term logging bug that cut off notifications.[XOuYJ1nxVJQ]

Updates also resolve system-level mismatches. A home battery installation with modules of mixed firmware generations had one module repeatedly shutting itself down; updating all modules to a common version restored normal operation with no hardware damage.[aANZNwmnC_4] Some product categories depend on updates by nature: banknote validators must receive new firmware to accept newly issued note series.[525] Consumer devices show the same pattern, with power consumption on the Nokia E71 improving across successive firmware revisions.[80]

## Version and toolchain compatibility

Firmware versioning can create chicken-and-egg dependencies. The Aorus B450M motherboard supports Ryzen 5000-series processors only from firmware version 60 onward, but applying that update requires an older working processor already installed — a board with the old firmware cannot boot the new CPU to perform its own update.[MiqxUaWkGYY] The general practice when upgrading a PC processor is therefore to update the BIOS to the latest version first, since socket compatibility alone does not guarantee processor support.[1280] A similar circularity afflicted the PICkit 3 under MPLAB X: the environment wanted to download new firmware to the programmer before it could talk to it, and the resolution was to run the older PICkit 3.1 standalone programmer software first.[841]

## Updatability as a design consideration

Many instruments are deliberately not field-updatable. Brymen multimeters historically used one-time-programmable microcontrollers, making firmware updates impossible; the BM780 series moved to a reprogrammable part with an internal programming port, but access to that port is not given to the public — only the Bluetooth module firmware is user-updatable.[0Oo2J66FLZA][XOuYJ1nxVJQ] Low-cost meters frequently ship with undisclosed chipsets and no update path at all, so the buyer gets whatever firmware shipped.[1731] Some updatable instruments restrict the function: the Agilent 34461A requires a secure code to apply firmware.[489] The 121GW is one of the few multimeters on the market offering genuine user firmware updates.[XOuYJ1nxVJQ] Older equipment may simply be abandoned — a 2014 Tagarno microscope has no path to the version 5.8 firmware released for later models.[JJsKYNh2H0A]

Firmware is also a product-tiering mechanism. The Rigol DS1052E and the 100 MHz DS1102E were the same hardware distinguished by a model string, so a firmware change could have closed the upgrade loophole — though a hardware modification tying a control line would remain as a fallback unless the board itself changed.[70] Trial option licenses on the Rigol DS2000 behave differently from purchased licenses, with only the latter reporting an "official version".[369]

Long-term update support differentiates manufacturers. Rohde & Schwarz released a feature-adding version 3 firmware for the RTB2004 seven years after its release, backward compatible from the renamed RTB 2 series.[hv8ptePTbAc] Siglent has continued supplying new firmware for the discontinued SDS2000 series as a matter of stated service policy.[826] By contrast, no firmware at all had been published for the Rigol DHO4000 and DHO1000 nine months after release.[r_BYYgCqScE] Within a large vendor, consolidating per-product update methods into a single common mechanism is itself an engineering goal, since maintaining several different update paths multiplies the testing burden and degrades quality.[1032] Forward-looking designs provision for updates: one isolated oscilloscope design left spare FPGA gates specifically so features could be added later via USB at no extra cost.[1119]

## Forced updates, remote updates, and right to repair

Updates can be made a condition of use. A discarded Sonos speaker refused to function at all until its firmware was updated, and the update itself demanded creation of an account with an email address and password.[1514] Manufacturers have also ended products through software, either by bricking devices with an update or by discontinuing support so the device disables itself.[1383] Enphase microinverter warranty coverage is reportedly conditional on keeping the system permanently connected to the internet so it can receive automatic firmware updates, and the vendor can push firmware remotely through back-end access to diagnose faults.[isnXYy9vCag][1626] A new solar installation required a firmware update on commissioning day, followed by a 24-hour wait before the owner was granted portal access.[1385]

The policy dimension has reached formal inquiry. The Australian Productivity Commission's right-to-repair inquiry sought evidence on whether manufacturers should be mandated to provide software updates for a reasonable period after purchase, weighing the cost of maintaining updates for discontinued products against the cost of products rendered unrepairable.[xiXucGYuQ6M][qDWLTSfrq_E]
