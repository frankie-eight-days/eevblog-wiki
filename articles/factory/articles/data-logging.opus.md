# data logging

Data logging is the automatic recording of successive instrument readings against time, either into memory inside the instrument or into a file on an attached computer. It converts a measuring instrument from something that shows a present value into something that captures behaviour over an interval — drift, thermal response, discharge curves, intermittent events — that no operator watching a display could usefully record by hand.[835][576][774] On handheld multimeters it is the single feature that most sharply separates top-of-range models from mid-range ones: the difference between a Fluke 289 and a Fluke 87.5 comes down to data logging, PC interface capability, and tighter accuracy specifications.[15]

## Memory depth and sample interval

An instrument-resident logger is defined by two numbers: how many samples it can hold and how fast it can take them. The Agilent U1272A carries 10,000 data points of sample memory,[249][216] the U1253A stores 1,000 measurements,[56] and the Gossen Metrawatt Xtra holds roughly 15,000 samples in about 540 KB of memory.[46] The Fluke 189/289 family provides tens of thousands of samples built in.[15] Bench instruments extend this considerably; the Keithley DMM7510 carries a large internal memory, and an unpopulated memory site on its processor board suggests a factory expansion option.[731]

Sample interval is usually adjustable over a wide range. The U1253A logs from one reading per second out to intervals of thousands of seconds.[56] The Fluke 289 allows either a total duration in days, hours and minutes or an explicit sample interval, and reports the remaining sample time once running.[15] One reading per second is a common working default for both handheld and bench logging.[774][1592][1379]

The lower bound matters as much as the upper. The Keysight EDU34450A will not go below a one-second sample interval in data log mode.[1382] A web-access adapter retrofitted to older instruments imposes a three-second minimum log time, though it does at least store readings internally rather than requiring a permanently attached PC.[1232] Where the interval floor is too coarse for the phenomenon under test, the log is simply the wrong tool.

## Trigger, hold and event modes

Logging need not be time-driven. The U1272A offers three distinct data logging modes — triggered, hand and event — selected from the DLOG menu.[216] Combined with trigger hold, this turns the meter into an unattended sampler: the reading settles, the meter locks and stores it automatically, and the sample counter increments without a button press.[249][216] Applied to a batch of 1,000 resistors with a purpose-built probe jig, the arrangement gave roughly a fivefold increase in testing speed over manual recording, with no script or software written at all.[216][249] The practical caveat is timing — probing too quickly does not give the meter time to register a new event, so the operator must confirm each event is actually captured; an audible event beep, configurable in the setup menu, resolves this.[216]

Autonomous logging can be pushed below the level of the instrument firmware. On the Energy Micro Tiny Gecko, a timer repeatedly triggers an ADC conversion and DMA moves each result to RAM, waking the CPU by interrupt only once a given number of conversions has accumulated — logging from the ADC without powering up the processor at all.[269]

## Retrieving and analysing the data

Reviewing a log should not require a PC. The U1253A and U1272A both provide a view mode reached by holding a dedicated button, identifying the sample index and cycling through stored readings.[56][249] The Fluke 289 goes further, offering either save-for-later-upload or an immediate trend plot of the captured data, auto-scaled vertically — a scale of ±0.001 V on a quiet DC measurement, which resolves noise invisible on the numeric display.[15] That trend plotting is not universal: the EDU34450A can data log but has no live trend plot, restricting on-instrument review to stepping through readings one at a time.[1382]

Graphical bench meters treat logging as one element of a wider analysis suite. The Agilent 34461A combines a trend chart, histogram and statistics with the log, and its data can be downloaded over LAN or GPIB or dumped to a CSV file on disk — a path that yields better resolution than the front-panel display shows.[489][515] The same reasoning applies at the low end: on a 6.5-digit meter measuring a 9 V battery, 10 µV of display resolution can be bettered still further through the logging and trend plot function.[515] The addition of a graphics display, data logging and histograms was a deliberate differentiator when the 34401A was succeeded.[1032]

Export formats are consistently CSV or plain text. The BK Precision 879B LCR meter records data at full resolution and saves to Excel or text format.[137] The Rohde & Schwarz NGP800 logs all four channels with a log indicator on screen and exports to CSV, and logging can be bound to the user-defined front-panel button.[1293] The Brymen BM787BT app saves a real-time logger session as CSV, though the route to it is buried under a dashboard rather than placed with the record function where it would be expected.[ruxzp0OMicg]

Nomenclature can mislead. On the LeCroy WaveJet Touch 354, the logging function under the measure menu does not log or download waveform data at all — it logs measurement results, specifically pass/fail measurement data, saved to USB memory as a CSV file.[792]

## Software as the weak point

The recurring failure in commercial data logging is not the hardware but the host software. A Rigol instrument with both Ethernet and USB interfaces shipped with drivers but no software capable of extracting and plotting the data, leaving LabVIEW or a third-party package as the only route.[647] An electronic load's bundled software would not log in constant-power mode at all, and its nominal Excel and CSV export simply did not work.[774] The Gossen Metrawatt's Windows software was rudimentary despite the interface cable and software being a several-hundred-dollar extra on top of a $499 meter.[46]

The consistent engineering judgment is that instrument vendors should not attempt to build analysis into their applications. The correct scope is to let the user log to text at the fastest rate the hardware supports and dump it out; spreadsheet analysis belongs to the user afterwards.[1558] A supplied calculation workbook that turns out to be a spreadsheet, or in-program arithmetic on the measurement input, adds nothing that export would not provide better.[1558] Third-party software fills the gap where vendors do not: general logging packages exist that drive a range of multimeters and instruments, support very large data files over long logging periods, and allow arbitrary logging intervals and user-added instrument support.[1684]

## Long-duration runs

Logging over days changes the failure modes. A resistance standard placed in a thermal chamber set to 20 °C was recorded continuously overnight, with a brief interruption while the leads were rerouted through the chamber's side port; the earlier data was retained rather than restarted, since prior history is itself evidence of stability.[835] A companion run captured a large sample set at intervals of a couple of seconds with 10 power line cycles of integration, resolving diurnal structure — a rise, a dip, and a reversal corresponding to the laboratory being occupied or empty.[834] A precision current-voltage generator was characterised over a couple of days of logging to establish how accurate and stable its 1 mA output was over time, and measured better than its data sheet.[576] The 34461A left running overnight showed visible drift when the full record was recalled.[489]

The dominant risk on such runs is loss of the entire record. A month-long thermal chamber cycling test set up without battery backup would have reset and lost all accumulated data on any mains glitch; the run completed only by luck, and full battery backup on all logging equipment is the correct practice for unattended tests.[s2KkgI-kyK0] Battery-powered loggers face the same exposure at the other end: a meter whose contacts are corroded from an alkaline leak may work well enough for casual use but is not something to entrust a logging run to.[1687] Better-behaved designs handle it gracefully — the BM787BT detects low battery, shuts the meter down cleanly rather than dying mid-record, and on fresh cells and reconnection both the waveform and the data log resume from where they stopped.[XOuYJ1nxVJQ]

Where absolute timing matters, the log's own clock becomes a measurand. Oscillator drift over a logging interval is not known in direction in advance, and if it is close to linear it can be removed after the fact: take an accurate timestamp at the start against a GPS-locked rubidium standard orders of magnitude better than the oscillator in use, repeat the calibration at the end, and offset the difference between true and recorded time across the record.[61] Logging a counter's output against a rubidium reference is likewise the way to characterise drift that is invisible on the display.[647]

## Battery life

Logging is not free in power terms. Around 300 hours is taken as a decent battery life for a handheld meter, and that figure applies to standard measurement functions — data logging and other extras draw more.[184] The Fluke 287 is an instructive exception: nominally 100 hours of battery life, but 200 hours in logging mode, because it samples more slowly there and consumes roughly half the power.[1592] Auto power-off, which normally protects battery life, is correctly suppressed in logging modes.[56]

## Implementation inside instruments

Logging capability shows up plainly in a teardown. In the Fluke 287, an MSP430 handles the multimeter function exactly as in the rest of the range, while a Freescale processor with two memory devices including flash was bolted on to drive the LCD graphics and the data logging.[1592] The Dräger Multiwarn II gas monitor logs for around 50 hours using an 8-channel, 8-bit ADC built into a 68HC11, with external memory holding the logged data — crude conversion, adequate for the purpose.[603] A smart meter separates a power board from a dedicated data logging and processing board.[409] Conversely, the absence of logging is visible too: the BM235 does no data logging at all, which makes the presence of two external EEPROM chips on its board unexplained.[912]

Storage medium is often decided incidentally. The 121GW gained a micro SD card only because a supplier changed part, and with it both firmware updating and a logging capability that had not been designed in — the original plan assumed limited internal EEPROM.[e9cpKN69Avk] Instruments that combine an SD card with a LAN interface are typically doing so precisely to support logging.[1745] A meter with internal memory and USB that nonetheless offers no recording of its trend chart is a genuine design omission rather than a cost decision.[1723]

Where logging is distributed, its location must be stated. In the Fluke CNX3000 wireless system, logging is done internally in each of the remote units; the CNX3000 meter itself has no logging capability and acts only as the star controller for the wireless network.[417]

## Multi-channel and PC-based logging

Single-channel logging quickly proves limiting. PC-based oscilloscopes are best suited to exactly the applications a bench scope cannot serve — multi-channel data logging and higher-resolution front ends of 12 or 16 bits — so a PC scope should be chosen for vertical resolution and channel count rather than bandwidth.[13] The same reasoning drove a proposed multimeter with two or three blade-type thermocouple inputs at the top of the case alongside the two volts and two amps channels, giving a six-channel logging instrument without consuming the main inputs with thermocouple adapters.[184] A 14-bit scope with a claimed data logger function is attractive for the same reason, though claiming data logging and providing a usable implementation are different matters.[ByUiOk00K0U]

Standalone multi-channel logging can also be built around a small computer running vendor Python software, driving the acquisition hardware and an HDMI monitor without a full PC.[1127] Where instruments are Ethernet/LXI controlled, the whole measurement can be scripted — sweeping a supply through output voltages while LXI-connected bench meters log the results — although for a one-off efficiency measurement, four meters read manually into a spreadsheet is faster than a day spent building the automation.[957]

## Applications

Logging is the enabling step in most quantitative bench work. Battery discharge testing runs a constant-power load down to cutoff with logging once per second and a 60,000-reading budget, stoppable early.[774] A cell tested under four-terminal sensing to eliminate lead voltage drop is logged to a USB stick so the curves can be brought into a spreadsheet and overlaid against others.[1732] Reflow oven characterisation pairs a reference thermocouple of matched thermal mass with a Bluetooth module on the meter and a logging app, producing profiles rather than spot readings.[558] Logging a meter's microamp range through a reflow cycle reveals current generated by capacitors during heating, with the caveat that different meters present different shunt resistances on the current ranges.[1455]

At longer timescales, a domestic solar installation logged continuously supports averages over time, which is what shows that cleaning panels that are not visibly filthy produces no measurable increase in output.[0KX5F7Si-TY][484] Radiation monitors store readings internally and continuously, including in nominally switched-off states, for later download to a PC, and maintain an event log of power-ups and activity.[775][1621] A slow log at one sample per second on a high-input-impedance meter, viewed afterwards as a trend chart, reveals the wandering couple of volts of pickup that a lower-impedance meter reads as zero.[1379]
