# 121gw

The 121GW is a handheld digital multimeter developed as an EEVblog-branded instrument and delivered in 2018 after roughly two years of development.[e9cpKN69Avk] It was designed and financed by the contract manufacturer rather than by the brand it carries, which shaped both its feature set and its compromises.[121][e9cpKN69Avk] Its distinguishing features are a 15 V diode test mode, a built-in low-burden-voltage current amplifier, Bluetooth and SD-card logging, and simultaneous voltage and current display.[1157][1604][96XE5Ye1q6I][1095]

## Origins and development

The project began as an attempt to rebadge an existing Brymen design; Brymen were not interested in producing a custom multimeter, and the idea was dropped.[e9cpKN69Avk] It restarted after a charity mud run in which a Klein Tools MM500 and a Tektronix oscilloscope were dragged through an obstacle course; conversations with Klein Tools — associated with Kain Test and UEI — led to an offer to design, develop and build a multimeter at no cost to the brand, on the reasoning that the audience was large enough to sell the volume.[e9cpKN69Avk][xUAKjWMsUfA] Formal development dates to 2014–2015.[e9cpKN69Avk] Early specification work benchmarked the Klein Tools MM6000, the Fluke 87 and the UEI 397, and at that stage the design still assumed AA cells and included power measurement.[e9cpKN69Avk]

Because the manufacturer paid for development, the brand side deliberately avoided micromanaging the board layout and circuit design, accepting design compromises in exchange.[121][e9cpKN69Avk] Development ran about two years — not continuous effort, but a back-and-forth cycle of prototype, shipment, investigation and re-spin — with the whole design re-spun once in the middle.[e9cpKN69Avk] By February 2016 the schematic, case, user interface and buttons were finalised and tooling was under way; the HY3131 chipset, the MOV and PTC input protection, and the MAX4258-based burden-voltage circuit were all in place by then.[e9cpKN69Avk] Through 2017 the outstanding work was calibration and settling-time issues, followed by a linearity bug and a lockup bug in June.[e9cpKN69Avk]

The first case design was rejected for size: it was well made but consumed too much volumetric space and weight for shipping in the thousands, so a smaller case was developed that suited both the 121GW and the BM235.[e9cpKN69Avk][xUAKjWMsUfA] The meter body itself ended up roughly on par with the BM235 in size, having originally been physically larger and differently shaped with AA cells.[e9cpKN69Avk][xUAKjWMsUfA] The earliest prototype was entirely 3D printed, including the knob and a plastic stand-in for the rubber boot, and predates the 121GW name — it was then called the EEVblog True RMS meter.[121][xUAKjWMsUfA] Case tooling was custom and expensive, and the manufacturer negotiated the right to reuse the case on other non-competing products.[e9cpKN69Avk]

Two years of development for a meter of this complexity is not unusual; the considerably simpler BM235 also took Brymen over two years from original concept to first shipment.[e9cpKN69Avk]

## Measurement architecture

The meter is built on the HY3131 measurement chipset, the same part used in the Keysight U1282A.[uUCQuIp_hzU][izCDWjK_pV4] The microcontroller is an STM32L1, chosen for low power, cost and availability at the time, and for its LCD driver capability.[1539]

Input impedance is the conventional 10 MΩ except in low-impedance mode, where a PTC sits directly across the input.[121] That PTC starts at roughly 2 kΩ cold and rises as mains current heats it.[121] The same range-switch wiper arrangement that creates low-impedance mode also shorts the input through the ohms path, which is what allows the meter to safely discharge a capacitor by switching briefly to ohms.[1655]

Late in development, thermal chamber testing showed more drift than intended, and a better voltage reference was fitted despite the cost target.[121] The design still avoids the laser-trimmed and ceramic hybrid precision divider networks used in higher-end meters, a deliberate cost decision.[121] A later revision, the 1707-dated model, added a further improved voltage reference and lower-tempco divider resistors.[Wwz_fdU17aQ] In a ramped thermal chamber test against a Gossen MetraHit Ultra and a Keysight U1282A, that revision held a 5 V reference to better than 0.01 % over a 32 °C delta, while the MetraHit dropped one least significant digit and the Keysight drifted substantially more.[Wwz_fdU17aQ]

## 15 V diode mode

The diode test mode drives up to about 15 V of compliance, well above the two or three volts typical of a multimeter.[1452] This makes it possible to test parts a conventional meter cannot: series diode stacks in an EHT section that read open on an ordinary diode range will read around 10.7 V on the 121GW, and an 8.2 V zener can be checked directly in its reverse direction.[1452][1394]

The high test voltage propagates into the input protection. The diode mode shares the ohms input circuitry, which is protected by the back-to-back dual-transistor zener clamp that Fluke popularised and that appears in countless multimeters.[1157] That clamp relies on the reverse emitter-base breakdown of a bipolar transistor, and VEBO is typically only six to twelve volts on general-purpose parts — a 2N2222 or 2N3904 is adequate for ordinary meters but not here.[1157] The 121GW instead needs a part rated around 20 V or more, which is scarce; the Rohm IMX25, a dual general-purpose transistor with VEBO of 25 V, is one of the few that qualifies.[1157] The clamp cannot dissipate much energy, but it clamps fast enough to protect the ohms circuit and survives long enough for the PTC to take over.[1157]

## Input protection and overload behaviour

The voltage input carries a nominal 1.5 kΩ PTC in series with a 1 kΩ high-voltage protection resistor, clamped by two 575 V MOVs.[YPUJipe8Loo] The meter beeps and shows a lightning-bolt warning indicator above 600 V.[YPUJipe8Loo] Against a high-voltage DC supply it survived 1210 V, the supply's limit.[YPUJipe8Loo] On AC — where the peaks are considerably higher than the equivalent DC — it sustained 1100 V against a 600 V AC rating, occasionally tripping its protection but continuing to measure correctly afterwards.[vwVXl-RQWXs]

Under crude high-voltage impulse testing, the MOVs clamp the incoming waveform in the same way as those in the BM235, letting the PTCs, series protection resistors and diode clamps do their work; meters with only a PTC and no MOVs or gas discharge tubes survive by luck rather than engineering.[1016]

The rotary range switch was retained rather than soft pushbuttons because a multi-ganged mechanical switch gets isolation between ranges more cheaply, simply and reliably; achieving the same integrity with electronic switching requires considerably more circuitry.[1095]

## Low burden voltage

The meter incorporates a µCurrent-style ×100 amplifier ahead of the current shunt, giving a burden voltage substantially lower than a conventional multimeter, where burden can reach a volt or more on the low current ranges.[1604][1533] The burden voltage is not zero — it is only lower — so the specified figure still has to be checked against the requirement at hand.[1604][1533] The low-burden range has limited headroom for load current: a device drawing 22 mA would not start up through it and had to be measured on the 500 mA range instead.[1238]

## Firmware and auto-ranging

Firmware is user-updatable, which is unusual — most manufacturers do not permit end users to reflash their meters.[XOuYJ1nxVJQ] The procedure copies a .bin file to the root directory of the SD card, then holds the HOLD and MEM buttons while powering on and presses SETUP to start the download.[96XE5Ye1q6I]

Ohms auto-ranging speed was the meter's weakest characteristic at launch and was addressed across several releases: 1.04 improved it, 1.05 improved it again, and 2.00 substantially increased both the high-resistance update rate and general ohms auto-ranging.[uUCQuIp_hzU][q4gXnpFPFzQ] Version 2.00 also fixed a capacitance-range bug that had previously required a manual step.[q4gXnpFPFzQ] By firmware 2.02 a 10 Ω resistor auto-ranged in roughly three seconds — on par with, or a shade better than, the Keysight U1282A running the same HY3131 chipset, and slower than the Brymen BM869, which settles in under a second.[izCDWjK_pV4] Auto-ranging speed is largely a matter of how each manufacturer drives the chipset rather than a hard limit of the silicon: other implementations of the HY3131 have reportedly reached about two seconds.[uUCQuIp_hzU]

An EEVblog forum user, tpwrules, decompiled the firmware from the binary and produced a modified build that fixed the slow auto-ranging without owning the meter.[3MD88EYLdQQ]

## Range switch defect

Some early production meters developed intermittent range-switch contacts, a fault never seen in any prototype.[e9cpKN69Avk] The cause was a minor, unannounced change in PCB thickness from the supplier: contact force in the rotary switch depends on a controlled board thickness, and once that tolerance stacked with the others, contacts could become marginal — enough to affect perhaps one meter in a thousand rather than the whole run.[e9cpKN69Avk][1259] The same tolerance mechanism was later considered as a candidate explanation for switch complaints on other meters.[9u8TEJdAVdQ]

The fix was a routed FR4 shim supplied by UEI, with tooling holes locating it against the routed edges.[u2-ot2vWLxI] Validation used a purpose-built automated range-switch endurance tester: 3D-printed knob adapters driven by a stepper motor and controller, with a fan fitted because the thermal behaviour of a continuously cycled switch was unknown, running batches of 1000 full end-to-end cycles.[QSmiMlWEpy0][u2-ot2vWLxI]

## Companion software

The mobile and desktop applications were written in Xamarin C# as a cross-platform project.[-_rqzsJy_Qs] The Bluetooth app connects without requiring an account or login.[1238] It does request location permission, which is a quirk of the Bluetooth driver rather than a data-collection measure.[1238] The Windows application was developed with high-DPI scaling disabled during development and the proper scaling applied afterwards, avoiding the blurry-font problem that afflicts applications compiled without DPI awareness.[qjLXJl0kDjg]

## Measurement capabilities in use

The frequency counter reads to 100 kHz, useful for switching supplies where a lower-bandwidth meter reads incorrectly.[1602] AC millivolt measurement resolves noise floors in the hundreds of microvolts — around 250 µV RMS across a 4 Ω tweeter in one measurement.[1128] Current logging over time is supported directly, allowing the current drawn by a component to be recorded through a reflow profile.[1455] Thermocouple temperature measurement tracks a reference meter closely, agreeing to within about a degree on a shorted-probe internal reading.[48J0m3dvpgA] Simultaneous volts and amps display on the two displays is a genuine capability, unlike meters that advertise a VA display without implementing it.[1095]

Capacitance and inductance are measured with a triangular test waveform at a little over 100 Hz, not the clean sine wave at a selected frequency that a proper LCR meter produces, and the test frequency varies with the measured capacitance.[1465]

## Production and related designs

Production units shipped in cases of 32, manufactured in Korea, with a thermocouple and a statement of calibration in the box, and factory firmware version 1.01.[1051] A hand-and-face motif is moulded into the inside of the case rather than printed on the front, a deliberate choice against putting it on the fascia.[121][xUAKjWMsUfA] Component cost crept above the contracted price during development, partly from the added voltage reference and other precision parts, and partly from broker pricing on scarce chips.[121]

Kain Test retained the rights to the design, having financed and executed it, with exclusive distribution rights to the 121GW itself held by EEVblog.[1668] A separate prototype meter, the EEVblog DMM555, was later built on the 121GW design and reuses the identical case moulding, including the battery compartment and range-switch contacts.[1668]
