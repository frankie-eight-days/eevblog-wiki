# grounding

Grounding is the practice of establishing a common conductive reference within a piece of equipment and, where required, bonding that reference to protective mains earth. It does three separate jobs that are often confused: it defines the node every signal is measured against, it gives induced and accumulated charge a low-impedance path to drain away, and it makes shields and enclosures electrically effective rather than decorative.[879][616][524] Because those jobs overlap in the same copper, a grounding defect rarely announces itself as a grounding defect — it surfaces as inexplicable noise, a measurement that will not repeat, a display that will not work, or an instrument that is dangerous to touch.[f58qoyCETAE][1664][PcxEO3fA_Ls]

## Ground as a measurement reference

Every voltage measurement is relative to whatever the instrument calls ground, so an oscilloscope probing two points that are electrically identical with respect to chassis ground should produce identical results, and when it does not the fault is in the connection rather than in the analog front end.[879] Once the reference itself is compromised, everything downstream is wrong: if the inductors carrying ground between board sections fracture under board flex, the grounding is ruined and the measurements are off, independently of whatever fault was originally being chased.[1449]

Reference problems are frequently mechanical. A marginal BNC connector produces exactly the symptoms of a mysterious instrument fault — the channel picks up all sorts of interference — and the cause is a dodgy ground contact whose tolerances are slightly out.[f58qoyCETAE] A scope left ungrounded shows the same behaviour, which is why a grounding lead is supplied with the instrument in the first place.[f58qoyCETAE] Grounding is also a diagnostic tool in its own right: an input deliberately grounded shows how much of an adjacent channel's trace is capacitive crosstalk coupling across the pin header, though shorting a differential input typically reduces the coupled signal rather than eliminating it.[692] A floating pin can be diagnosed the same way — an LCD contrast pin left floating gives a display that powers up without usable contrast, and grounding it restores the correct contrast voltage.[1664]

Deliberately tying a node to ground rather than letting it float is a design decision, not just a bench trick, and it is used in protection circuits so the protected line is held rather than left to drift.[HBLKxC4JzTA] The corresponding trap is ordering: bypass capacitance placed downstream of a protection device stores energy that keeps dumping after the supply is switched off, so the bypassing must sit on the protected side.[HBLKxC4JzTA]

## Safety earth

Mains-referenced equipment must be earthed, and an oscilloscope run floating is a genuine hazard rather than a stylistic preference: floating scopes are generally a bad idea, they are sometimes used that way for legitimate test reasons, and the correct provision is that "you must have a ground lug and you must keep it grounded".[PcxEO3fA_Ls] The related body of practice covers grounding and isolation together, since the two determine whether a probe connection destroys the instrument.[680]

In construction, the quality of the earth bond is judged by whether it will survive vibration and corrosion. Good practice is a professionally crimped lead landed on a dedicated stud with a shake-proof nut or washer, and serious designs run more than one such cable to separate ground points.[731][261][497] Routing the earth through an intermediate connection and then down to the board, rather than a direct crimp onto a stud and screw, is a downgrade even where it works.[1038] Attaching extra earth leads to a transformer mounting lug without scraping the paint back is worse still — it functions in practice, but it is not best practice and it is not a reliable connection.[440] Multiple grounding points on a mains-connected inverter are expected rather than optional.[kKfZgAyFu8Q] Exposed metalwork that a user can touch gets its own green earthing wire specifically to guarantee the metal is earthed.[1489] Clearance is the other half of the problem: a design running the earth ring around the outside of a mains board leaves only about 5-6 mm of creepage between mains and earth, which is adequate but not generous.[1293]

Grounding a person is not automatically a good thing. The common assumption that self-grounding is protective is wrong in a lot of cases; "Grounding yourself is a horrible thing to do in a lot of cases".[CYm-4gbl1Zc]

## ESD control

Static control grounding works by dissipating accumulated charge to earth, which is what a corded wrist strap plugged into a bench ESD point does.[768] A typical home lab implementation is a bonding point tied back to mains earth, with the wrist strap plugging into that point.[107] Antistatic mats should be referenced to mains earth; they retain some usefulness ungrounded because charge spreads across the surface and dissipates into the bench, but they should still be tied back to a ground point so the charge can bleed off.[400] An ungrounded mat is an active source of trouble, producing coupling effects that mimic an equipment EMC fault.[933] Connecting a floating mat through to mains earth, or connecting the operator to the mat, visibly changes the coupling behaviour of a bench setup.[933][Q_RYG_5cQk8]

Contact area governs how good a human ground connection actually is: a small touch grounds poorly, while wrapping the hand around a conductor gives a much better ground.[Uk5F3rQNUkU] The human body works as a reasonably neutral ground because its exterior is conductive.[BUW6h88weXU] Footwear matters at the generation end — walking on the same floor with shoes off produces peak charging that does not reach 100 V.[768] Charge generated near an already-grounded product is much less consequential than the raw voltage suggests; unrolling cling wrap near grounded equipment reads a couple of hundred volts and settles to practically zero.[623]

The classic ESD failure case shows the limits of grounding as a safety measure. An electro-explosive bridge wire on a spacecraft was dead-shorted, twisted so it could not pick up broadcasts, grounded, and shielded inside an aluminium cylinder — everything electrical engineering of the period could demand — and was still vulnerable, because the polyethylene dust drape hanging over it was outside the electrical model entirely.[BUW6h88weXU]

## Shielding, chassis bonding and enclosures

A shield only works if it is grounded, and finding the ground path is part of assessing any shielded design. A metal mesh screen attenuating low-frequency interference must be grounded somehow, and where no obvious connection exists the path may be through a nickel-screened plastic front panel that is conductive over its whole surface.[524] Conversely, shielding tape applied over capacitors and an inductor with no deliberate ground connection is only nominally a shield.[907] The consequence is measurable: an oscilloscope case that is not electrically grounded because the shield has been moved away shows markedly worse noise on channel one than the same instrument with its electrical and magnetic shields in place.[1355]

Well-executed products bond aggressively. Shield cans are earthed and carried across to the main chassis of the mechanism they sit on, sometimes with grounding straps soldered directly to the frame.[937][937] Battery contacts are decoupled directly to chassis ground, with the grounding and decoupling treated as first-order design concerns rather than afterthoughts.[1090] Where a board must be grounded to a back panel repeatedly through service cycles, spring-loaded grounding clips that engage as the board seats are used, four to a board, which is elaborate hardware for the sole purpose of grounding to the panel.[278] A soldered tab bridging a keyboard board to the main chassis exists for an EMI reason and is a nuisance during reassembly.[360] Other approaches include a diecast moulding shaped to guarantee a proper ground between a large stud and the surrounding structure, flat flex single-conductor runs used to join screw bosses and tie grounds together, a dedicated ground lead joining the grounds of two separate modules that share a battery connector, beefy copper strapping to keep a folded-out assembly grounded, and earthing from a panel up into the middle frame of a chassis.[951][860][375][1256][WFhk1aRqLD8] A grounded metal interface under a sensor package can serve thermally as well, improving heat sinking while providing the ground.[399]

Grounded chassis metal near an unsupported board is also a hazard during service: a board back making contact with a grounded power supply is enough to stop equipment working correctly.[1395]

## Ground planes, layout and inductance

At RF the relevant quantity is not resistance but inductance. Via stitching along board edges is common in RF systems specifically to lower the inductance of the ground plane and the grounding system.[571] The same reasoning explains copper strapping added between shielded cans on a double-sided board, where the intent is a lower-inductance path and the change reads as an RFI or ground fix retrofitted late.[907]

Constrained layouts force compromises that are visible in the finished product. Green jumper wires joining ground regions across a board indicate the grounds could not be routed on the layout itself.[1153] A star grounding point with many branches, jumpered across the board with an additional wire to reach an AM antenna section, reflects the same problem — a decent ground could not be obtained through the layout, and running an extra wire plus its assembly labour was still cheaper than moving to a double-sided board.[1243] Splitting grounds by function is a routine design consideration, with an ADC ground and a power ground tied in at different points.[87]

Individually grounded miniature coaxial outputs on an instrument indicate each channel is intended as a Z0 probe, completed with a series resistor of 327 ohms.[1720] High-bandwidth power rail probing sits in the same territory, since ground system issues are among the reasons for probing a rail in the first place.[1735]

## EMC test setups

Conducted emissions testing is built on a ground plane, which exists to stop capacitive coupling between the setup and other objects in the room.[548] A LISN is mounted above the ground plane by a specified amount, or the plane is removed and the box placed directly on the bench; the source side is grounded to the grounding plate while the device-under-test side is not.[548] The bond from the equipment to the plane must be very short and very closely connected, because a long lead is a high impedance and therefore not a good grounding point across the frequency range of interest.[548] In a full anechoic facility the same principle appears at scale: the copper table is grounded to the chamber's outer walls, using wide strips mandated by the standard to obtain a genuinely good RF ground.[202]

Grounding also appears inside signal-chain design for the same shielding reason. In a condenser microphone, one electrode is grounded while the other is polarized through a very high value resistor, hundreds of megohms and often gigohms.[609] The Faraday shield wrapped around a microphone's input circuitry is grounded so that any capacitively induced current has somewhere to drain to — a connection that is sometimes omitted in practice.[616] In balanced audio, grounding either leg of a balanced output reproduces the same conducted-mode noise that an unbalanced adaptor causes, since the adaptor is simply shorting one output to ground.[KQTt-7F3MYw]

## Assessing grounding in a design

Grounding quality is one of the cheapest readable indicators of how much care went into a product. Adequate earthing with exposed cable and no refinement is entirely serviceable and merely lacks polish, and bare-bottom cost designs can still have adequate earthing.[1434][1679] The judgement is therefore not binary but about method: crimped and stud-mounted rather than lug-and-paint, short and direct rather than long and routed, bonded rather than left floating.[440][1038][907]
