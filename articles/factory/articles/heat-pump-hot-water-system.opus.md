# heat pump hot water system

A heat pump hot water system heats a storage tank of domestic water using a vapour-compression refrigeration cycle rather than a burner or a resistive element, moving heat from ambient air into the water instead of generating it directly.[1517] Because it moves heat rather than making it, it delivers several times more thermal energy than the electrical energy it consumes, which makes it the most energy-efficient of the common domestic hot water options.[1517][1630] In a home with rooftop solar, the tank doubles as a thermal battery: a low-power load that can be timed to run entirely on excess generation, storing the energy as hot water rather than exporting it.[iavM2IqueM8][1517]

## Operating principle

Cold mains water enters the bottom of the tank; water is drawn from the bottom into the heat pump unit, and the heated output is returned to the top of the tank.[1517] The mechanism is the same as an air conditioner or reverse-cycle air conditioner — the unit is physically comparable in size to a small split-system air conditioner head.[1517] Delivered water leaves the heat pump at roughly 60–63 °C.[1517] The elevated outlet temperature is not arbitrary: keeping the tank hot suppresses Legionella growth.[1517] Control is by a temperature sensor whose set point determines when heat input stops.[1517]

Modern high-efficiency units use CO2 as the refrigerant, which yields a higher coefficient of performance than earlier refrigerants.[1517] Coefficients of performance well above four are achievable, and manufacturer claims run up to about five.[1517] A measured installation drawing 2.83 kWh per day of electrical energy delivered the equivalent of 10 to 12 kWh of heat energy — a ratio, not a violation of energy conservation, since electrical energy in and heat energy out are not the same quantity.[1517]

## Power draw and duty cycle

The defining electrical characteristic is low peak power over a long run time. A 415 L residential unit draws about 1 kW while running.[1517][gAu8CvMjDrU][1634][1651] Measured averages sit slightly under that figure, around 950 W approaching winter.[wQbCsk7UAFc][5x8UVZnwyL8] Run time is seasonal: roughly two and a half to three hours in winter and an hour and a half to two hours in summer, for a total of about 2.5 to 3 kWh per day.[1630][1517] Manufacturer specifications give 3 kWh to heat a full 315 L delivery, varying with inlet water temperature — cold climates degrade the coefficient of performance, which matters less in a temperate location such as Sydney.[pXtSybs9QRs] A single hot bath the previous night is enough to lengthen the following day's run measurably.[1630]

Because the running load is around 1 kW, a dedicated 20 A circuit is far more than the unit needs, though that is how such systems are wired at the switchboard.[1651] The unit's nameplate may read 2.3 kW even where measured consumption is about half that.[1517]

## Comparison with gas and with resistive elements

Replacing an old high-efficiency gas storage system with a heat pump cut daily energy consumption from 12.8 kWh to about 1.7 kWh — a factor of roughly seven and a half.[iavM2IqueM8] Manufacturer figures for the same comparison give 6,100 kWh per year for gas against 1,100 kWh per year for the heat pump.[pXtSybs9QRs] On pure energy consumption, gas is worse than even a straight resistive element electric hot water system, which needs the full 10 to 12 kWh per day of electrical energy to achieve the same temperature rise.[iavM2IqueM8][1517] Against gas, the heat pump wins without contest on energy consumption.[1517]

Instantaneous electric water heaters lose on peak power rather than on total energy. A single-phase instant unit draws around 7 kW while running, against 1 kW peak for the heat pump, which is precisely the wrong characteristic in a home with solar and battery storage where the objective is to minimise peak current.[pXtSybs9QRs] The heat pump's thermal mass converts a large intermittent demand into a small scheduled one: 1 kW for three hours fills a 315 L tank with energy that can then be drawn on over the day.[pXtSybs9QRs] Instantaneous systems retain an advantage only under severe space constraints.[pXtSybs9QRs]

Solar thermal collectors were once common on Australian rooftops but have largely fallen out of use.[pXtSybs9QRs][1630] They cannot deliver consistently hot water year-round and therefore still require a boosting system behind them, and the roof area they occupy is better dedicated to photovoltaic panels, whose output can serve every electrical load rather than water heating alone.[1630]

## Integration with solar generation

Units intended for solar self-consumption include a timer restricting operation to daylight hours — one such timer works only between 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m.[1517] Because the load is only about 1 kW, even a poor overcast day leaves an 8 kW nominal array with enough surplus to cover it.[1517][iavM2IqueM8] Setting the start time too late risks overlapping with other daytime loads such as a pool pump, doubling instantaneous consumption; moving the start earlier separates them.[5x8UVZnwyL8]

The share of household generation consumed is small. At the start of summer, water heating took about one sixteenth — roughly 6% — of the daily output of an 8 kW system serving a family of four.[WtGlolw-BxA] In midwinter the same household's peak generation falls to around four to four and a half kilowatts, and on a good winter day producing 30 kWh the hot water system accounted for about four and a half to five kWh.[WtGlolw-BxA][1630] The magnitude only becomes intuitive when the consumption trace is compared as area under the curve against the generation trace.[WtGlolw-BxA][5HikvxaQ_Z8]

Deliberately oversizing the tank is defensible on these grounds: a 415 L tank in place of a 175 L one gives substantially greater heat capacity — a larger thermal sink — for little or no additional energy, since standing losses did not correlate measurably with the increase.[1517][iavM2IqueM8]

## Cost, reliability and noise

A top-of-the-range installation cost $5,500 including installation, disconnection of the gas service and repiping, against an annual saving of $722, giving a payback period of about five years.[iavM2IqueM8][1517] State and federal rebates of roughly $1,000 apply in Australia, and in some states government rebates bring the installed price close to zero.[iavM2IqueM8][1517] Systems at half that price exist, and rising gas prices shorten the payback further.[pXtSybs9QRs][1517]

Cheap bottom-of-the-line units are a false economy — they break down, to the point that some installers refuse to fit them.[1517] The engineering case for paying more rests on the same reasoning applied to air conditioners: a heat pump water heater is mechanically the same class of machine as a reverse-cycle air conditioner, and units of that kind routinely run 10 to 20 years without failure.[1517][1630] Domestic units are quiet, rated around 36 dB and barely audible standing directly in front of one at full output; the noise objection applies to the much larger pool heat pumps, not to hot water units.[1517]

## Measurement caveat

Third-party energy monitoring platforms have misreported this load. One reported 79 to 85 W for a heat pump hot water system that actually draws about 1 kW — an order of magnitude error, mirrored on the same platform by a pool heat pump reported at 200 W and an EV charger reported at 400 to 500 W, below the vehicle's minimum charge rate.[vDtxmikcYuc] Verifying a claimed daily consumption figure needs no elaborate instrumentation: a kilowatt-hour-per-day number from a simple mains energy logger is sufficient.[iavM2IqueM8]
