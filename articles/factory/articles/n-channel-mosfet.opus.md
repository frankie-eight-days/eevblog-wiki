# n channel mosfet

The N-channel MOSFET is the default switching element of modern electronics: a three-terminal gate-drain-source device that conducts between drain and source when the gate is driven positive with respect to the source.[242][748] MOSFETs come in N-channel and P-channel varieties, and most designs use N-channel because most switching is done on the low side — driving a relay, an LED string, or the switching element of a DC-to-DC converter — with N-channel occupying the same circuit role that the NPN occupies among bipolar transistors.[1736][748] The preference is visible in the supply chain as much as in the schematic: a parametric search at a major distributor turns up roughly 8,800 N-channel parts against about 1,580 P-channel ones, an imbalance summarised as "N-channel snobbery".[1387][1461]

## Behaviour and terminals

At circuit level a BJT and a MOSFET can be dropped into the same configuration and behave much the same way, and the N-channel device maps onto the NPN.[748] The practical difference is at the control terminal. A bipolar switch driven from a logic pin needs a base resistor and suffers a base-emitter drop; substituting an N-channel MOSFET removes both, allowing the gate to be tied directly to the driving junction and cutting the component count.[242] Where a MOSFET is driven from a low supply rail, a logic level part is required — one whose threshold falls inside the available gate drive.[102]

P-channel devices can be treated loosely as the mirror image of N-channel ones, but the two are not simply inverted: they differ physically, operationally and parametrically, and P-channel parts typically carry somewhat less continuous current and a higher on-resistance than an otherwise comparable N-channel part.[748][1736]

### On-resistance

R<sub>DS(on)</sub> is the drain-to-source resistance when the device is switched hard on, and it is the parameter that usually decides part selection in a conduction-limited application.[931] In a multimeter current path, where a wafer range switch cannot carry the current and a big TO-220 or D²PAK part rated for tens of amps must switch the 10 mA shunt instead, a low R<sub>DS(on)</sub> is what keeps the added burden voltage acceptable.[931] Typical jellybean power parts land in the low tens of milliohms: an FDS6680 is rated 11.5 A at 30 V with about 10 mΩ nominal on-resistance, and a 500 V 18 A part is specified at 0.27 Ω nominal — on-resistance rises steeply with voltage rating.[397][1387]

### Body diode

Every such MOSFET carries a parasitic body diode between source and drain, inherent in the physical construction rather than added deliberately.[811] It sets a hard constraint on where the device can block current, and it can also be exploited. In a NiMH cell-bypass arrangement, a lower N-channel MOSFET turned on shorts across a cell; if a cell is then inserted, the body diode of the upper device sits reverse biased — its cathode positive with respect to its anode — and with the gate held at zero no current flows out of the battery at all.[812] Some integrated drain outputs add Zener clamping alongside the intrinsic body diode, which removes the need for external clamp devices.[952] The body diode is prominent enough to be part of a MOSFET's identity: a low-cost component tester correctly reports an IRF610 as an N-channel MOSFET and draws the internal diode in its symbol, though the same instrument misidentifies a VN10K as an NPN transistor, recognising transistor-like behaviour and enormous gain but not the device type.[1020]

## Part numbering and sourcing

MOSFET part numbering is de facto standardised around current, channel type and voltage, which makes cross-manufacturer substitution unusually straightforward.[1387] In the common formula, an 18N50 is an 18 A, N-channel, 500 V part — the trailing 50 denotes 500 V, not 50 V, and a P-channel equivalent would carry a P in place of the N.[1387] An 18N50 from essentially any manufacturer will do the job in most positions, so a repair search can begin from the naming convention alone.[1387] The remaining parameters may still matter a great deal, particularly in a design context such as a mains power factor correction stage.[1387]

Substitution is not always so easy. Parts from second-tier Asian manufacturers — Sino Power, Alpha & Omega, China Resources Microelectronics — are extremely common inside consumer equipment yet may not be stocked by the mainstream distributors at all, and a device combining an unusual package with an uncommon feature such as built-in ESD protection may have no true equivalent.[1460][1461] Sponsored "equivalent" listings returned by distributor search are paid advertising, not verified substitutes; a 300 V part in the wrong package is not an equivalent regardless of where it appears in the results.[1461] Package matters as much as the die: a 2N7000 in SOT-23 rated at 300 mA is no substitute for a power part, whatever the channel type.[1461]

Common jellybean N-channel parts span the range from signal to power. The 2N7002 is ubiquitous and adequate for switching LEDs at tens of milliamps up to about 100 mA.[1736] The AO3400 costs roughly twice as much for around ten times the capability at 30 V, with the AO3401 or 3401A as its P-channel counterpart at the same 30 V and about 4 A continuous.[1736] Larger surface-mount parts include the FDT459N in SOT-223 and the IRLML2803 in SOT-23 — the latter sharing its footprint and marking style with ordinary bipolar switching parts, so that reverse engineering a board may not distinguish a 2N2222 from a small power MOSFET until the circuit around it is drawn out and analysed.[1708][675]

## Switching and power applications

**Low-side load switching.** The canonical use is a low-side switch driven directly from a microcontroller pin: a motor connected across the battery with an N-channel MOSFET returning it to ground under IC control,[284] a PWM LED driver taken straight from a micro pin,[661] or a motor drive output with low-pass filtering on the gate and a back-EMF diode across the load.[759] Reverse-polarity protection can also be built this way, with the N-channel device switching the ground leg and its gate fed from a divider off the positive input, although a P-channel part on the high side is the simpler arrangement.[397]

**High-side switching.** An N-channel device can be used on the high side, with drain and source swapped relative to the low-side case, but the gate must then be driven above the load: V<sub>GS</sub> must reach at least the switched output voltage plus the device's gate-source threshold.[995] This is why P-channel parts are usually chosen for high-side applications despite their worse parameters — grounding the gate pin is a far simpler control operation.[995] Where an N-channel and a P-channel device are combined, the N-channel part often serves as the low-voltage control element that latches and pulls the P-channel high-side switch on.[1520]

**Linear and pass applications.** An N-channel MOSFET used as a series pass element with an op-amp closing the loop is the standard constant-current dummy load: a logic-level MTP30 with an LM324 in the original form,[102] and the same block reappearing as an N-channel device over a bank of ten parallel 1 Ω resistors to make a cheap 100 mΩ sense element,[661] or in commercial hobby chargers where the source returns through a sense resistor tapped off to an ADC pin.[397] The same topology serves a precision current source, with an RFP3005 sourcing 1 A through a four-terminal 1.25 Ω precision resistor dissipating up to 1.25 W in free air.[577] Bench-supply pass elements are the same device class: a CEP60N15 at 150 V and 30 to 60 A depending on package,[861] four high-voltage, high-current IRFP460s on a fan-cooled, temperature-sensed heat sink in a laboratory supply,[268] and a large N-channel power device per channel in a source measure unit, replicated four times with identical values so each section dissipates a quarter of the total.[607][268]

**Switch-mode conversion.** N-channel MOSFETs are the switching element in boost converters, sitting between the inductor and ground ahead of the output diode and filter capacitor,[397] and integrated as on-chip switches in DC-DC controllers — for example a 2 A part specified at 140 mΩ and 75 mΩ.[139] In synchronous buck stages two N-channel devices are used, sometimes paralleled with all their pins shorted together to lower on-resistance.[1606] In inverter service, alternately switched N-channel MOSFETs drive the primary of a step-up transformer, producing a square wave unless sinusoidal drive control is added.[449] In a hybrid solar inverter the split is by voltage: high-voltage IGBTs on the DC bus side and ordinary N-channel MOSFETs on the 48 V battery side, there 100 V devices rated 240 A behind a 150 A fuse.[1620]

**Series-connected charging.** A more unusual arrangement uses pairs of N-channel MOSFETs across each cell holder in a totem-pole configuration, with a constant-current DC-DC output fed into the centre point and each cell's negative terminal tied to the centre point of the pair below it.[811] The result charges the cells in series at around 8 A while allowing any individual cell to be bypassed or switched out by alternating the two FETs of its pair.[811][812]

## Complementary pairs

Where both polarities are needed, N-channel and P-channel parts appear as matched sets. A CMOS inverter is one P-channel and one N-channel device with gates tied together and drains joined at the output, one on whenever the other is off.[941] A CMOS Schmitt trigger input extends this to four stacked devices — two N-channel below, two P-channel above — with two further transistors acting as source followers that feed the output back into the middle of the upper or lower totem segment.[941] The same symmetry governs push-pull power stages: an audio output stage uses matched N-channel and P-channel MOSFETs, or NPN and PNP bipolars, to drive hard to each rail about the centre ground point;[970] a UPS output uses two P-channel and two matching N-channel devices per heat sink;[504] an electronic speed controller uses three N-channel devices on the bottom and three P-channel on the top;[1457] and an AC voltage standard uses a 2SK1058 N-channel part matched with a 2SJ162 P-channel part, each individually fused.[709] Dual devices are also packaged together — a dual N-channel power MOSFET, or an N-channel and a P-channel in a single six-pin SOT-23 suited to totem-pole drive.[1415]

## Robustness and failure

MOSFETs are not as robust as bipolar junction transistors, and this is their principal disadvantage as pass elements.[861] A series pass MOSFET in a bench supply that fails destructively — taking the fuse with it — points at a design that does not adequately protect the device, since a correctly designed product should not permit the pass transistor to be blown up in normal use.[861] Failure is often unambiguous once measured: a short from gate to drain, or drain to source, on a device that should read open.[861] Under severe fault the package itself can be destroyed, as with 60 V 50 A FP50N06 power MOSFETs from a line-interactive UPS whose cases were blown apart.[SoeVNaz7KGc] In a multi-device stage, damage is often confined to part of the array: in a burnt-out speed controller the three P-channel devices along the top rail were destroyed while the three N-channel devices below them survived.[1457]
