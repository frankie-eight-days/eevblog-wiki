# pull up resistor

A pull-up resistor is a resistor connected between a signal node and the positive supply rail, holding that node high whenever nothing else is actively driving it low. It exists because a large class of outputs — open collector, open drain, wired-or comparator outputs — can only sink current and have no transistor capable of driving the line up to the rail, so the resistor supplies the rising edge instead.[1208][1464][fm13tIe5wSc] The same component also defines the resting state of inputs that would otherwise float, sets the off state of switches and gates, and appears in the earliest logic families as a load element.[1611][961][638]

## Open collector and open drain outputs

An open collector output has no totem-pole stage: there is no active device in the output that can drive the pin quickly up to the positive rail, so a pull-up to VCC is mandatory for the high level to exist at all.[1208] The mechanism is visible in a single bipolar transistor stage — base current turns the transistor on and the output is pulled down to 0 V; remove the base current and the output goes positive purely by virtue of the pull-up resistor.[748] Open drain behaves identically in MOSFET terms, and it is not magic: an open drain output switching a 12 V load still needs a pull-up resistor returned to a real voltage, in that case the 12 V input rail, before it can control a MOSFET or solid state relay.[971]

Whether the pull-up is needed at all depends entirely on the output structure. A 555 timer's discharge pin is open collector and needs a 1K pull-up; pin 3, the Q output, is not open collector, so rearranging a PWM circuit to drive from pin 3 instead of pin 7 removes the resistor from the bill of materials outright.[392] The same distinction separates comparator families: an open collector comparator's positive-going transition is set by the external pull-up the designer adds, while nano-power push-pull parts actively drive the output high and need no external pull-up.[1464]

Detection circuits exploit the arrangement directly. In an EPIRB test path, an RF amplitude rising past a threshold switches on a transistor whose open collector output, held up by a pull-up resistor, then switches low so the microcontroller can confirm the transmitter is producing RF.[368] Removing the output transistor, its AC coupling capacitor and the 10K resistor leaves the pull-up with nothing to pull the node down, which is how it was established that the output is ignored during transmission.[368]

## The I2C bus

I2C works over two wires shared by many devices precisely because those wires are open drain: the driving side only ever drives low, and each wire must have a pull-up so that the line returns high when it is not being pulled down.[fm13tIe5wSc][9hMsNOwY5AQ] Driving the pins actively would violate the protocol specification; the only way to meet it is to leave the output high impedance and let the resistor do the work.[9hMsNOwY5AQ] Two resistors returning to the power rail alongside an SCL/SDA pair on a schematic are always this.[fm13tIe5wSc][912]

The value is a speed-versus-power trade-off. The typical recommendation is 2.2k, dropped to around 1k where the bus must run faster or carries more devices, since more devices mean more capacitance, which means longer rise time on the low-to-high transition.[1208] Lower resistance gives a faster response, and the appropriate value tracks the speed grade of the interface.[fm13tIe5wSc][1307] At the extremes the nominal 2K2 of the standard stops being adequate — bus capacitance dominates by the time an interface is advertised at 10 MHz, and pull-up selection becomes the limiting question for a 100 MHz I2C stimulus.[991][fM4bbKVw2og] A rise time failure captured on I2C lines during instrument testing was traced to the pull-up value rather than to the transmitting device.[876] Bus analysers accordingly expose the pull-ups as a user-selectable setting alongside the 100 or 400 kHz speed choice.[1291]

## Rise time, capacitance and slew rate

The pull-up and the load capacitance form an RC that governs every rising edge, and the resulting asymmetry can be severe. In one optocoupler-driven clock path the fall time was 500 ns with the transistor actively pulling down, against a datasheet rise time of 45 microseconds specified for a nominal 4.7K pull-up into 30 pF — a figure representative of a gate's input capacitance.[1208] With a 2K7 pull-up the rise remained in the order of tens of microseconds, far too slow for the clock input it fed, driving the input into a metastable state where the counter skipped or doubled pulses.[1208] Two remedies apply: reduce the pull-up resistor, or interpose a Schmitt trigger that converts an arbitrarily slow edge into a sharp one before the clock input.[1208] Optocouplers are poor at driving logic directly for exactly this reason.[1208]

## Choosing and consolidating values

Selecting a pull-up is genuinely a design calculation rather than a default, and the arithmetic runs into manufacturing economics. If a calculation yields 1.8K and no other 1.8K appears in the design, using an existing 1.5K or 1K value instead costs a little extra power but saves a reel on the pick-and-place machine.[1307] Boards that need a second pass through the machine to load extra reels cost money, so component-by-component consolidation is worthwhile: a 15k pull-up can usually become a 10k already used elsewhere on the board.[127]

CMOS relaxes the constraint at the other end. The 4000 series has effectively infinite input impedance, so 10 meg pull-ups work fine and dissipate essentially nothing, enabling ultra low power designs — with the caveat that 10 meg is not a hard pull-up and copes badly with long lines.[801] Unused inputs on spare logic gates should still be tied rather than left floating; routing the spare input to a solderable pad or via and adding a very weak pull-up of around a meg holds the input defined while leaving the gate available for later bodging.[1611] Historic practice sat between these extremes: diode-transistor logic built its gates from diodes, transistors and pull-up resistors, with the Apollo LVDC modules using a 2.5k pull-up and values generally in the range of several K to tens of K, consistent with a low-current digital machine.[638]

## Internal pull-ups

Microcontrollers commonly provide software-configurable pull-ups, which removes the external part entirely. Optical rotary encoder outputs that would normally need external pull-ups can go straight into the micro with the pull-ups enabled internally, saving components and board space, and an I2C chip with internal programmable pull-ups does not strictly need external ones — external resistors added anyway are candidates for removal on a later revision because they consume layout area.[232][245] GPIO configuration in either register-level or abstracted embedded code sets pull-up state along with mode and speed.[A_saS93Clgk] Some peripheral controllers advertise pull-ups on every one of dozens of software-controlled digital outputs.[377] Internal pull-ups on logic analyser inputs, where present, tend to be weak and unreliable.[436]

## Reset, power and switch circuits

Active low reset pins are normally held high by a pull-up and pulled low only at power-up, so an RC network to VCC on the reset pin provides both behaviours without additional parts.[710] Reset pull-ups are also where marginal designs surface: a 10K pull-up that failed to reliably reset an intercom chip was replaced by progressively lower values until a 1K worked, with 100 ohms known good, pointing at something wrong with the reset pin itself rather than the resistor.[710] Reset nodes are sensitive to loading in general — hooking instruments and parallel resistors to a reset line disturbs the pull-up enough to stop the product switching on.[u2-ot2vWLxI] The sensitivity cuts the other way too: with a 2.2 meg pull-up on the reset pin, a microcontroller can be kept alive through its input protection diodes with the VCC pin disconnected entirely, whereas a 100K pull-up drops too little voltage to matter and cannot supply the chip or its LEDs.[831]

In power switching, a pull-up from gate to source holds a P-channel MOSFET off until a switch grounds the gate.[995] Soft latching switch circuits use a pull-up from the input rail to a transistor base to arm the latch.[262] Soft power buttons routed across a system are traced by following the pull-up: a single pull-up resistor can be at the far end of a flat flex cable from the processor that reads it, with 4.8 volts measurable across the whole path.[378] Power good outputs likewise arrive with a pull-up already fitted.[1710]

Mechanical contacts are read the same way: a switch shorting a pulled-up node to ground generates one counted edge per press in principle, and contact bounce in practice — a single manual press registering hundreds of counts on a totalize-mode counter.[961] Instrument trigger inputs therefore offer selectable pull-up strength, such as 20 mA and 2 mA options, alongside debouncing.[947] Two pull-up resistors to a 9 V rail around an inverter stage allow either junction to be dragged low or driven high without shorting out the rail.[242] Multiplexed front panels use pull-ups and pull-downs feeding a priority encoder to report which range switch position is selected, or sense the position through a node pulled high inside the micro.[853][1520] A refinement puts several switches on one microcontroller pin by switching different resistances into the pull-up divider and reading the result with the ADC — elegant until a tactile switch with contact resistance varying from a couple of ohms to many hundreds corrupts the divider.[1361] A Hall sensor feeding an interrupt pin is wired the same way, with a 1K pull-up between 5 V and the signal pin.[EHLRQbCW5oM]

Pull-up and pull-down strapping can also carry information rather than just a logic level: USB speed grade is signalled by which of the data lines carries a pull-up.[ql7J_tFBVfY]

## Recognising pull-ups on hardware

In reverse engineering, a resistor running from a signal pin to a large copper pad that is almost certainly a power plane identifies itself as a pull-up or pull-down before any measurement is taken.[1475] Resistance measurements then confirm the topology — 100k measured to a common node identifies a network rather than discrete resistors, and 200k between two pins with 100k to each confirms which end is common.[1245] Resistor networks used as pull-ups cluster around microcontroller ports and interface connectors, such as the 100k network on a portable computer's RS-232 port or the array serving a vacuum fluorescent display's control lines.[1662][717] Value alone is diagnostic: on an unfamiliar board some resistors sit at pull-up-like values and others clearly do not.[8qmU7SZu_-I] Not every resistor to a rail is a pull-up, and a 10k to a node with a capacitor on the other side is more likely a bypass arrangement than a pull-up.[1360] Even printed carbon resistors, whose value depends on track length, width and thickness and which are typically 30% tolerance parts, are used for pull-up and pull-down duty.[335]

## Pull-ups as after-the-fact fixes

Omitted or wrongly valued pull-ups are among the commonest reasons for a factory bodge. Sleeved leaded resistors bussed together on a digital IO board, a single leaded resistor tacked onto a CPLD where a pin was probably left floating, and hand-added pull-ups on a computer bus are all recognisable as pull-up mods rather than respins.[738][598][Gbn_51IoJiM] Repairs use the same lever: fitting a low-impedance 100-ohm pull-up to a clock rail restored a NAS whose clock had failed, an inelegant fix but preferable to cutting tracks and running mod wires, with the alternative being a mod board carrying its own 25 MHz oscillator at the risk of losing clock synchronisation.[1288]
