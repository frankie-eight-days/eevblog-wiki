# bill of materials

A bill of materials, universally abbreviated BOM, is the itemised list of every component that goes into a product: quantities, reference designators, descriptions, footprints, values, manufacturer part numbers and supplier part numbers.[1307][127] It is the document that connects a design to the physical world, because it is what the purchasing department buys from and what the assembly house builds from.[676][127] Producing and optimising it consumes far more of a design engineer's time than drawing the circuit or laying out the board — plausibly 80 to 90 per cent of it, spent on parametric searches, sourcing and cost optimisation.[87][239][555]

## What a BOM contains

Most CAD packages will generate one automatically as a CSV or spreadsheet, listing quantity, reference designator, description, value, footprint, and where the library has been populated properly, manufacturer and supplier part numbers.[1307][253][Ep4r-wD7PPs][990][255] For a high-volume product the useful form is a spreadsheet that carries the manufacturer's part number, a supplier part number, and at least one alternate supplier part number — a Digi-Key number, a Mouser number, an Element 14 number — plus a column for reel quantity.[127] Serious manufacturing BOMs go further and list a main supplier plus a set of alternative approved suppliers, so purchasing may buy from any approved source without re-engaging engineering.[1752][1697]

Specifying a part loosely is a real failure mode. A 10 µF bypass capacitor whose BOM entry does not pin a specific manufacturer, part number and model can vary between production batches, and a substituted ceramic can introduce audible piezoelectric behaviour that the original did not have.[855] The same laxity at the purchasing end produced the well-known bad-capacitor monitor failures, where dodgy caps were bought in place of the Panasonic parts the design engineer had specified in the BOM.[347]

Dave Jones's own practice on the µCurrent was to give the assembler a BOM with an item number against every line, and to number the physical reels to match, so reel five and item five cannot be confused; the entry also carries the description and the component designator it populates.[m9tza_c4sxc]

## BOM consolidation

Reducing the number of *different* BOM items — not the number of parts — is the central optimisation.[1307] Every distinct line item is another reel to buy, another slot occupied in the pick-and-place machine, another feeder change in production, another opportunity to run out of stock or order the wrong thing.[1307][239][1281] The more distinct part types on a board, the higher the risk, the greater the assembly cost, and the greater the chance of getting everything manufactured except for one part.[1307] The effect scales: the bigger and more complex the board, the more this matters.[1307]

The consequence is a counterintuitive rule: it is often cheaper and better to use *more* components than to use the theoretically correct one. If a divider calculation produces an oddball 4.99 kΩ, two 10 kΩ resistors in parallel from a reel already loaded beats buying another reel for a single value, at the cost of some board area.[1307] Design practice can bias toward this from the start — fix the bottom resistor of a divider at 10 kΩ and calculate the top one, so only one oddball value is needed instead of two.[1307] The other valid workflow is to complete the design, generate the BOM, and then go back through it looking for consolidations.[1307]

Reuse shows up everywhere once the habit is formed:

- Three 330 µF capacitors already in the design instead of a new 1000 µF line item, with the added benefit that they all come off one reel.[742]
- A second instance of an already-specified beefy MOSFET, mounted in reverse and gate-tied, used purely for its body diode to make a back-to-back protection pair — one less reel on the pick-and-place machine.[931]
- A 1N4007 across a battery rather than a lower-voltage 1N4001, because the 4007 is already stocked for protection elsewhere and the parts are cheap enough that a separate BOM item is not worth having.[1449]
- Diodes and capacitors already in the BOM used to build a voltage doubler or inverter, instead of a dedicated charge-pump chip costing around a dollar.[473][483]
- Transistor zener clamps reused across several places in a design.[1157]
- The same processor carried onto a secondary interface card even though it is overkill there, because the part and its development tools are already committed.[589][571]
- Ballast resistors for current sharing implemented as PCB traces, adding nothing to the BOM at all.[222]

Jellybean components exist to serve this discipline. Parts like the LM317, the LM311 comparator and the common 7400/4000 logic should sit permanently in the CAD library with all their footprints and multiple manufacturers already attached as BOM and supplier entries, so they can be dropped in without further sourcing work.[1438][1464][1611] The habit extends to their support components: a 240 Ω resistor is the nominal fixed leg of an LM317 adjustable divider, so if LM317s are in use the 240 Ω should be loaded on the pick-and-place ready to go, and reused elsewhere — a 220 Ω requirement somewhere else in the circuit can often be met by the 240 Ω already present.[1438]

Consolidation also cuts against itself sometimes. An adjustable regulator is one BOM item but needs three placed parts once its setting resistors are counted, where a fixed regulator is one; a dedicated crystal oscillator module is one BOM item instead of a crystal plus load capacitors, usually more expensive but smaller and pre-characterised.[1147][1614] And commonality should not be pursued past the point where it costs money: expensive banana binding posts justified on a precision input are not justified on an output that permanently connects to a multimeter.[72]

Mechanical parts count too. Using one type of screw throughout an enclosure and its internal modules reduces the BOM, removes a way to get assembly wrong, and means no tool changes during assembly, disassembly or repair.[1281][1717][619] Conversely a light pipe added to bend an LED's output to the front panel, a bus bar substituted for heavy copper, a daughterboard that requires an extra cable and connectors, a single tantalum on a board otherwise free of them, and an extra capacitor for a level-shifting scheme are each a new BOM line with engineering, sourcing and assembly cost attached.[1552][1559][3_CQKSC1JNE][148][1074][1229][72]

## Sourcing and manufacture

Design for manufacture starts by walking the BOM component by component and asking, of each part, whether the chosen assembly house can actually place it — assembly houses differ in their pick-and-place capability and requirements.[127] For a 500-part board that is 500 separate judgements.[239] The next pass is a purchasing pass: every line item is priced on reels or tubes at the intended build quantity, with reels preferred, and excess purchased over what the build needs must be absorbed and recovered through sales.[239] A quantity of 1,100 components at 35 cents each to build 1,000 boards is a typical shape for that arithmetic, allowance for machine wastage included.[239] Alternatively the whole package — board, Gerbers, BOM, components — can be handed to a turnkey assembly house that handles panelisation, fiducials, tooling and procurement.[127]

Specifying a part does not secure it. Once a part is in the BOM the design engineer's control ends and supply chain management takes over, and parts routinely cannot be obtained from the nominal source.[676] This is why alternates matter, and why equivalents must be qualified rather than assumed. Two MOSFETs sharing a number in their part codes are not interchangeable: substituting on top-level specifications alone can ship 100,000 units that suffer field failures because the replacement lacks the ESD structure of the original.[1461] For static switching, matching R<sub>DS(on)</sub> — 21 mΩ in one such comparison — may be sufficient; for switching-mode power conversion, equivalence requires extensive dynamic testing.[1461] Alternates are also insurance against geopolitical supply disruption for a single-source cheap part.[1461] Package variants are a separate trap: ordering the wrong LQFP pin pitch from a supplier offering a good deal on 10,000 units leaves stock that cannot be assembled.[1074]

Manufacturers can also break equivalence silently. A part sold under an identical number can be changed such that a previously connected pin is no longer connected, which purchasing would have no way of detecting; where a manufacturer does assign a new suffix and document legacy-versus-new differences, a properly detailed BOM lets a purchasing officer flag the substitution and refer it back to engineering.[1752] Mixed-brand jellybean parts appearing on one production board are normal and expected under this system — the design should not care which approved manufacturer's LM358 arrives.[1697][1438]

Purchasing discretion also cuts quality. A power supply subcontracted to a specialist who specifies Nippon Chemi-Con capacitors can arrive with Jamicon parts fitted, because the purchasing department found those cheap that week.[824] Tighter control over the BOM and component sourcing is part of what separates a premium instrument manufacturer from one building to a lower price point.[1447]

Some organisations formalise this to the limit. Large vertically integrated manufacturers maintained internal part catalogues where designers could only select from a pre-authorised list, each item carrying an internal part number mapped by separate documents to the actual generic device — an ordinary 74LS245 bus transceiver, for example, appearing on the board only under a house number.[960]

## BOM cost

BOM cost is the primary lever on product cost, and at volume the increments that matter are small. The difference between a 6-cent regulator and a 1-cent one is negligible per unit but becomes real across multiple products at thousands of units each.[1147] Chinese distributors such as LCSC serve this end of the market with domestic brands not carried by the Western catalogue houses.[1147][1306] At the other extreme, specifying a bare die version of a part that costs 5 cents packaged can put a $704 line item in the BOM.[1435]

Features are traded directly against BOM cost. Most multimeters requiring true RMS have to spend on a dedicated converter such as the AD737; a manufacturer with high enough margin may fit the chip regardless and run it in average mode for build consistency, while a cheaper meter simply omits the converter and saves that money.[1448][344] Instrument front-end topology is re-optimised between models to hit price targets, because a handful of premium analog parts in the BOM makes an aggressive price point unreachable.[674] A microcontroller with an integrated LCD driver removes both a separate driver chip and the PCB area it needs, which was the deciding factor in one meter's processor choice.[e9cpKN69Avk][1074] A single-chip microcontroller containing RAM, ROM and I/O made early electronic games possible at a $25 retail price, because everything else was LEDs and moulding.[1111] Choosing a two-cell-maximum battery management part saved a couple of dollars of BOM cost at the price of reducing the achievable output range of the supply.[259]

Cost-reduction can justify respinning a board several times before production, since a chip a fraction of a cent cheaper multiplied across millions of units pays for the respins.[219] Ultra-low-cost products are built to the same logic from the start — a single-chip camcorder with external memory and a couple of passives, a promotional power bank with two pouch cells in parallel and a handful of LEDs, a $5 phone reduced to a main ASIC, memory and an RF chipset on one board.[595][aaxHhCM0P6c][514] Estimating a competitor's or a product's BOM from a teardown is a standard analysis: a $9 process meter's semiconductors totalling perhaps $30 before case and LCD, a smartwatch costed at quantity 1,000, a $5,800 arbitrary waveform generator whose BOM includes power supply, LCD, membrane keypad, mouldings and metalwork, and a tablet whose estimated BOM roughly equalled its $199 retail price — though such estimates tend to understate the buying power of very large purchasers.[1248][761][1679][220]

Cost pressure can also force redesign. Ian Johnston's PDVS2 became too expensive as component prices rose and was redesigned from scratch into the PDVS2 Mini, dropping the rotary encoder, moving to a smaller LCD and a custom CNC aluminium housing, which took a substantial amount off the BOM.[a4Xpsenpd6E]

Not every cost decision is legible from outside. Populating termination resistors on a board whose corresponding feature is disabled costs little in parts but consumes production time and feeder capacity, and is hard to justify on BOM-reduction grounds.[1563]

## Documentation and open hardware

A BOM is a required part of any complete hardware release. Publishing schematics and Gerbers alone is not sufficient: the original schematic and PCB source files, firmware, and the BOM must all be released for hardware to be meaningfully open.[195] This is codified in the SPB labelling scheme and in the gear-logo scheme where separate letters mark the release of schematic, PCB, BOM, documentation, and a commercially permissive licence.[951][921] Releases that omit the BOM are incomplete in practice as well as in principle — a hardware wallet project supplying only Eagle schematic and board files leaves a reimplementer unable to buy parts, since generic Rs and Cs are trivial but the specific LCD and similar parts are not.[1374] A ventilator release consisting of PDF schematics and a service manual without a BOM is unusable for its stated purpose, because the motors and valves are the essence of the device.[1wrin8FYtEo][TltM74rNasM] The same question applies to any open-source announcement: whether it includes the complete BOM and supplier information, or only service manuals and miscellany.[1581]

The practical goal for a modern open design is a BOM that a distributor's upload tool can consume directly. Component suppliers provide BOM import tools that take a CSV of manufacturer part numbers and populate a cart, which removes the manual search step entirely and makes a project genuinely turnkey.[1306][1374] KiCad's interactive HTML BOM plugin, installed separately, provides a cross-referenced assembly view alongside the conventional CSV.[1307] Getting a project into that state is not trivial: importing a design from another EDA tool can require hours of work to clean up footprints, repair the schematic and rebuild the BOM with proper datasheet and supplier links.[1374] Board release can be automated so that DRC, Gerber generation, NC drill files and BOM export happen in one step.[990]

Vintage service manuals set the standard the field has largely abandoned. Manuals from the era routinely carried theory of operation, troubleshooting flowcharts, full schematics, PCB overlays and an exhaustive BOM — running to dozens of pages of part numbers, with individually numbered nuts, screws and every capacitor and resistor given a house part number complete with description, value, tolerance and voltage rating.[116][863][ZBVyJaLAZDY][1662][1376][904][803][272][756][1169][1429] Scanned and OCR'd, such a BOM lets a repairer search a designator like R901 and jump straight to the part.[ZBVyJaLAZDY] The absence of one is immediately felt: a service manual with full schematic and PCB overlay but no BOM reference will not say which op-amp populates the front end, forcing physical disassembly to find out.[529]
