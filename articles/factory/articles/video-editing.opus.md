# video editing

Video editing, in the EEVblog production workflow, is the post-shooting stage in which raw camera clips are assembled on a timeline, trimmed, overlaid with a small number of supporting images, and rendered for upload.[454D6aisiAE][943] The defining characteristic of the process is deliberate minimalism: the edit is treated as a cost to be minimised rather than a creative stage, and almost all production decisions are pushed forward into the shooting itself.[943][1451] The governing rule is that the less time spent editing, the better.[1451]

## The single-pass timeline workflow

The core loop is unvarying. Clips are shot in the order they will appear, dropped into the timeline in that order, and trimmed at the start and end of each clip; a small number of image or text overlays are added, and the project is rendered and uploaded.[454D6aisiAE][3_CQKSC1JNE][1451] Because the footage is already in sequence, no assembly or reordering step is required, which is the single largest saving in the workflow.[454D6aisiAE][cPuMgh-BElU] There is no colour grading and no graphical effects work; the operations amount to joining clips, occasional audio-level tweaks, and a few overlays.[WLcVcOgmzpE][698]

Shooting in sequence is enforced strictly enough to accept redundant physical work during filming. If a close-up lens must be fitted and removed ten times across a shoot to capture successive components on a board, it is fitted and removed ten times, because batching all macro shots for later leaves an unordered pile of clips that costs more time in the timeline than it saved on the bench.[1451] Shooting in sequence is described as a big thing, with getting everything right in camera being critical.[943]

The timeline itself stays shallow: typically two or three video tracks and rarely more than one audio track, with a second audio channel used only when other footage is being mixed in.[943] Projects with a dozen tracks — heavy text fly-ins and overlays — are the exception rather than the practice.[943]

Whiteboard tutorials admit one relaxation. With a single board that will not be wiped, a segment can be reshot after editing has begun if something was omitted, and material can be moved earlier in the timeline without a visible discontinuity, provided clothing and framing continuity are preserved.[2FbwdnMARAw] The same mechanism allows a thought that arrives late in a shoot to be filmed at the end and dropped back into the timeline five or ten minutes earlier.[2FbwdnMARAw]

## Cutting decisions and the two-pass refinement

The first pass is pure trimming of clip heads and tails.[b-v3tz6beN4] A second pass, adopted after roughly a decade of single-pass editing, goes back through the material to remove filler: ums and ahs, pauses, repeated statements, redundant information, and waffle judged not to be helping.[1v2_h-_YHEE][b-v3tz6beN4] The yield is modest — around one to two minutes off a 25-to-30-minute programme — and it does not quite double editing time, but the effect accumulates across a body of work.[1v2_h-_YHEE] Earlier material was not treated this way; repetition was simply left in.[1198][b-v3tz6beN4]

Cuts are frequently made from the audio waveform alone, without playback. A repeated passage or a fluffed take is recognisable by its shape on the timeline, and the cut is executed with a single hotkey bound to the slash key, taking seconds.[4Up3dshwg8Q] The same shortcut is why a 29-minute single-take whiteboard shoot reduces to 19 minutes with essentially no content removed — the ten minutes are goofs, double takes, misstatements and changes of direction.[4Up3dshwg8Q]

Information is preserved in preference to brevity. Raw footage is included by default, and material is only tightened where it is waffle rather than substance: 22 minutes of raw footage might reduce to 18, and 45 minutes to about 35.[943] Where an explanation genuinely requires the running time, it is kept even at forty minutes.[b-v3tz6beN4] The one length-driven intervention is reshooting a passage more concisely — condensing a two-minute explanation into one — to bring a finished piece under the 30-minute or 20-minute psychological thresholds.[2FbwdnMARAw]

## Editing time

Reported effort is roughly proportional to runtime, with a ratio near or slightly above 1:1. A 30-minute piece takes 45 minutes to an hour; an hour-long piece takes two hours at the outside and often much less; an hour of raw footage takes about an hour and a half.[454D6aisiAE][943][500] In the earliest years the ratio was far worse: ten minutes of filming could take an hour or two to edit, plus format conversion, upload and posting.[74] Overhead is dominated not by cutting but by gathering supporting material — locating data sheets and screen captures to overlay — rather than by the trims themselves.[943]

Clip count, not runtime, is the real driver of editing labour. Multimeter shootouts shot as short repeated tests produced 150 and over 200 individual clips, each needing its own start-and-end trim, and took hours.[89][97] Compilation work is worse still: assembling a retrospective requires loading many full-length source programmes rather than isolated clips, which historically brought the editing machine to a crawl and took a week for a hundred-episode compilation.[400]

## Overlays and supporting material

Overlays are the one production flourish considered worth the time: data sheet captures, screen grabs, and annotated stills — for example an image with a red circle drawn around the MOSFET under discussion plus a line of text.[1v2_h-_YHEE][943] Each is quick individually but the total adds up.[1v2_h-_YHEE] On a whiteboard tutorial, sourcing and preparing overlay images can take longer than the cutting.[4Up3dshwg8Q] Text overlays, and in extremis a voice-over, also serve to patch in information realised to be missing during the edit.[2FbwdnMARAw]

Synchronising independent sources is a separate class of work. Two dash-cam feeds from a vehicle — one forward-looking, one on the driver — were inlaid and aligned by hand against a known event to produce a single combined view.[1066] Live-show post-production requires the chat log to be synced against the audio, for which a timestamp is noted during recording so the alignment point can be found again.[400] Separate video feeds from a remote guest must be individually cut and rescaled to size the guest larger than the host.[XAbeXAFpyh0] A green screen was considered and rejected on the grounds that it is difficult to get right, messy, and adds editing work.[ti8mbPIAvbI]

## Unedited output

Not all material is edited. Single-take formats are recorded, stopped, and uploaded straight from the card with no editing, rendering, transcoding, or format conversion, specifically as a way to produce content quickly.[9_qhlFc-MVU][943] Unpolished material is published without any spit and polish in the editing.[1220] Continuous off-the-cuff monologue recordings were an early experiment in exactly this, presented as unedited in contrast to the normal heavily edited segment-by-segment construction.[62] The distinction is maintained deliberately: polished material receives the full two-step editing process, while quick material bypasses it.[T5cRvs44LcE]

## Verification and irreversibility

Individual clips are not watched through and analysed during editing; doing so would take two or three times longer than trimming.[76] The finished render is not reviewed at all. The justification is that trimming every clip amounts to having watched the whole thing already, leaving high confidence that nothing is badly wrong.[454D6aisiAE]

This tolerance is reinforced by the cost of correction after publication. Re-filming and re-uploading a piece to fix a minor error destroys the existing links, resets the view count, and disturbs the ordering of the archive, so published material is generally not revisited.[76] Errors are handled by correction in the accompanying text rather than by re-editing.[76]

## Delegation

Handing editing to someone else is judged unworkable on time grounds rather than quality grounds: reviewing another editor's cut of an hour of material takes about as long as editing that hour personally, which eliminates the saving entirely.[500][XzmuKIYZkNo] A second objection is stylistic — externally edited material would take on the character of a highly produced, polished house style rather than the existing one.[500] Professionally produced and edited programmes of that kind carry very large budgets.[500] The practical answer is that shooting in sequence makes the edit short enough that delegation is not worth the overhead.[454D6aisiAE] Editing work has, however, been brought in-house through hiring.[1763]

Editing is also acknowledged as a tool of misrepresentation: with enough research and selective cutting, material can be assembled so that its presenter appears to know everything off the top of their head.[500]

## Software

The editing application has changed several times. Sony Movie Studio was used for years and was chosen for workflow fit and benchmarked performance rather than for feature depth, on the reasoning that the projects are untaxing.[698] The workflow later moved from Vegas to DaVinci Resolve, which became the standard tool.[4Up3dshwg8Q][3_CQKSC1JNE]

A dedicated hardware speed editor was evaluated against this workflow. Because the operations reduce to placing already-ordered clips on the timeline, setting in and out points, ripple deleting, and start/stop, only about ten percent of the panel's keys would see use.[3_CQKSC1JNE] Automatic silence removal — automatic detection and ripple deletion of gaps — was similarly assessed as unsuitable for main content, applicable at most to long material destined for quick publication, and dangerous if applied across an entire timeline rather than per clip.[cPuMgh-BElU]

By contrast, mid-1990s non-linear editing hardware costing tens of thousands of dollars was limited to motion JPEG and 640 by 480 titling, which was state of the art for the period.[969]

## Hardware and media handling

Footage is shot as AVCHD at 1920 by 1080, around 28 Mbit/s.[943] Files are copied off the SD card before work begins; editing directly from the card is too slow.[943] Mixed-source projects require reconciling cameras running at different frame rates — 60 and 50 frames per second — with output at 50, which cannot simply be dropped onto one timeline without a few minutes of extra handling.[943]

Storage is a four-bay NAS populated with 6 TB drives in a single-redundancy array, giving 18 TB usable, with editing performed directly across a gigabit Ethernet link to the unit rather than from local copies.[taKKSSi0Td8][XsR_5-FJ05M] Working files and raw footage both live there.[XsR_5-FJ05M]

The editing workstation runs three monitors, with a 1080p display used for capture and general browsing and a larger-real-estate display used for the timeline.[qjLXJl0kDjg] GPU-accelerated rendering roughly halves render time on this kind of simple, overlay-light content.[WLcVcOgmzpE] Memory requirements are correspondingly modest: 8 GB is genuinely sufficient for this class of editing and 16 GB provides comfortable margin, with 32 GB fitted only for parity with the desktop.[p287qKMXqxQ] Battery life is the real constraint on mobile editing — 4K editing on a discrete-GPU laptop drains it in barely two hours.[zNG_oNhKCBg] An SD card slot on a machine is valued specifically for ingest.[1695] Editing has at various times been done at a lab, at a satellite office, and at home, with the SD card physically carried between them.[943][500] Transferring several gigabytes of part-edited project to a USB stick to continue elsewhere can itself take twenty minutes and must be planned for.[500]

## Monitoring

Because editing sessions run to many hours and some projects contain hundreds of clips, audio monitoring is a working concern rather than an audiophile one; headphones were the long-standing default before powered studio monitors were sought.[169] Small monitor speakers have been assessed specifically for voicing on video editing work.[1162]
