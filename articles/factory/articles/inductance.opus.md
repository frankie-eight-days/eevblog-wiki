# inductance

Inductance is the property by which a conductor opposes a change in the current flowing through it, storing energy in a magnetic field as it does so. It is measured in henries, and the defining relationship is that the instantaneous voltage across an inductor equals the inductance in henries multiplied by the rate of change of current in amps per second.[1660] Inductance matters in practice for two quite different reasons: it is deliberately designed in, as inductors, chokes, transformers and magnets; and it is unavoidably present everywhere else, because every wire, every PCB trace, every component lead — every conductor in electronics — has some inductance.[1439][1117][304]

## Behaviour in circuits

At the instant a switch closes, an inductor with no established magnetic field behaves as an open circuit: no current flows at all, because the inductance resists the flow of current until the magnetic field builds up.[1406] The current then rises exponentially, reaching 63.2% of its final value in one time constant.[1406] The reverse case is the dangerous one — interrupting the current collapses the field and drives the terminal voltage as far as the physical limits of the inductor allow, so hundreds or even thousands of volts can appear from a source of only a few volts, the magnitude depending on the magnetic properties of the inductor and the amount of inductance present.[1406]

Inductors and capacitors only matter for transient and AC cases; once a DC circuit reaches steady state the inductance no longer matters and only the line resistance remains.[1439] Simulators encode this directly: a DC operating point analysis computes its answer by treating capacitances as open circuits and inductances as short circuits, because that is exactly what those components do at DC.[516]

There is no such thing as a purely inductive circuit.[1728] Real components are equivalent networks — an inductor has inductance in its connecting leads and capacitance between its windings, and a resistor has some inductance of its own — so every practical circuit is a complex one in which voltage and current can be out of phase.[1728]

## Parasitic inductance

Every piece of wire, every trace on a PCB, every conductor has inductance in some form, and even a ground plane has spread inductance.[1117] The consequences are pervasive and mostly bandwidth-related.

Test leads used to jump from a jig to a board are inductors, and an LCR meter must be recalibrated open/short/load at the end of them to take them out of the measurement.[1117] Even at the level of a supposedly pure resistive measurement, inductance in the test leads shows up as a phase angle, and swapping the leads over changes the result.[1473] A power rail probe soldered directly to a board rather than plugged into a two-pin header carries extra inductance and correspondingly less bandwidth,[1733] and shorter probe leads are preferred precisely because they give higher bandwidth, less inductance and less interference.[1631] A fast-edge pulse generator is plugged straight into the scope input with no lead at all, since any lead has inductance and the resulting overshoot and undershoot corrupt the rise time.[304]

The same reasoning applies inside packages. A bypass capacitor built into a crystal oscillator module still sits behind the inductance of the internal lead lengths and traces, so it is not equivalent to a capacitor connected directly to the external ground plane that the measurement is referenced from.[1089] When shorting out an RF bead on a board, a long mod wire can act as an inductor in its own right, so proper surface-mount zero-ohm resistors are the better substitution.[987]

Electrolytic capacitors are specified for ESR at typically 100 kHz because that is roughly where they are purely resistive; above that frequency the inductance dominates and the impedance climbs again, which is why electrolytics are poor at bypassing an IC.[1103]

## Inductance in PCB layout

The default ground strategy is a flood-filled plane — a big low-impedance, low-inductance ground over the whole board — with star grounding reserved for cases where there is a specific known reason for it.[500] Multiple vias placed around a board's outer edge lower the inductance of the ground return,[1193] and dense via stitching top and bottom serves the same purpose on RF boards.[856] In fast ECL designs the inductance of the ground and power planes is essential to operation: such a board almost certainly would not work on two layers, because the edges are too fast and the current demand at each switching event too large.[452] A large ground pin on a connector is sized for inductive reasons — a low-impedance ground path — rather than for current-carrying capability.[1416]

Bypassing is an exercise in minimising loop inductance. Placing capacitors on the underside of a BGA directly beneath the pin, with vias running straight up to the balls, bypasses individual pins and is about the only way to improve further.[1512] Where a design instead relies on one large 3.3 V plane and a single ground plane over the whole board, inductance is not being optimised and a bulk decoupling capacitor or two suffices — a capacitor per pin is not required.[1512] Bulk capacitance can sit further from the chip, even on another board, because it does not handle the high-frequency current spikes and the inductance of the longer traces therefore matters less — though placing it remotely is not good design practice.[326]

## Transmission lines

Distributed inductance is one of the three elements of a transmission line. A line is analysed by breaking it into a lumped element model of Ls, Cs and Rs, each unit length having series inductance, series resistance and shunt capacitance, with the elements chained together and the chain extended as far as needed.[1439][756] The unit length is arbitrary — a centimetre, an inch or a metre — and the same construction is used to simulate long cable runs.[1439][756] The same lumped model underlies oscilloscope probe analysis, where inductance is present in any lossy transmission line model even when it is not drawn.[453]

Modern passive probes achieve their high-frequency performance using lossy coax, simulated with inductance in henries per unit length alongside capacitance per unit length and a conductance term G in siemens, the last of which is usually negligible enough to omit.[1445] A worked probe model uses 210 ohms, 83 pF and an inductance of 208 over a length of 1.2 m.[1445]

## Reactive power

No real power is dissipated in inductance. Real power is dissipated only in resistances, not in the capacitance of wires nor in the inductive magnetic fields of transformers,[1730] yet the reactive current still causes real I²R copper losses elsewhere in the delivery system.[1730] Reactive power Q is computed from the reactive component alone — the current or voltage in just the capacitance or just the inductance — multiplied by the reactance in ohms, which is why an X appears in the formula rather than an R.[1730]

Correction consists of adding the opposite kind of reactive component. Large inductive loads are compensated with parallel capacitance, which is why power stations and factories install capacitor power factor correction banks; a capacitive load, such as the switch-mode supply in a piece of test equipment measured at a power factor of 0.45, is instead compensated by adding inductance.[1730]

## Practical values

A henry is a massive amount of inductance; ordinary work is done in millihenries, microhenries and nanohenries.[827] The extremes bracket a wide range. A niobium-titanium superconducting solenoid in a quantum computer magnet has an inductance of about 10 henries, and at around 100 amps produces roughly 6 tesla.[1594] A 1944-vintage field coil measures 3.4 millihenries.[705] An ultrasonic delay line uses four inductors of about 33 to 37 microhenries for the inner pair and 24 microhenries for the outer pair, with 370 picofarad capacitors on a common pin.[381] At the other end, a standard 0603 capacitor contributes roughly 450 picohenries of parasitic inductance.[9V99J22aiLE]

For a wound core, inductance follows from the AL value given in the core's data sheet: L equals n² times AL, with n the number of turns.[708] A core specified at 17,600 nanohenries gives 17.6 microhenries for a single turn passing through it, and three such cores stacked together sum to on the order of 50 microhenries — a substantial inductance for a high-frequency oscillator running at hundreds of kilohertz or into the megahertz range, and far too large to be dismissed as a bare wire with no coil in the circuit.[708]

Surface-mount power inductors trade inductance against current. Higher inductance in a given case size comes with a lower rated current, so a 470 microhenry part in the same package is unusable where a 1 microhenry part rated at around 1.8 amps will do; case sizes such as 1210, 1812 and 2220 set the available range, and a part too small for the current is a failure waiting to happen.[1299]

## Measurement

Most modern multimeters measure capacitance, but very few can measure inductance — that is the distinguishing capability of an LCR meter.[115] Beyond L, C and R, a good LCR meter also yields quality factor, dissipation factor and ESR.[115]

The underlying technique is the same across instruments. A known waveform is applied through a series shunt resistor; the voltage across the device under test and the current through it are measured, and because the generating micro knows the phase of its own test signal, the phase relationship is known too.[81] From voltage, current and phase, every parameter follows — inductance, capacitance, reactance, series and parallel resistance, dissipation factor and quality factor.[81][757] The same method is what turns an impedance analyser instrument into an LCR meter.[858][2] Results are reported in series or parallel form, each paired with Q or D as a secondary reading.[757] Accurate phase is the binding constraint: a scope intended for frequency response analysis needs very low phase error — on the order of ±70 picoseconds between channels — precisely because measuring inductance or capacitance depends on it.[1119]

Measured inductance is not a single fixed number. It changes with test frequency and with whether series or parallel mode is selected, and LCR meter accuracy specifications are typically met only in the middle of each range, degrading at the extremes.[115] A handheld LCR meter reading 1,055 microhenries and 2.6 ohms series resistance at 1 kHz on a surface-mount inductor compares well against a DC resistance measurement of 2.5 ohms on the same part.[115] Bench-grade tweezer meters quote figures such as 0.5% on capacitance and inductance with a measurement range topping out around 10 kHz.[477] Cheap component testers degrade sharply with value: a 1 millihenry inductor reads reasonably, a 10 microhenry part suffers poor resolution, and a 1 microhenry part cannot be measured at all — the tester simply reports a resistor.[1020] An auto-ranging tweezer meter with its probes shorted may report inductance as the primary parameter and resistance as a 0.02 ohm secondary, when resistance is plainly the dominant quantity.[1375] An ordinary multimeter can be pressed into service for at least usable indications of reasonably sized inductors, provided units are tracked carefully.[1465]

Using coil inductance as a position sensor — measuring the small difference between two coils as an iron core moves, by feeding a low-level signal through when the coil is not being driven — is technically conceivable but not a solution to be relied on.[1351]
