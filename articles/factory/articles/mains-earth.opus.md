# mains earth

Mains earth is the protective earth conductor of the AC supply, brought into an appliance on the third pin of its IEC or mains connector and bonded to the chassis and to any exposed metalwork. In test and measurement equipment it is not merely a safety conductor: it is the reference node that the instrument's measurement common is usually tied to, which makes it the single most consequential fact about how a bench instrument may be connected to a circuit under test.[279][131] Almost every hazard, measurement artefact and blown input on an earthed bench traces back to the fact that mains earth is common to every earthed instrument, every earthed mat and every earthed device under test simultaneously.

On a schematic and on chassis hardware the chassis mains earth symbol is distinct from the internal signal ground symbols, and the two are not interchangeable.[279]

## Earth referencing in oscilloscopes

On almost all benchtop oscilloscopes the outer metal ring of the input BNC is mains earth grounded.[279][131] The shell is connected through to the earth pin of the IEC inlet by a very low impedance path — measured directly between the earth pin and the BNC shell, the reading is 0.9 ohms.[279] "It's effectively a dead short."[279] Because the probe's ground clip lead is bonded to that shell, the alligator or crocodile clip on the end of the probe sits at mains earth potential too.[279][131]

The consequence is asymmetric. Clipping the ground lead onto a point in a genuinely floating circuit does no damage; it simply mains earth references that node, which in most cases has no effect beyond possible hum in audio work.[279] Clipping it onto any node of a circuit that is itself already mains earth referenced — anything other than that circuit's own earth — creates a fault loop: current flows from the node, through the probe lead, through the BNC shield, through the oscilloscope, out through mains earth, back through the power board and the IEC lead into the device under test.[279][131] The ground lead and the instrument input are what absorb it.[131]

The path has to be able to carry that fault current. Early Tektronix TDS220 boards were recalled and modified with a strap taking the earth terminal directly down to the BNCs, because the original path did not have low enough impedance to carry the current safely through to mains earth; a fault could destroy a channel and leave it floating.[690] Later Tektronix designs make the point emphatically — the 2 Series has a substantial dedicated grounding point and issues a boot-time warning insisting the earth terminal be connected.[1477]

## Working safely around an earthed reference

The first rule when probing unfamiliar equipment is not to assume that a black wire or a labelled ground in the target is mains earth referenced.[1360] Where it matters, the assumption is tested rather than trusted: buzz the suspect node against a known mains earth point, confirm it reads essentially zero ohms, and confirm there is no AC voltage on it.[1360] The same buzzing technique identifies polarity on unmarked connectors — a barrel connector whose outer shell goes to mains earth must be the negative.[pKV_JiauAE4][1071]

Where the target genuinely cannot be earth referenced, the alternatives are a high voltage differential probe or a floating system.[1360] A differential probe removes the constraint on the input side but not on the output: the common of its output is connected to the oscilloscope and therefore through to mains earth, so its common mode voltage is measured with respect to mains earth and its common mode rating is the limit that matters.[932] Floating means either battery power or a supply that is not itself mains earth referenced.[319] A circuit powered from a floating bench supply can be probed anywhere with impunity — until something re-earths it, such as plugging in a USB cable to a mains-powered computer.[717]

Conversely, where everything else in a setup is floating, deliberately commoning one node to mains earth is the safe move rather than the dangerous one.[1557] The same logic makes secondary-side repair work routine: once the chassis is confirmed to be mains earth and the output is mains earth referenced, the scope probe can go on the chassis and the secondary side probed freely, while the primary side remains off limits.[1301][1664] An element or panel confirmed to be at mains earth can be touched.[1481]

## Soldering irons and live work

A mains-earthed soldering iron tip is shorted through to mains earth, which is why soldering on a powered circuit that is not floating shorts the circuit through the tip.[319] Tip earthing is a design decision that varies: the Quick TS11 runs the earth through to the front so the tip is earthed, with a 1 megohm resistor from mains earth as well, while the JBC CD-2BB takes earth from the mains inlet only as far as the main board, leaving the tip isolated from earth.[1645][472] Internal earth wiring is also easy to misread — on the Bakon BK212 a connection that looks like a mains earth is in fact the sleep-sensing line back to the processor.[OvGdE5hC1Ro]

The hazard extends to component-level rework inside earthed instruments. Replacing the calibration backup battery in an HP 3478A requires care because the battery's negative terminal is mains earth referenced.[427]

## ESD bench earthing

Anti-static mats and wrist straps should be referenced to mains earth, though a mat retains some usefulness unearthed because charge still dissipates across its surface and into the bench.[400] A dissipative mat is built as a very high resistance top surface over a conductive backing, and it is the backing that is taken back to earth.[679]

The correct topology is a star: every strap and every mat runs its own lead back to one central mains earth point, and mats are never daisy chained in series.[679] Each path carries a 1 megohm protection resistor, either built into a proper bonding point or into the strap lead itself.[679] That resistor is not optional — a direct zero ohm short between the wearer's body and mains earth is dangerous, and the resistor is what stands between the operator and anything that goes live.[679] A typical strap therefore sits at a couple of megohms to mains earth.[1567] A grounding bonding point wired back to mains earth, with the wrist strap plugged into it, is the minimum practical provision for a home lab.[107]

## Earth as a measurement reference and a noise path

Because mains earth is shared, it couples as well as protects. A dodgy or absent earth connection at a probe raises the high frequency noise on a trace visibly, and a direct mains earth connection attenuates it substantially.[f58qoyCETAE] The Keysight U1272A EMC anomaly involved conducted noise referenced to a mains earth referenced output, with the coupling persisting whether or not the operator was tied to the ESD mat.[933] Earth referencing is also enough to drive a display: with a common pin returned to mains earth, LCD segments can be turned on and held on by the electric field picked up by a body alone, then decay slowly.[1044]

In precision measurement the earthed shield is doing a specific job. The outer shield of a triaxial connector on a source measure unit goes to mains earth to keep out 50 Hz mains hum, with a separate inner guard shield inside it for the measurement itself.[607] EMC pre-compliance hardware makes the choice explicit, with a switch to connect either directly through mains earth or through an inductive filter, plus a separate case earth.[993] In an anechoic test facility, the connector shell is at mains earth purely for safety, so that an accidentally energised fixture does not become a hazard.[202]

## Earthed versus floating instrument architecture

Whether an instrument's front-panel common is earthed is a deliberate design choice with direct implications for how it can be interconnected. Bench multimeters are expected to float: the HP 3457A front panel ground has no connection whatsoever through to mains earth, and in the Keithley DMM7510 the input shielding and coax braid are tied not to mains earth but to the negative input, so the measurement ground is not earth referenced.[426][731] The NI VirtualBench is mains earth referenced throughout, with the earth pin soldered to the ground plane, except for the multimeter, which floats.[875]

Mixed architectures are common and easy to get wrong. On a Siglent SDG5000, two of the rear connectors are mains earth referenced and three are isolated, distinguishable by the presence or absence of the white insulating collar.[497] The current monitor output of a BK Precision 8601 electronic load is isolated from mains earth, but is tied to the negative input terminal rather than being truly floating.[862] The pass/fail output of an Owon SDS oscilloscope is not isolated from mains earth or from the inputs at all.[480] Where the reference is left to the user, a mains earth binding post or terminal is provided so the outputs can be earth referenced by choice: an Agilent 6643A supply takes an optional earth binding post, and a variable frequency converter with isolated outputs brings out a ground that connects through to mains earth if an earth referenced output is wanted.[667][449] Interconnect ratings are quoted against it — a mystery instrument input rated 100 V AC/DC maximum also carries a separate 300 V to mains earth limit.[1334]

Isolation can also be deliberately bypassed. A Ness D16X alarm panel is fed from a three-wire plug pack that carries earth around the transformer rather than through it, so the panel's sensor ground — and everything referenced to it — sits at mains earth.[682]

## Earth bonding as a build-quality indicator

Inside an appliance the earth path is judged by its mechanical and electrical integrity. Good practice is a dedicated mains earth wire running to the earth terminal, terminated in a properly crimped spade lug with a shake-proof or split washer, giving a very low impedance path.[828][842][791][903] Creepage between mains and earth is checked as well — around 5 to 6 mm in a Rohde & Schwarz NGP800.[1293] Cabinet and chassis parts are bonded for the same reason, including the die-cast front of a studio monitor and the earthed racking of a roof solar array.[1156][1385] Where the bonding is not what it appears, it shows up on the meter: in one HEPA fume extractor the mains plug earth is not connected through to the chassis stud, only through a wire to the motor.[1305]

Earth and neutral are deliberately not joined inside a product; under a multiple earth neutral system they are bonded only back at the distribution point.[1171] Transient protection is referenced to earth as well — MOVs from the input lines down to mains earth, and, in a Keysight EDU34450A, a 100 ohm resistor to mains earth in series with a MOV and a spark gap.[682][1382] Supply outputs commonly carry suppression capacitors to mains earth.[667]

Claims made about earthing are also a useful test of a product's honesty. A television antenna marketed on the basis that its special circuitry is grounded appears to do nothing more than couple to mains earth through a capacitor, which is not a mechanism by which it could receive anything.[LzAUa_FtuC0]
