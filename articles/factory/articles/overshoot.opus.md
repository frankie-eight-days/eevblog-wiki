# overshoot

Overshoot is the excursion of a signal beyond its intended final value before it settles, and its mirror image, undershoot, is the excursion below.[859][304] It matters because the excursion is real energy delivered to whatever is connected: a supply set to 3.3 V that overshoots to 4 V on switch-on can damage the board it is powering, and a logic edge that overshoots into an indeterminate region can be latched as several clock pulses instead of one.[315][1365] Overshoot appears wherever a system with energy storage is driven faster than it can settle — power supply control loops, amplifier compensation, transmission lines, thermal controllers — and in nearly every one of those domains its presence or absence is a headline acceptance criterion.[224][629][487][VVOi2dbtxC0]

## Physical causes

In a digital power distribution network the culprit is inductance. An ideal output would switch from 0 V to 5 V with no ringing, overshoot or undershoot; the real waveform rings because of inductance in the supply trace, which cannot be eliminated by local bypassing alone since the capacitor cannot sit exactly on the pins, and because of further inductance inside the package in the bonding wires to the die.[859]

On fast edges the mechanism is transmission-line reflection. Reflections from an unterminated or badly matched line produce overshoot and undershoot that arrive back in time to corrupt the following symbol, which is the intersymbol interference component of an eye diagram budget.[1247] The same non-perfect transmission line shows up directly as level problems at a USB 3.0 receiver.[340] A pulse generator with a 350 ps edge must be plugged straight into the scope with no lead at all, because any lead has inductance and even the PCB traces, laid out poorly, degrade the rise time and add overshoot and undershoot.[304]

Capacitive loading is the third mechanism. Stray capacitance alone — the wire and the circuit board, with nothing deliberately added — is enough to produce visible overshoot and undershoot on an amplifier output.[n7XX75S98e8] Adding load capacitance makes the response progressively worse until the gain peaks and the output overshoots severely.[l2LBkXxN81Y]

Probing adds overshoot that is not in the circuit. A channel run without a ground connection shows conspicuous overshoot and undershoot on fast transitions, degrading signal integrity even though the underlying event is still visible for timing purposes.[1362] Between two active probes on the same signal, the one with the longer ground lead shows extra undershoot and a slower recovery while the other shows extra overshoot.[1368] AC coupling itself generates an apparent overshoot at the start of a captured burst that has nothing to do with the signal under test.[762]

## Bench power supply switch-on

Switch-on behaviour is among the most important criteria for a lab bench supply: the output must ramp cleanly to the set voltage and stay there, not spike above it and then settle back.[224][8] The test is a single-shot scope capture of the output as the supply is switched on, repeated at several set voltages and with the load in constant-current mode at various currents, since the behaviour differs between them.[439][315] A supply that does not overshoot is safe to put on a board that cannot tolerate more than its rail; one that does is not.[1691]

Well-behaved units ramp almost linearly and then clamp into voltage regulation with no overshoot at all — observed with a linear ramp under full load and again with no load, with a switch-on of roughly 300 ms, with about 50 ms from 0 to 5 V and identically from 0 to 45 V, and with about 2 ms of ramp into a 2 Ω load at 3.3 V with a 2 A limit.[439][1691][1265][1402] Small artefacts short of true overshoot are routinely tolerated: a knee or step partway up the ramp, a brief shelf, or a barely visible excursion on exit from constant-current mode described as "nothing to write home to your mom about".[224][315][1265]

Failures are judged harshly and by repetition. A supply set to 3.3 V that ramps up to 4 V is unfit for powering a large 3.3 V board, and the finding is confirmed by re-triggering to establish that the behaviour is repeatable rather than a one-off; changing the constant-current load to 1 A produced a different but still unclean switch-on from the same unit.[315] Switch-on testing is also applied to DC-DC converters at full load, where a 5 V nominal output was seen reaching just over 6 V and taking around 50 ms to come back down.[324]

## Loop compensation and stability

In a compensated amplifier, overshoot is the deliberate design target rather than something to eliminate. The intended step response is a slight taper — driven to the point of a small amount of overshoot, which indicates the circuit is stable while remaining fast; a fully critically damped response is not aimed for, and a response tuned any further risks turning into an oscillator once production variations are accounted for.[629]

The complementary use of overshoot is as a stability stress test. Deliberately loading an op-amp output with worst-case capacitance to force severe overshoot, and confirming that the circuit still does not oscillate under that condition, is a sound way to qualify a compensation fix short of a full network-analyser sweep.[l2LBkXxN81Y][n7XX75S98e8] Output capacitance also damps transient overshoot in a current source: with the 470 µF output capacitor removed the circuit remained stable but produced a large amount of overshoot on transients.[577]

Overshoot on the output of a peak detector appears when the input frequency rises and the discharge load is too large for the circuit to track.[490]

## PID control

In a temperature control loop, overshoot is the excursion past the set point that follows an over-large accumulated control effort, and its principal cause is integral windup — the build-up of accumulated error area behind the current reading.[VVOi2dbtxC0] Where the integral component dominates, integral windup is the likely source of the observed overshoot, and the standard countermeasure is to clamp the integral term to a fixed maximum value.[VVOi2dbtxC0]

Tuning trades the three terms against each other. Increasing the integral component removes steady-state error but substantially increases overshoot, as does increasing the proportional component; the derivative component, which resists change, then reduces the resulting oscillations and overshoot because both are forms of change.[VVOi2dbtxC0] Starting from Ziegler-Nichols constants, reducing the integral component kills the oscillation, and taking the average of the pre- and post-reduction constants recovers response time at the cost of a little overshoot, which the derivative term is then tuned to handle.[VVOi2dbtxC0] The same criterion applies to soldering station firmware: a station whose tip temperature does not overshoot has an adequate PID loop regardless of other complaints about it.[1106][1110]

## Overshoot as an instrument specification

Oscilloscopes expose overshoot as an automatic measurement, generally split four ways by edge and by side of the edge: overshoot after the rising edge, preshoot before the rising edge, and the falling-edge equivalents, sometimes under opaque abbreviations such as FOV, FPRE and ROV.[797][1220][474][1501]

Instruments are also judged on the overshoot they add to a clean input. A 100 MHz scope was distinctly ringy on a 700 ps pulse with visible overshoot and undershoot where a competing 100 MHz model was not, and a 500 MHz instrument on the same pulse showed overshoot and undershoot attributable to its greater bandwidth rather than to a fault.[487] Aggressive sin(x)/x interpolation is a known source of artificial overshoot and undershoot on reconstructed edges; an instrument without it shows little of either.[1231] A pocket instrument that produced gross overshoot and undershoot on a 1 MHz square wave, with no compensation adjustment available on the instrument or the probe, was useless for high-frequency detail.[359] Pulse response also depends on the source and on the vertical scale in use, so the same generator into different instruments yields differing degrees of overshoot and ringing.[6XpyOGw6RFM]

Digital multimeters are subjected to a simple overshoot test: with all meters reading a steady voltage of about 3 V, the source is switched off and back on and each display is watched for an excursion past the final reading.[99] Meters that merely ramp up slowly without overshooting pass.[99][60] Overshoot on the display is treated as a minor defect rather than a disqualifier when the rest of the meter is good.[91]

Probes carry an overshoot budget of their own. Passive probe design is largely a matter of getting the physical properties of the cable and its lossy transmission-line parameters matched to the compensation network so that no overshoot or undershoot appears at the top of the band.[453] In a probe design tool optimising step response for a 1.4 m cable of 140 pF, 22 Ω was the optimum series value, with any departure from it changing the overshoot in one direction or the other.[341] Plain unterminated coax used as a ×1 probe shows overshoot and ringing.[453] Overshoot is also a datasheet line item for amplifiers, and its removal from the specification table of a revised NE5532 datasheet is a loss of characterisation data.[1752]

## Measurement consequences

Overshoot corrupts derived measurements. Rise-time-to-bandwidth calculations using a 0.4 factor depend on the waveform having as little overshoot as possible, on a sufficiently extended pulse, and on knowing which formula and factor the particular instrument implements.[311] A pulse source producing overshoot followed by ringing compromises the rise-time figure it is meant to establish.[316] In true-RMS measurement, overshoots and spikes raise the crest factor of the waveform, which is why converters intended for such signals provide both a main averaging capacitor and an additional higher-frequency one.[972]

Not all overshoot warrants attention. Overshoot on a digital bus line being probed for protocol content can be ignored, since the interest is in the data rather than the edge quality.[1522] Overshoot introduced by the horizontal position control of a scope's user interface is an instrument annoyance rather than a signal property.[771] Overshoot and undershoot on top of DC characteristics are simply part of the dynamic behaviour a PCB layout has to accommodate, alongside transient response to large load steps.[1216]
