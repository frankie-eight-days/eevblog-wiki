# duty cycle

Duty cycle is the fraction of a repeating waveform's period during which the signal is in its active state, conventionally expressed as a percentage of on time to total period.[225][NvIv-0-R6qQ] For a periodic pulse train at a fixed repetition rate, the frequency stays constant while the duty cycle varies from 0 % — the output never leaves the low state — up to 100 %.[225] A signal at 20 % duty cycle is on for 20 % of the time and off for the other 80 %.[NvIv-0-R6qQ]

It matters because it is the control variable behind pulse-width modulation, and through PWM it sets average voltage, average current, brightness, motor and fan speed, and switching converter output — all from a purely digital output pin.[225][717][-9l41zNWlxY]

## Duty cycle as an analogue control variable

A PWM signal is nothing more than a digital waveform with a varying duty cycle, so it can be generated by a dedicated PWM peripheral in a microcontroller or, failing that, bit-banged on any general-purpose I/O pin.[225] A hardware PWM module is preferred where available, since it generates the waveform without software intervention.[225]

Passing that waveform through an RC filter averages the on time into a linearly proportional DC voltage spanning the full logic swing.[225] A 10 % duty cycle on a 5 V rail filters to one tenth of 5 V, or half a volt.[225] This makes a PWM output plus a filter functionally a digital-to-analogue converter: a value from 0 to 100 % is converted into a duty cycle from 0 to 100 %, and the filter turns that into a proportional voltage.[225] In a lab power supply control loop driven from a 3.3 V microcontroller rail through a gain-of-two amplifier, a 20 % duty cycle produces the expected 1.3 V from a 6.6 V full-scale range, and the relationship holds down to 1 % and 0.1 % duty cycle.[225]

The averaging behaviour also underlies power ratio arguments. Where the on current is fixed, shrinking the duty cycle — a short active burst against a long sleep interval — reduces average current proportionally, and battery life rises accordingly.[1331] The same reasoning applies at extreme peak powers: accelerating structures energised only for 10 microsecond bursts draw very high peak power but, because the duty cycle is very low, dissipate a relatively low average.[836]

Duty cycle also enters switching converter design directly, where the on-time to off-time ratio T_on/T_off is the quantity computed from the datasheet formula and the desired output voltage.[110]

## Applications

**Display brightness.** Multiplexed vacuum fluorescent displays are driven with bursts, and adjusting the duty cycle and frequency of that drive sets display brightness.[717] LED brightness control by PWM behaves the same way, with a 555-based modulator sweeping illumination smoothly across the range.[392]

**Fan control.** A thermal fan controller with a low and a high temperature threshold ramps the PWM duty cycle on its output pin linearly from 10 % up to 100 % between the two setpoints.[-9l41zNWlxY]

**Signalling.** The pilot signal on an electric vehicle charger is a 1 kHz square wave whose duty cycle encodes the available current; 53 % corresponds to the full 7 kW, roughly 32 A.[1437]

**Phase control.** In a triac circuit, adjusting the timing resistors shifts the conduction duty cycle of the current through the device, which is equivalent to controlling its turn-on and turn-off points within the AC cycle.[1172]

**Clock timing constraints.** Duty cycle is occasionally a hard requirement rather than a control knob. DMA memory processing in a Tandy 1000 required a fixed 4.77 MHz clock at 33 % duty cycle; substituting a different clock crashed the machine, so a turbo modification had to synthesise the correct signal regardless of the CPU speed selected.[32]

**Noise rejection.** Threshold-based detectors can exploit duty cycle as a discriminator: a Schmitt-trigger stage biased so that only a correctly shaped input crosses the thresholds will reject noise that arrives at the wrong duty cycle.[267]

**Thermal lifetime.** Equipment specified for a long service life is rarely run continuously, and the fact that a public-address amplifier would never be driven at 100 % duty cycle for speech was part of the argument that its electrolytics would not spend their rated hours at peak temperature.[354]

## Where duty cycle causes damage

Driving a liquid crystal segment with a plain digital output technically works but is an incorrect method.[1045] A 50 % duty cycle from a 5 V drive signal leaves a 2.5 V DC average across the cell, and DC offsets on an LCD will eventually destroy it.[1045] The correct approach avoids any net DC component rather than relying on duty cycle balance alone.[1045]

## Generating and setting duty cycle

Older function generators built square waves by slewing a triangle wave into a comparator, which limited them to a handful of fixed slew rates and a minimum duty cycle of about 10 %.[1032] Direct waveform synthesis lifts that to below 1 % with continuously variable slew rates, which materially widens what the instrument can be used for on the bench.[1032] A typical analogue function generator's duty cycle control covers roughly 20 % to 80 %, and 20 % is a common lower stop in practice.[5][225] Vendors marketing pulse-specific modes cite lower phase noise and jitter along with finer resolution in setting duty cycle and pulse width.[497]

Wide-range modulation is achievable with very simple hardware. A 555 astable driving an LED reaches only about 8.8 % maximum duty cycle when taken from the output pin, but moving the measurement and drive to the discharge pin — the not-Q output of the internal flip-flop, of which pin 3 is a buffered inverse — yields a smooth sweep from roughly 0.3 % to better than 99 %.[392] The oscillation frequency shifts somewhat across that range, which for a brightness control is inconsequential.[392]

Extremely asymmetric duty cycles have diagnostic uses. Multimeter continuity latching is probed with a pulse whose duty cycle is 99.9 % or higher, so the input goes low for only 0.1 % of the period — enough to reveal whether the meter latches on a brief open.[1671] Battery-powered instruments with automatic power-off are similarly tested with a slow, low-duty stimulus: a 100 mHz signal at 10 % duty cycle, one 0 V to 5 V event every 10 seconds, checks whether periodic activity resets the sleep timer.[BuFoA-qt1PY] Discharge-load experiments run the other way: a low discharge resistor value drives a self-oscillating load to essentially a 50/50 duty cycle, and at 130 ohms the circuits did not work at all, requiring an increase to 180 ohms.[1653]

Some instruments carry a duty-cycle-defined output for no obvious purpose, such as an industrial handheld meter's 600 Hz, 50 % duty cycle pulse output.[832]

## Measurement

Duty cycle is a standard horizontal measurement on digital oscilloscopes, sitting alongside frequency, period, rise and fall time and pulse width.[1751][704] Some instruments break it out further, offering negative duty cycle as a separate reading.[480] Logic analysers offer the same set — width, period, duty cycle and frequency — over digital captures.[436] Universal counters provide duty cycle among their phase and timing modes.[869] Function and pattern generators expose it as a definable parameter on synthesised clocks, alongside frequency, idle time and output type.[692][f_SdM6sXHD4]

Duty cycle measurement is also common on handheld multimeters, generally grouped with the frequency function and reached from the same button in voltage or current modes.[249][10][1083][1731] Applied to a 1 kHz square wave at 20 % duty cycle, such a meter reads back 20 % and 80 % correctly as the source is varied, with the corresponding 0.2 ms and 0.8 ms high and low pulse widths.[249] The pulse width reading, however, degrades in the presence of large DC offsets — accurate at low offset, but jumping at an offset of 2.5 V.[249]

Adjusting the source duty cycle is a standard way to exercise a probe or front end. Sweeping the duty cycle of a GaN switching stimulus while observing it through an optically isolated probe demonstrates common-mode rejection on the order of 100 dB, a measurement a differential probe could not make cleanly because of ringing.[1557]
