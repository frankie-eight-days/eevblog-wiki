# solar power

Solar power is the conversion of sunlight into electricity by photovoltaic cells, and in its dominant practical form it is a rooftop DC generator feeding a grid-tied inverter.[1417] It is treated throughout the corpus as an established, well-understood technology whose engineering interest lies not in whether it works but in whether a given installation pays for itself, and in what a household has to do to actually consume what it generates.[743][681] Rooftop solar is characterised as simple and reliable, and simultaneously as a borderline economic proposition: "solar power is already a borderline marginal payback product," requiring a 10- to 20-year service life before the dollar payback closes.[743]

## Economics of a domestic installation

Installed system cost is quoted in dollars or euros per watt rather than per square metre, because output scales with rated capacity rather than area.[681] Market saturation drives that figure: with Germany producing over 50% of its electricity requirements from solar, German installed cost ran about half that of the United States and Japan, giving a generous benchmark of roughly 2 euros per watt total installed.[681]

The payback arithmetic is dominated by the gap between the retail import price and the export price. A domestic system generating 858 kWh over an 85-day billing period was credited at 6 cents per kilowatt-hour exported, roughly a quarter of the buy price from the grid, against a quarterly bill of $274.[724] Export credits have continued to fall, to the point that households with solar find themselves receiving power bills again — the underlying cause being consumption at night, not generation during the day.[eldXifbNm2A] The residual value of a kilowatt-hour that must be exported is low enough to be described as a pittance.[iavM2IqueM8]

Network supply charges move in the opposite direction. Distribution businesses attribute rising fixed daily charges to the new lines required for connected solar, so a household can face an increasing standing charge even as its energy consumption falls.[GnUiB8Zh4FE] Australia is noted as having the world's largest available land area for solar alongside the largest uranium and coal reserves.[GnUiB8Zh4FE]

## Self-consumption as the design problem

Because exported energy is worth a fraction of imported energy, the practical engineering objective of a domestic system shifts from generating more to consuming what is already generated. Every controllable load in the house becomes a candidate for daytime scheduling.[iavM2IqueM8]

Hot water is the largest such load and the most amenable to time-shifting. A heat pump hot water system is used explicitly as a thermal battery, storing excess solar energy as hot water rather than exporting it.[iavM2IqueM8] The approach depends on a household pattern of evening rather than morning showers, so the tank can be charged during daylight by the unit's built-in timer.[iavM2IqueM8][pXtSybs9QRs] A 415-litre tank against an actual draw near 175 litres per day gives substantial thermal margin; the modelled consumption of about 1.7 kWh per day is drawn against an 8 kW nominal array that will still deliver on the order of a kilowatt on the worst overcast day.[pXtSybs9QRs][iavM2IqueM8] Costed at $5,500 installed against roughly $722 per year saved on gas, with the input energy effectively free because it would otherwise be exported, the payback lands near 7.6 years before any allowance for future price rises.[iavM2IqueM8]

The same logic drives smaller decisions: dishwasher and hot-wash laundry cycles run during the day, and a heat recovery ventilation unit drawing an average 31 W is run without concern because the surplus exists to cover it, with the option of dumping further excess into an inline duct preheater in cold climates.[iavM2IqueM8][K1vt6t0P81g] Where a load has no native solar-tracking control, a plain mechanical timer switching on at 9:00 a.m. and off around 4:00 p.m. is sufficient to bias a portable battery's recharging into daylight hours.[5JT8M8v_zNg]

## Electric vehicle charging

An electric vehicle is the largest discretionary load a solar household can add, and home charging from excess generation removes essentially all fuel cost from the vehicle.[1432][1337] The comparison point is stark: a public DC fast charger at 60 cents per kilowatt-hour is more than double the domestic retail rate and unboundedly worse than solar that would otherwise be exported.[zW_Po9XNSxk]

Surplus-following chargers implement this directly. In the Zappi's eco-plus mode the charger modulates its output so that total household draw tracks solar production, taking nothing from the grid and exporting nothing; logged against a monitoring system, the consumption curve matches the generation curve to within roughly ±10 or 20 W, deviating only briefly while the control loop settles between the vehicle and the charger.[EYx46kRv2Bw][hn7qHJ8CKHs] The tracking holds down to about 1.2 kW, and shading transients can stop and restart the charge session outright.[EYx46kRv2Bw] Fast mode overrides the surplus constraint and charges at the full 7 kW regardless of generation; a front-panel LED green indicates all charging power is coming from solar, orange a mixture, and the session summary reports the percentage of energy that came from solar.[EYx46kRv2Bw][1437]

The mode is not without faults. In full sun with 6 to 7 kW available, a unit placed into eco-plus mode has been observed to sit idle waiting for surplus, and on another occasion to draw a fixed 6.5 kW without following the household profile at all — behaviour inconsistent with the same unit tracking correctly on adjacent days.[hn7qHJ8CKHs]

## Battery storage and grid interaction

Battery storage closes the remaining gap, since the reason a solar household still receives bills is night-time consumption.[eldXifbNm2A] A 15 kWh LFP home storage installation prioritises household load: with solar producing 2380 W against a house load of only 262 W, the residual 2300 W flows into the battery, and when a large load such as an oven or air conditioner is switched on, the inverter supplies it from whichever source is available, drawing 2.6 kW from the inverter into the load with the solar/battery split determined by instantaneous string output.[1634]

Australian grid conditions have pushed retailers toward paying households to consume. Every energy retailer is required to offer a plan option including a free three-hour energy window per day — in one implementation 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. — during which unlimited grid energy is free, a direct consequence of excess daytime solar on a network that was built without the ability to curtail it.[3hHG_WcJtQo][aANZNwmnC_4] The window is used to charge both the home battery and the vehicle.[3hHG_WcJtQo]

Distributed generation also raises local mains voltage. In a nominally 230 V network where the premises already sit near 240 V, solar export pushes the measured voltage up to around 245 V; the resulting lower current for a given power reduces copper losses in the charging cable, measured at roughly 10 °C above ambient for a 6 mm² radial circuit.[31gkF0q9VfE]

Monitoring is a weak point in practice. Microinverter systems can lose both an inverter and the reporting gateway simultaneously, leaving the owner without the telemetry needed to diagnose the fault.[uE0x8YR7nMg]

## Solar on vehicles and vessels

Photovoltaics integrated into a vehicle body are supplemental, not a substitute for charging. Working from a domestic array's measured output and scaling to a car's panel area, a solar-bodied EV yields about 23.8 km of extra range per day in winter conditions; even taking best-case summer figures with no cloud — around 40 kWh peak daily from the reference array, where a cloudy day can more than halve that to about 14 kWh — the claimed 70 km per day does not survive.[1480] The considered judgment, from someone operating a rooftop array roughly eight times larger, is that solar-only operation is not viable and that integrated panels are worthwhile only as a supplement, cost of integration aside.[1480]

Low-power and duty-cycled applications fare better. A 15-foot solar-powered catamaran running at 80% speed operates entirely on solar while the sun is up and still puts a little back into its battery.[E6XVc2fUrKg] A tourist battery-electric train converted from 1950s carriages, with roughly a 30 kW array and battery storage, shuttles passengers about 5 km and runs net energy positive over its duty cycle.[1570] A camping setup pairing a modest array with a 400 Wh battery runs a fridge for a couple of days, the fridge itself drawing very little.[t9o1xhAHREE]

## Consumer-scale photovoltaics

Amorphous cells in consumer products operate at indoor light levels far below full sun. A credit-card-format calculator remains functional down to about 150 lux and continues to run with a thumb partly covering the cell.[1078] Solar power in this class removes the battery as a maintenance item entirely, giving an instrument that is always available and effectively never needs servicing.[IsIKyt64Tt4] The limits are real, though: for a thin scientific-calculator design with a dot-matrix LCD and a microSD card, solar power was evaluated and rejected as not possible for that particular design, and replaceable lithium coin cells were used instead.[130]

## Implausible and overstated claims

Solar power's public appeal makes it a common vehicle for weak or fraudulent proposals, and the corpus separates genuine support for the technology from credulity about specific products. The position is stated plainly — "I love solar power." — alongside a design engineer's assessment that particular schemes are impractical.[681][632][1025]

Solar roadways are the recurring case. A deployed installation producing more than 1,300 kWh per year is less than half of what a 3 kW rooftop system of about the same area produces, at an order of magnitude greater cost and an order of magnitude worse reliability.[1356][681] Horizontal road-surface panels take at least double and possibly quadruple the cost of rooftop equivalents before lifetime is considered, with a prototype panel cracking within the first couple of weeks; solar cycleways and pathways carry the same cost penalty plus guaranteed maintenance.[743] Rooftop solar, by contrast, is simple and reliable.[743]

Other claims fail on physics or on arithmetic. Panels advertised as generating electricity at night — reported credulously in mainstream outlets — do not do what the coverage says.[1467] Novel cell-manufacturing processes have been promoted as revolutionary while reducing a cost that is already a small fraction of module cost, taking it to sub-10% of module cost, which is not groundbreaking.[1025][IbYtn420QBM] A glass solar roof tile takes roughly three times the manufacturing energy of the clay tile it replaces, before the cells themselves are accounted for.[938]

Solar branding also attaches to products where it contributes nothing measurable: a kinetic "molecular heater" whose only power input is a small top-mounted cell claimed to charge in two hours of ample sunlight, a graphene heating panel marketed as working from solar or wind at any voltage, an over-unity gravity generator claimed to replace wind and solar generation, a hyperloop proposal described as powered by sustainable solar electric, and a dynamo torch for which solar is offered as an alternative that could equally wear out.[1585][1186][aTpm62ToNOM][1498][986] Atmospheric water generators are a partial exception — solar-powered dehumidifiers that genuinely extract water from humid air — but the energy accounting is unfavourable, since 14 kWh per day for around 10 litres of water is comparable to an entire household's daily consumption.[1454]
