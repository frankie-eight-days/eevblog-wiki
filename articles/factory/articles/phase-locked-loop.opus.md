# phase locked loop

A phase locked loop (PLL) is a feedback circuit that locks the output of a controllable oscillator to a reference, most often to multiply a low-frequency crystal up to the much higher frequency a system actually runs at.[496][334] It is the reason a board carrying only a 20 MHz or 25 MHz crystal can clock an FPGA at a couple of hundred megahertz, a processor at 400 MHz, or an ADC at a gigasample per second.[496][334][1038] Because the PLL sits between the crystal and everything downstream, its purity sets the jitter and timebase accuracy of the whole instrument, and a badly compensated loop degrades a design that is otherwise sound.[976][699]

## Structure

The classic arrangement is a voltage controlled oscillator, an output divider feeding a phase detector, and a reference oscillator input, with the detector output filtered and fed back to steer the VCO.[574] Block diagrams of such loops show the divider feedback, the phase detector and the VCO as separate stages, sometimes with divide-by-16 and divide-by-8 chains visible in the hardware itself.[574] Multiplier detectors were once sold as discrete parts for this purpose; the XR 2228 is a multiplier detector normally used in PLLs.[709] A related but distinct topology is the frequency locked loop, which locks an internal crystal oscillator to a reference by frequency rather than phase, as used inside rubidium standards.[235]

Modern parts integrate the synthesiser and VCO on one die with a serial control interface: the Analog Devices ADF4360-7 is an integrated synthesiser and voltage controlled oscillator that multiplies a 25 MHz crystal to produce a differential clock output.[699][985][800] Its multiplication ratio and internal coefficients are programmed by firmware at startup over a serial bus, matched to the particular loop filter components fitted on the board.[699]

## Recognising a PLL on a board

A PLL is one of the easier blocks to identify by inspection. The signature is a small chip with a ring of inductors, capacitors and a few resistors around it, an oscillator nearby, a single-ended input and a differential pair leaving toward the sampling ADC or FPGA.[1038][1355][1761] Those surrounding passives are the loop filter, and it is common in this class of design for half the pads to be left unpopulated so the loop can be tuned across product variants.[842] The proximity argument is often enough on its own — a chip sitting next to the crystal, driving the clock, with that passive cluster around it, "That kind of looks PLL-y to me."[1289][738]

Loop components are also the part of the circuit that radiates. Sniffing a near-field probe over the PLL chip itself can show nothing at all, while moving the same probe a few millimetres onto the loop components brings up a large spike at the PLL output frequency.[699]

## Clock generation in test equipment

Nearly every digital oscilloscope, function generator and spectrum analyser contains a PLL as its clock source. Documented examples include the ADF4360-7 in Siglent oscilloscopes,[985][800] an IDT dual-output RF frequency synthesiser generating the ADC clock in the Rohde & Schwarz HMO1202,[842] an LMX2582 clock generator running from a 10 MHz reference in the Keysight EDU33212A,[1431] a Texas Instruments clock driver PLL multiplying a 25 MHz crystal in the Siglent SDG5000,[497] an ADF4001 200 MHz clock generator PLL — a part intended for clock sources requiring very low noise stable reference signals — in the Siglent SSA3021X,[892] and a Hittite PLL paired with a Z-Communications VCO can to form the first local oscillator of that same analyser.[892][1109] Higher-end designs use dedicated network synchroniser clock generators specified for ultra low jitter.[1477]

It is not universal. The Keysight 1000 X-Series contains no PLL at all, running instead from a single main oscillator.[976] Elsewhere PLLs turn up wherever a stable derived frequency is needed rather than a sample clock: an ADF4117 1.2 GHz PLL sets the local oscillator frequency in both the transmitter and receiver of a Sennheiser EW100 G3 wireless microphone, selected by serial command from the microprocessor,[571] and a Mitel Networks MT8941 serves as a T1 trunk PLL chip in telephone exchange hardware.[619]

## PLLs inside programmable logic and microcontrollers

Clock management blocks inside FPGAs are PLLs by another name, and their whole purpose is to multiply and phase-shift the external clock.[496] A low-cost Cyclone III carries five internal phase locked loops whose output phase can be set in increments of 98 picoseconds, which is what allows a bank of ADCs to be interleaved by staggering their clocks.[19] A DE0 Nano class device has four, of which a simple design might use one.[636] Smaller devices may omit them entirely — a low-power Actel Igloo part with no internal PLL also removes the need for a dedicated VCCPLL supply rail,[193] whereas parts that do have one add another voltage to an already crowded rail list, with 2.5 V a typical requirement for phase locked loop supplies.[193] The presence of PLLs alongside PCI interface blocks in a gate-array part is what makes it FPGA-like.[591]

Microcontrollers and SoCs follow the same pattern. A PIC32 has a built-in PLL able to multiply an 8 MHz crystal by at least four, giving around 48 MHz,[Xg_niU86bhI] an STM32 uses an internal PLL to derive the clock required for USB operation,[900] a PXA255 XScale core running at 400 MHz is fed from a 20 MHz oscillator through internal phase locked loops,[334] and an e-book reader SoC boosts a single 24 MHz crystal to the 600 MHz needed by its processor.[295] The alternative — clocking directly from a 100 MHz crystal rather than multiplying a 20 MHz part up inside the FPGA — is unusual enough to be worth remarking on.[Xg_niU86bhI]

## Loop performance, jitter and phase noise

Loop quality shows up as phase noise on the clock and as jitter in the acquired waveform. In a spectrum analyser comparison, feeding an external rubidium frequency standard into the instrument produced the same elevated noise skirt as its internal reference, establishing that the excess spectral noise came from the PLL rather than the reference oscillator.[891] The Rigol DSA815 measured about −87 dBc against a −80 dBc specification, so it passed, but the shape of the response showed its internal PLL was not as good as the competing unit.[891]

In precision phase noise measurement the loop is itself the problem. With a phase locked loop method, any instability of the device under test that falls within the loop bandwidth is absorbed by the loop and lost.[1041] Residual phase noise measurement instead locks the timebases of the device under test and the reference oscillator together so that the instability shows up in the result, which is a tighter method than the absolute or phase locked loop approach and reveals the inherent stability of the two devices.[1041]

## The Rigol DS1054Z case

The DS1054Z jitter fault is the corpus's fullest worked example of a PLL going wrong in production. The cause was traced to the PLL that generates the main sample and FPGA clock — not the crystal, which was fine, but the programmable PLL chip and the loop components around it, which were failing to lock properly.[699] If the main clock for the ADC, sampling system and FPGA is jittering because of a poor lock, every downstream measurement is affected.[699]

The fix shipped as firmware, reprogramming the loop coefficients the microcontroller writes into the chip over its serial bus at startup, coefficients that must be chosen to suit the loop filter components actually fitted.[699] Reprogramming visibly changed the output spectrum, and the jitter on the tested unit was resolved, with a delta of about 68.334 kHz corresponding to a 7.31 µs offset.[699] The clock was still not clean afterwards: a 500 kHz span showed a 67.5 kHz delta and further peaks only about 37 dB down from the carrier, none of which should be present, a result fairly described as "pretty piss-poor".[699] Fixing it properly may require changing the loop component values in hardware, which is unattractive because it would mean recalling units already sold and has to be weighed against the measured performance of the instrument.[699]

The engineering lesson is about test coverage rather than topology. Many units in the field showed no problem at all and locked correctly, so the defect survived a sample that was not statistically significant.[699] The design engineer should have measured the output of the ADC clock directly.[699] The same failure is remembered as a general caution when examining any new scope's clock section, since a PLL is easy to get wrong and there is no way to check the loop values by eye.[976][1038]

## Lock time and diagnosis

Because the loop takes time to settle, PLL lock behaviour is a natural subject for time-correlated measurement. Commanding a VCO to move from 2.2 GHz to 2.4 GHz and watching the spectrum step through 2.309, 2.317 and 2.348 GHz shows the loop still hunting before it settles, and correlating that against the PLL control voltage in the time domain gives the settling time directly.[199] Markers placed at the command edge and at the point the loop pulls in allow the interval to be read off.[199]

For fault-finding, the loop can sometimes be taken out of the equation entirely: in a Fluke calibrator repair the phase lock circuit was disabled, which removes a large block of circuitry from consideration and narrows the search to the amplitude control stage.[XRyDyOANQhc] The PLL's own supply rail is worth checking as a routine step, since a healthy phase locked loop supply rules out one class of fault.[1322] Some instruments expose loop control to the user, allowing the ADC clock to be overclocked deliberately.[lXvXBdTP7Tg]

## Other appearances

PLL blocks appear inside serialiser and re-timer silicon, alongside data registers, PLL control and signal detection stages configured over I²C.[vq89dWPCxUY] In serial data analysis the loop is a software construct: oscilloscope clock recovery offers PCI Express, Fibre Channel and FlexRay presets alongside first- and second-order PLL models, and choosing the wrong nominal data rate recovers no clock at all — a first-order PLL set to 2.5 Gbit/s yields nothing on a link that locks cleanly at 5 Gbit/s.[340]

Frequency standards use the same principle at the top end of the accuracy scale. A cesium beam tube disciplines a temperature-controlled crystal oscillator through a phase locked loop system, combining the tube's long-term stability with the crystal's short-term stability.[423] Older discrete implementations survive in unlikely places: an IBM L40SX laptop mounted a 4066 phase locked loop and a 7404 on a ceramic hybrid riser board, an unusual amount of effort for clock generation in a portable.[639]

The 555 timer traces its origin to this circuit family. Its designer developed it from a timer circuit he had built as the timing source for phase locked loops he was working on at the time.[1746] Independently, a phase locked loop circuit was central to an early modem design, needed because reading data off cassette tape required a variable reference rather than a fixed one.[FLB4Xvt9Szc]
