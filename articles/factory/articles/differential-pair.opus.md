# differential pair

A differential pair is two PCB traces carrying complementary signals, routed together as a matched unit so that the receiver reads the difference between them rather than either one against ground. It is the default signalling scheme for essentially every high-speed interface on a modern board — DDR memory, PCI Express, USB, LVDS display links, gigabit Ethernet, and the links between an oscilloscope's analog front end and its ADC.[587][462][340][631][1216][1530] Because the pair is only as good as the match between its two halves, the routing rules that govern it — equal length, controlled impedance, an uninterrupted reference plane — dominate the layout of any board that uses them, and their visible signature is the serpentine wiggle found across dense multilayer boards.[1247][860][720]

## Length matching and skew

The defining constraint is timing. The two traces of a pair must arrive together, and where several pairs carry one bus, the pairs must arrive together as well. This produces two distinct kinds of serpentine trace: wiggles added *within* a pair to equalise its two halves, and wiggles added *between* pairs, or between single-ended traces such as D0 to D7 and A0 to A7 on a memory bus.[1247] On a DDR bus the pair is a signal such as DQS3 positive and negative, and the matching target is on the order of a millimetre or two of length difference across the group.[1247][587]

Geometry forces the work. Where a bundle of pairs fans out around a curve, the pair on the inside of the bend is naturally shorter than the pair on the outside, so the inner pairs need progressively more wiggles to catch up; the traces stop at staggered points until the inside pair is exactly the same length as the outside pair and the timing between all the data is identical.[781] The same trick appears at every scale, from a bundle of LVDS lines on a TV processor board down to a single wiggle beside one trace on a compute module carrier.[781][E1IqcGcZKHE]

The underlying discipline is a timing budget, and it is the reason autorouting is inappropriate on such buses: "don't auto route. Manually route."[1247] Autorouting remains defensible on a dense but low-speed digital board where there are no high-speed design rules, no differential pairs, and signal integrity between chips is not critical.[975] Length matching is occasionally even done as a rework — added wiggles cut into an existing board, sharp corners and all, to bring a pair back into match.[1079] The Saturn PCB design toolkit is a free calculator covering differential pairs alongside via resistance, crosstalk, conductor impedance and pad stacks.[1216]

## Impedance and reference planes

A differential pair is normally a controlled-impedance structure, and this is visible in the copper. The traces are noticeably fatter than ordinary signal routing, which is a reliable giveaway when reverse-engineering an unfamiliar board — wide traces running as a pair, with a ground plane beneath them, are a controlled-impedance pair.[564][565] Pairs are also held at a fixed minimum spacing from one another, with a ground plane layer directly underneath.[462]

The reference planes are a layer-count driver. On a ten-layer board, the high-speed differential gigabit Ethernet routing on an inner signal layer required a further ground layer beneath it, and the need for multiple ground layers is what pushed the stack-up to ten in the first place.[1216] Grounds are sometimes also run in the plane of the signal, flanking each side of a pair going into an ADC.[1639] That flanking is ambiguous evidence, however: two closely spaced traces separated by ground may equally be two single-ended channels rather than one pair, and the presence of the separating ground is itself a reason to doubt the differential reading.[1639]

Controlled impedance is not always required. On a short flex prototype carrying USB, the pair is marked as a differential pair in the schematic and routed as one, but controlled impedance and length matching can be skipped because the link is not high-speed in any demanding sense.[1262]

## Interfaces built on differential pairs

USB 3.0 adds two extra high-speed pairs, one transmit and one receive, on the outside of the connector, carrying 5 Gb/s; these sit alongside the backward-compatible USB 2.0 single pair and its 5 V power.[340] USB-C carries five differential pairs, bidirectional, each capable of 400 Mb/s, plus power sufficient to charge a phone.[1753] PCI Express routing fans out from the chipset to the slots as pairs at minimum spacing.[462] Ethernet pairs run matched-length from the MagJack — which integrates the isolation transformers and termination resistors that were once separate parts — straight into the host microcontroller.[145]

In flat-panel displays the LVDS link between the processor board and the T-con board is a bundle of pairs, on the order of eight or sixteen, carried over a ribbon cable; the T-con takes the incoming LVDS pairs, performs timing and control, and emits further pairs to drive the panel itself.[781][631] The same arrangement carries video to the screen in bench instruments and to LCD driver boards in office equipment.[842][1100] In display-controller datasheets the word *lane* denotes exactly this: a lane is physically a differential pair, a high-speed data path in copper.[1154]

Because the pairs are the interconnect, their absence is diagnostic. Where two large chips on a board show no differential routing and no parallel bus between them on any visible layer, the high-speed interface must be on inner layers or must not exist as assumed.[875] Conversely, on a display fault where menus render but image data does not, a bundle of pairs between processor and T-con is a prime suspect precisely because a whole-bus failure would produce widespread corruption rather than a selective one.[781]

## Oscilloscope signal paths

Inside a digital oscilloscope the analog front end ends in a differential driver: the programmable gain amplifier converts the single-ended, ground-referenced input into a differential output and drives it down a pair into the ADC.[1530] This is a deliberate choice of part — a 1 GHz differential driver at the end of the variable gain amp, with a test point on the pair so the path can be characterised in production.[976] The pair layout is often the clearest map of the instrument's architecture: following the controlled-impedance serpentine pairs from an acquisition ASIC reveals which channels it actually serves, and which of two similar ASICs is master and which is slave.[148][144]

Pair counts read directly as sample-rate architecture. A scope with a single ADC serving four channels shows four length-matched pairs converging on that one converter, and the missing second ADC on a cheaper variant explains a halving of sample rate from four to two gigasamples per second.[1563][1510] Where two pairs emerge from one front-end block, the design is switching between converters to double the sample rate, with LVDS pairs on the output.[1503][1477] A minimalist 500 MHz front end may amount to little more than a single pair going straight into the ADC.[1124]

The scheme also structures faults. In one Tektronix hybrid amplifier the chip splits the signal into high-frequency and low-frequency components and drives two separate differential pairs to the ADC, which localises a channel failure: probing showed the same signal present on both pairs of a good channel, isolating the defect on the faulty channel to one of the two paths.[565] Identifying the differential output on such a hybrid is straightforward — two pins, a pair of coupling capacitors, and two conspicuously fat traces heading for the ADC.[564][565] Elsewhere a pair emerges with 200 Ω series resistors, indicating a clock rather than data.[824]

## The circuit-level sense of the term

The same phrase denotes the analog topology: two matched transistors sharing a common emitter resistor. Small transistor arrays such as the MC3346 package a differential pair together with a couple of discrete transistors on one die, chosen over discrete parts for thermal matching among other reasons.[208] In precision instruments a matched pair device is given extraordinary treatment — the SSM2212 in a 7.5-digit multimeter sits on a completely isolated island of board, with differential traces in and vias carrying the signal away, so that no leakage current can flow into or out of it.[731]

This dual meaning is a trap when reverse-engineering. A cluster of traces that resembles a differential pair with a common tail resistor may turn out, on tracing the copper further, to be nothing of the kind.[714] Two traces routed side by side and dragged together in the layout tool are not necessarily a pair either — routing them as a unit can simply be convenience, since they head to the same part of the board and the software maintains the spacing.[245] And a device drawn in a datasheet as a pair, such as the 4052 dual four-way multiplexer, is drawn that way because it is convenient for switching signals in pairs; there is nothing differential inside the chip.[1611]
