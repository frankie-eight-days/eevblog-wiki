# battery

A battery is an electrochemical energy store: it holds energy as chemistry and releases it as current flow when a circuit is completed, but it stores energy rather than power — "You can't store power."[1009] Unlike a mains supply or bench power supply, a battery cannot hold a constant terminal voltage while delivering current, because its internal (ionic) resistance causes the terminal voltage to sag under load.[779] This single property — load-dependent terminal voltage — governs how batteries are specified, measured, and designed around.

## Capacity and energy ratings

Battery capacity is commonly quoted in milliamp-hours or amp-hours, but the strictly correct figure for stored energy is watt-hours, because a battery's voltage is not constant over discharge — it tapers off rather than staying flat and then dying abruptly.[1009] A 25 watt-hour battery can in principle deliver 25 watts for one hour or 1 watt for 25 hours.[1009] Constant-current discharge testing therefore accumulates amp-hours, not watt-hours, because the load current is fixed while the voltage falls.[1009] Representative nameplate ratings encountered in practice include a single-cell lithium polymer at 3.7 V, 4325 mAh (16 Wh) in a tablet,[321] a 1420 mAh, 5.25 Wh single cell in an e-reader,[370] a 15.4 V, 5180 mAh (80 Wh) laptop pack,[p287qKMXqxQ] and a 7.3 V, 20,000 mAh (74 Wh) pack in a portable oscilloscope.[1761]

## Internal resistance and terminal voltage

The electrochemistry inside a cell presents an internal ionic resistance that is highly dynamic, varying with load, state of charge, temperature, and discharge history.[789][779] Terminal voltage under load is the only voltage that matters to a powered device; a bench power supply, having essentially zero internal resistance, behaves as an ideal battery and can deliver whatever pulse current a load demands, which is why products that refuse to run on depleted cells still work on a power supply.[779] Battery equivalent series resistance is also not constant — it changes with load and internal heating, particularly at higher powers — so characterising a cell for emulation requires discharging it at several different loads to build up the ESR curves.[Y2rcx4vKxlc]

## Open-circuit voltage is misleading

A discharged cell recovers to a higher terminal voltage once the load is removed, because the internal drop disappears when current stops flowing.[779] Cells discharged all the way to zero and held there have been observed to recover to 1.2 V open circuit, and a cell discharged to 1 V at half an amp recovered to 1.38 V.[789] This recovered voltage implies almost nothing about remaining energy — fully depleted cells may retain perhaps 1% of their energy, enough to run a low-power device dimly for a while, but the open-circuit reading does not reveal this either way.[789] The sound engineering rule is to never ever measure the open circuit voltage when assessing state of charge or cutoff behaviour; manufacturers publish loaded discharge curves for exactly this reason, and measuring a battery removed from the product after use produces the recovered, unrepresentative value.[779][789]

## Product cutoff voltages

Well-designed battery products extract nearly all usable energy from a cell. Typical cutoff thresholds measured under load sit around 1.1 V per cell, with many devices operating lower: an Xbox controller and a Game Boy function down to 1 V per cell, a vacuum-fluorescent calculator works at 0.8 V per cell, a DAT Walkman at 1.1 V per cell, and a four-AA differential probe at about 0.95 V per cell.[751][779] Below roughly 0.8 V per cell the remaining capacity "drops off a cliff" and essentially nothing is left.[1331] Because waiting days or months for a real battery to reach cutoff is impractical, the standard method for measuring a product's cutoff voltage is to power it from a bench supply and ramp the voltage down; the supply's zero source resistance means the measurement reflects the product's true terminal-voltage threshold.[779]

## Measuring batteries

Meaningful battery measurement requires a load. A multimeter's low-impedance mode, presenting a load of a couple of kilohms, is useful for this: a cell reading 1.21 V and falling quickly under that modest load is effectively dead, while the small droop seen at the top of a fully charged cell's range settles and stops.[1667] Note that an unloaded cell can read acceptably on a 10 MΩ digital meter input while still failing in circuit due to high ESR.[9s9LXOBknck] Dedicated battery-emulator instruments combine a power supply and electronic load to record a real cell's discharge behaviour and then reproduce it, allowing a product to be tested against a simulated battery at chosen states of charge rather than a fixed voltage.[Y2rcx4vKxlc][1331]

## Charging and management

Lithium packs increasingly embed authentication: one e-reader's battery carries an I²C bus (SCL/SDA) with an ID chip, and the device refuses to power up from a source lacking that chip, so fitting a larger aftermarket cell requires reverse-engineering the interface.[370] An over-discharged lithium cell that has fallen to around 2.5 V can enter undervoltage lockout and refuse normal charging; kickstarting it with a bench supply in constant-current mode at a few hundred milliamps can raise it out of lockout so the normal charger takes over.[KTr-44n0bbU] Fast NiMH chargers may interleave charge and bypass windows — 500 ms charging, 500 ms bypassed, alternating between cells — with charge and bypass never active simultaneously since that would short the battery.[812] Where one charger IC must serve two packs that operate in series, the packs cannot be paralleled for charging, so the system charges them one at a time under microcontroller control.[1460]

## Failure modes and degradation

Lithium polymer cells swell visibly when degraded — a "puffy" pack is a recognised end-of-life sign.[1330][o4S-TkTuHZs] Capacity also fades with age; decade-old cells may still function but deliver only a couple of hours of runtime where they once delivered many.[1613] Cells in series strings discharge unevenly, with one example pair reading 1.477 V and 1.29 V from the same device.[9s9LXOBknck] Alkaline leakage can creep under the outer wrap and corrode the label and cell internals, and on some cells the positive terminal is directly welded to the can.[1350] A direct short across battery terminals is destructive even in small consumer devices — shorted toy battery wiring melted the conductors and the adjacent switch body.[1219] Batteries dislike heat, a constraint when a product's enclosure doubles as a heatsink.[744] Battery management system software defects can themselves be a safety hazard serious enough to trigger a vehicle recall.[xb-uEIz_XKo] Abuse tolerance varies by design: some lithium automotive replacement packs survive a nail penetration test by catching fire without exploding.[UXnVOoBtlcU]

## Backup and long-life cells

Equipment that must retain calibration or timekeeping uses long-lived backup cells, often integrated into battery-backed SRAM modules. A 1987-vintage Dallas SRAM module has been found still holding its checksum decades later, and losing that battery corrupts the stored checksum at every power-up; good practice is to read out and archive the contents before the cell dies.[615] Instrument designs that store factory calibration separately in EEPROM allow recovery after backup-battery failure.[615] Where a device must sit dormant for years, low self-discharge cells rated at a maximum of 5% per month are still a marginal choice compared with primary cells.[1136]

## Large-scale storage

Home storage batteries apply the same principles at kilowatt-hour scale. A 15 kWh lithium iron phosphate system is typically configured with a 20% discharge floor — using only 80% of nameplate capacity — to extend service life, though the full range remains available under warranty.[gAu8CvMjDrU] High-power household loads drain such a system quickly: a 3 kW oven plus air conditioning can pull a full charge down to the floor within an evening.[gAu8CvMjDrU] In hybrid-inverter installations, a current clamp on the grid connection detects export and diverts excess solar production into the battery, and storage discharges to cover load whenever solar output falls short.[1634] Electric vehicles recover kinetic energy the same way in reverse, with regenerative braking returning energy to the traction battery instead of dissipating it as brake heat.[7nUgmEuyKIU] A full hybrid-battery recall remedy can consist of nothing more than updated battery-management software.[xb-uEIz_XKo]
