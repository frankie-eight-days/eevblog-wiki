# input capacitance

Input capacitance is the shunt capacitance presented at the input terminal of a device — an oscilloscope probe tip, a scope's front end, a logic gate pin, a MOSFET gate, or the DC bus of a switching converter. It is a parasitic in most contexts and a design parameter in a few, and because capacitive reactance falls as 1 / (2 · π · f · C), a few picofarads that are irrelevant at kilohertz become a dominant load at hundreds of megahertz.[1718] Wherever a signal has to be measured at speed, driven fast, or supplied with surge current, input capacitance is usually the limiting term.[1715][sr1DOHnJi8I][1707]

## Oscilloscope probes and scope inputs

The capacitance a probe presents is specified either on the probe body or in its datasheet, and it is often quoted as total system capacitance — the value seen at the tip once the probe is plugged into a scope, so probe and scope input combine into one number.[1367] That figure is what the circuit under test actually sees, and it loads the circuit; the headline 10 MΩ input impedance is a DC figure only, and at frequency the relevant quantity is capacitive reactance.[1367]

Typical passive-probe values cluster in the low tens of picofarads. Rigol's RP2200 is a switchable ×1/×10 150 MHz probe with a nominal ×10 input capacitance of 17 pF; the 350 MHz RP3300 is marginally lower at 16 pF, and in ×10 mode the two are essentially the same.[704][707][441] A 500 MHz passive probe comes in at 11 pF.[1367] The Tektronix TPP1000, a 1 GHz passive probe at roughly 800 dollars each, reaches 3.9 pF while keeping the same 10 MΩ input impedance and 300 V rating.[1367] One 1 GHz-rated passive probe carries 13 pF, which undercuts the point of the bandwidth rating once high-frequency loading is accounted for.[1718]

Scope front ends vary similarly: 16 pF on a 1 MΩ-only GW Instek GDS-2000A input,[474] a nominal 15 pF on a Siglent,[1445] and 24 pF on a Keysight Megazoom V 1 GHz instrument, notably higher than the 14 pF of the previous generation and enough to raise questions about probe compensation range.[1638]

The attenuation setting dominates everything else. In ×1 mode a probe is close to a length of coax straight through to the scope and presents about 100 pF; switching to ×10 drops it to roughly 16 pF, equivalent to the tip capacitance.[453] That difference is why ×10 is the default working mode and why many probes ship as ×10 only: bandwidth on one Rigol probe is DC to 8 MHz at ×1 against DC to 350 MHz at ×10, and the input capacitance is the reason.[441] The compensation trimmer at one end of the probe exists to match the probe network to the scope's input capacitance so the combination gives a flat total system response.[1445]

Capacitance also accumulates inside the instrument. A relay-switched attenuator chain contributes the capacitance of every tap and every switch in the divider, on top of the deliberate compensation caps.[1260]

## Active and low-capacitance probes

Above roughly 500 MHz, where practical passive probes stop, an active FET probe is the answer, and the reason is input capacitance — as the design tradeoff is usually framed, "it's all about the input capacitance."[1715] Even a 1 GHz passive probe at a few picofarads loads a fast node too heavily.[1709] The Euler Precision eSAP-30, a 3 GHz active probe, specifies 1 MΩ input impedance with under 3 pF, and measurement confirms the effective capacitance is well under the claimed 3 pF; at 1 GHz its input impedance is around 159 Ω, consistent with slightly over 1 pF.[1715] Agilent's N2796A active probe reaches 1 pF, as does a 2 GHz Agilent active probe.[1081][1733] A 100-dollar 2 GHz power rail probe does better still at roughly half a picofarad, with a 50 kΩ DC load that does not hold at frequency precisely because of that capacitance.[1733] Layout inside such a probe matters for the same reason: putting the input amplifier as close to the tip as possible, rather than at the end of a long microstrip, is what keeps input capacitance down.[1733]

Very low probe capacitance also removes an error source in high-voltage work — a 1 pF probe input contributes nothing meaningful to the charging of a capacitor bank being ramped up.[1016]

Input capacitance is not always benign in a purely resistive sense. Together with the inductance of a probe's coax cable it forms an LC tank at the scope input, which broadband static discharge — from something as ordinary as standing up out of a chair — can ring.[14] On the digital side, input capacitance sits alongside inductance, skew, crosstalk, and metastability among the signal-integrity problems that make logic-analyzer inputs hard to get right.[44]

## Logic gate inputs

Digital logic datasheets specify input capacitance as C<sub>I</sub>, in picofarads, over the full operating voltage range; manufacturers frequently give only a maximum, with no typical figure and no characteristic curves.[sr1DOHnJi8I] A working rule of thumb puts the typical value around 3 pF per input.[sr1DOHnJi8I] Because ordinary system design has one output driving several gates, the load capacitance C<sub>L</sub> at the driver is the sum of those inputs, and that total sets the achievable slew rate and the input transition rise and fall times downstream.[sr1DOHnJi8I] This was the historical basis of the maximum fan-out limit in 74-series designs, where one chip might drive twenty or thirty others and pin capacitance genuinely constrained the architecture.[sr1DOHnJi8I]

C<sub>I</sub> is only the static part. Datasheets also give C<sub>PD</sub>, the dynamic power dissipation capacitance per gate — quoted in picofarads despite describing power dissipation.[sr1DOHnJi8I]

The gate input capacitance is also loaded by the interconnect: a trace driving a CMOS or TTL input has its own capacitance to ground in parallel with the gate's, and during a fast transition that combination behaves briefly as a low impedance, drawing a current spike through the signal trace and the ground plane.[1085] In DDR memory layout the problem becomes a timing one — input capacitance variation across devices, documented in the datasheet, adds to the propagation-delay budget alongside crosstalk between signals and the termination voltage reference.[1247]

## MOSFET gates

For MOSFETs, input capacitance is the datasheet parameter C<sub>ISS</sub>. It is loosely called gate capacitance but is not strictly the gate-to-source capacitance: it is measured with drain and source shorted, so it includes gate-to-drain, and it represents the total capacitance the gate driver actually sees.[1736] Its magnitude tracks die area, so the higher the current rating, the larger the input capacitance — a 2N7002-class small-signal part sits around 20 to 22 pF, while a power device runs to 630 pF.[1736] C<sub>ISS</sub> is quoted at a specified V<sub>DS</sub>, for instance 25 V.[1387]

Driving the gate is where large input capacitance causes trouble.[1387] When selecting a replacement, the input capacitance of the candidate is compared against the original: an ON Semi part at a nominal 2200 pF against a MagnaChip original at 2400 pF is a lower figure, and lower is the safe direction — it will not hurt, though reverse transfer capacitance still has to be checked separately.[1387] In parametric search, input capacitance appears next to gate charge, output capacitance, and reverse transfer capacitance as one of the dynamic characteristics.[1180][1461] These matter mainly in switching converters; for logic-level or power switching in a repair context they are usually not the deciding parameter, whereas a low gate threshold and low input capacitance are what allow direct drive from 5 V or 3.3 V logic.[1461][1736]

Low input capacitance can also be the headline feature of an analog input device, as it was for the JFET-type parts used in a bootstrapped microphone preamplifier input stage, where it displaced the need for an expensive very-high-value bias resistor.[629]

## Bulk input capacitance in power circuits

In power conversion the term refers to real bulk capacitance on the input rail, and here more is generally wanted. In a non-isolated LED driver, an input capacitance of only 330 nF let the rectified input rail sag almost to ground between half-cycles and produced visible flicker; adding 33 µF of input capacitance eliminated it outright and left the output ripple in good shape.[1253] A commercial flicker-free driver of otherwise identical topology carries 12 µF at 450 V — the voltage rating following from the full-wave bridge sitting directly across the filter cap — which is enough input capacitance to do the job.[dLlhoUHlnjE] Controller datasheets may assume far less; one specifies input capacitance of only 47 nF while giving typical output capacitance figures and a worked example showing that 470 µF of output capacitance still leaves 30 percent ripple current.[1253]

Excessive input capacitance is a fault mode in its own right. A mains inverter with too large an input capacitance drew enough inrush charging current at switch-on to upset the host controller in a lithium power station, which repeatedly attempted to start, shut down, and retry — a hiccup loop visible as flashing.[1707] A load with very little input capacitance, and therefore very little surge current at turn-on, gave the same controller no trouble.[1707]

Input capacitance also participates in stability. In a precision 1 A current source built around an LTC6655 reference, the combination of output capacitance, load current, and input capacitance is enough to make the loop oscillate under some conditions.[577]
