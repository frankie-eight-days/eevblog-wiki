# capacitor

A capacitor is two conductive plates separated by a dielectric — an insulator, whether air, mica, ceramic, plastic film or the epoxy resin of a circuit board — with no direct electrical connection between them.[1406][486][1117] It stores energy in the electric field across that dielectric, the complement of the inductor, which stores energy in a magnetic field in a coil and its core.[1409][1406] Because it stores rather than dissipates, it introduces time into a circuit: charge takes time to build and time to leave, and that single property underpins timing, filtering, energy buffering, coupling and phase shift across essentially all of electronics.[1406][1470]

The governing relation is Q equals C times V, charge in coulombs equalling capacitance times voltage, from which the current relation I equals C dV/dt follows directly.[1406][486] Increasing the dielectric constant of the material between the plates increases the capacitance; air has a dielectric constant of one and most other materials are higher.[1406] Capacitance is not a property reserved for components: any two conductors in any space form a capacitor, including the contacts of an open switch, adjacent wires, a PCB trace and the ground plane beneath it, and the input of every logic gate.[486][1085][1728]

## Charge, discharge, and the short-circuit rule

The foundational rule for transient analysis is that a fully discharged capacitor behaves as a short circuit at the instant voltage is applied. If there is no charge on the plates there can be no volts across them regardless of the capacitance value, so current at time zero is at maximum and then tapers off as the voltage rises, a mirror image of the voltage curve.[1406][486] Inductors are the exact inverse: at time zero they are an open circuit.[1406] Once charged, a capacitor holds its voltage after the source is removed, and a capacitor cannot change its voltage instantaneously.[1406][483]

This rule has direct practical consequences. Plugging a mains cable into a supply with large DC capacitors after the rectifier produces a spark because the capacitors are momentarily a short and the only limiting impedance is the connectors, wiring and the equivalent resistance of the bridge rectifier.[1406] It is also why capacitors interfere with continuity testing: applying the meter's voltage to a capacitor in circuit makes it act as a short and produce a false beep, which is why fast-latching continuity functions are a liability when probing for shorts on a populated board.[1671] Small capacitors charge too quickly to register at all on a slow, non-latching continuity function.[1671] The same effect explains the low-impedance measurement mode found on many multimeters, which shorts out capacitively coupled ghost voltages so that only real voltages are read.[CYm-4gbl1Zc]

## Does current flow through a capacitor

No electrons cross the dielectric. Charge accumulates on the plates, equal and opposite, setting up an electric field between them; electrons flow into one plate and out of the other, and in a real part a negligible leakage current sneaks through the insulator.[486] Yet every basic formula treats current as flowing through the capacitor in a series circuit, and it can be measured doing so.[486] The resolution is Maxwell's displacement current: as the electric field between the plates varies, it creates a changing magnetic field, and that term — required to complete the theory of electromagnetism and electromagnetic wave propagation — is the equivalent current through the dielectric.[486] In practical electronics, current flows through a capacitor, with the word current understood to mean electric current and displacement current together; the question of whether it really does is a physics question rather than an engineering one.[486][ItoRt1buLkM]

The inverse relationship with inductors falls out of the same picture: the greater the rate of change of the electric field, the more current a capacitor passes, while the greater the rate of change of the magnetic field, the less current an inductor passes.[486] Put simply, capacitors block DC and pass AC or changing currents.[486]

## AC behaviour

Introducing a capacitor or inductor into a circuit causes phase shifts between current and voltage.[1470] In a capacitor the current leads the voltage; in an inductor the voltage leads the current, conventionally remembered with the CIVIL mnemonic, in which C is the capacitor, L the inductor, V the voltage and I the current.[1660][1469] The physical reason is the short-circuit rule: at switch-on the current goes immediately to maximum while the voltage is still zero and has to build up on the plates.[1660]

Every capacitor presents a reactance measured in ohms, XC, equal to 1/ωC at an angle of minus 90 degrees — anti-phase with inductive reactance.[1470][1728] In complex form these reactances combine with resistance into impedance, and a circuit's current cannot be found without first converting each capacitor and inductor into its reactance.[1728][1661] Ideal capacitors and inductors dissipate no real power at all; the energy shuttled to and from them is reactive power, designated Q and measured in volt-amps reactive.[1730] The sinusoid is the only waveform not distorted in shape by passing through capacitors and inductors, which is why a filter built from them returns a sine wave for a sine wave in.[1417]

Deliberate use of reactive current is the basis of power factor correction: adding parallel capacitance compensates a large inductive load such as a factory full of motors, pulling the power factor toward unity, and power stations and factories install banks of capacitors for exactly this.[1730] The consumer energy-saver products sold on this principle are simply a mains-rated capacitor, in one case a 3 microfarad 450 V part, wired straight across active and neutral; domestic customers are billed for real power, so the device saves nothing and costs a fraction of a watt.[1191][847]

## The real component: ESR, ESL and resonance

A real capacitor is not just a capacitance. It has an equivalent series resistance, the ESR, and also an equivalent series inductance, the ESL, arising from lead and construction inductance — making it an RLC circuit rather than an ideal element.[859] Even a surface-mount part has resistance in its end caps and terminations, contributing I squared R losses.[1401] The ESL may be only picohenries, but at some frequency it dominates: capacitive reactance falls with frequency until it meets the rising inductive reactance at the self-resonant frequency, above which the part no longer behaves as a capacitor.[859] This is why bypassing uses several values and types rather than one large capacitor.[859][1323]

## Bypass and decoupling

A bypass capacitor stores charge locally so that when a chip switches and demands a sudden gulp of current, that current comes from the adjacent capacitor rather than from the far side of the PCB through long series inductance.[859] The further the capacitor sits from the chip, the greater the loop inductance and the worse the behaviour at high switching frequencies, so the goal is a low-impedance, low-inductance supply element.[859] Current is drawn on every transition even with no resistive load at all, because traces and gate inputs present capacitance — sometimes only a few picofarads — and at fast edges that capacitance is effectively a short circuit.[1085] A common starting rule of thumb, as recommended by FPGA vendors, is 100 nF and 10 nF per device power pin, with ESR and self-resonance considered where the design warrants it.[1323] The power and ground planes of a multilayer board form a capacitor in their own right, the fiberglass-epoxy laminate acting as the dielectric.[1117]

## Types, ratings and construction

Electrolytic capacitors use aluminium foil with a liquid electrolyte in a metal can and are poor performers in almost every respect, but they provide bulk capacitance cheaply.[33] Their electrolyte can leak, and the scored vent in the top relieves pressure build-up.[1636] Solid types can be identified by the absence of the vents that wet electrolytics carry.[1606] Class Y capacitors are used between either mains line and protective earth; mains-rated film types are self-healing, so a small internal overload sparks, extinguishes itself and destroys a minuscule amount of capacitance rather than shorting or exploding.[33]

Ceramics are divided into classes with very different behaviour. Class II dielectrics show pronounced voltage dependency: applying a larger excitation to a Y5V part visibly changes its charging characteristic, making such parts unsuitable for timing applications where an RC charging to a threshold sets the interval.[626] Capacitors in general are not high-tolerance devices.[626] Volumetric efficiency has advanced enormously — a 0.1 microfarad part once required a 1210 case and is now available in 0201 or 01005 sizes, with 1 microfarad in 0105 under development.[9V99J22aiLE] A further trick is stacking two chip capacitors at the factory to produce a through-hole part, leveraging the volume manufacturing of the standard parts to reach higher values where board area is not the constraint.[1525]

Two further non-idealities matter in precision work. Dielectric absorption gives a capacitor a memory of its previous charge, so that a shorted capacitor may not stay at zero volts, the effect depending on the dielectric.[490] And capacitors, like inductors, are microphonic: vibration couples into them, and an audible high-pitched squeal from a power supply can originate in its capacitors or inductors.[1607]

## Failure and diagnosis

Capacitors are among the components that cannot be screened by electrical test at manufacture: a fine crack invisible even under magnification will pass every test and fail later in the field, giving ceramics a long-term failure rate rather than a detectable defect.[684] Electrolytics dry out with age, ESR rises and ripple exceeds the design limit, which can present as a system-level fault such as a machine rebooting under peak current load.[eSx8MeY6V48] Bulging is the usual visual giveaway and bulged or leaking caps are worth looking for as soon as a lid comes off, but a capacitor can be electrically dead without any bulge.[523][365][-bPGrSSaXhE]

ESR measurement is the most direct test. A 3300 microfarad part that should read around 0.06 to 0.1 ohms reading 0.9 ohms is finished, while another that has visibly leaked can still measure 0.07 ohms and be electrically sound.[763] Current draw is another discriminator: a failed capacitor drawing 240 milliamps where the specification allows an order of magnitude less is unambiguous.[365] Capacitance loss can be dramatic and subtle at once — a 22 nanofarad film capacitor that fell to 500 picofarads still produced a nominal output voltage while corrupting the timing that depended on it, and a 220 nanofarad mains dropper losing capacitance raised its series impedance until there was insufficient current to hold a Zener in regulation and the 24 V relay it fed would no longer pull in.[379][1482] Leakage matters as much as value: a tantalum measuring correctly on capacitance but drawing 150 microamps of leakage at 6 volts, in series with a 1 megohm resistor, pulled a node to mid-rail.[1245]

Recapping an entire unit rather than hunting the single bad part is defensible practice when intermittent breakdown is suspected, since a voltage-dependent breakdown in one capacitor may not reproduce on the bench.[1714] Where multiple capacitors are replaced at once, counting the parts removed and comparing against the parts fitted catches the omissions that produce a dead channel afterwards.[1714] Assembly also matters: soldering a capacitor directly across large-mass pins surrounded by copper forces a great deal of heat into the part, and the resulting damage can end with the capacitor on fire when a supply capable of a kilowatt delivers 20 amps into it.[1035] Mechanically, tall capacitors are secured with adhesive so they do not vibrate loose in equipment destined for rough service, and their height must be accounted for against exposed vias on any board mounted flush above them.[WFhk1aRqLD8][1262]

Before any of this, large capacitors must be discharged. A television supply designed without a bleed resistor can sit at 150 V DC long after being unplugged, decaying only through the 4 megohm load of a probe; a meter's low-impedance function will drain a modest capacitor safely.[1388][663] The energies involved elsewhere are considerably higher — a capacitor-discharge supply for ocean bottom profiling charges a large capacitor to 4.2 or 4.3 kilovolts and dumps it through SCRs in under a millisecond, and an electric fence energiser stores its joules in 230 microfarad 900 volt capacitors.[84][1292] An airbag controller's reservoir capacitor is enclosed in its own protective cage with compliance in the leads, so that it survives the crash long enough to fire the charges and commit the crash data to EEPROM.[517]

## Measurement

An LCR meter distinguishes capacitance from inductance by the sign of the phase angle: negative series reactance is capacitive, positive is inductive.[1473] A good capacitor reads close to minus 90 degrees with a dissipation factor around 0.001.[757][847] Capacitance functions on general-purpose multimeters are useful for troubleshooting supplies — a 100 microfarad part reading 95 microfarads is fine — but ranges are limited, and pushing beyond a meter's stated maximum yields a slow and badly wrong reading rather than an overrange indication.[1636][1692][1335]

In-circuit measurement works better than expected, but only within limits. Surrounding components — regulators, ESD protection diodes inside ICs, parallel resistances — mean a capacitor is rarely measured alone, and it is these that confuse the meter rather than the capacitor itself.[1473][1474] The key variable is the meter's excitation level: with a 10 microfarad capacitor at 100 Hz and the range resistor the meter selects, the signal is a few hundred millivolts peak-to-peak, too small to turn on any active device in circuit, and the measurement is good.[1474] Large values at low frequency therefore measure well in circuit even with 100 ohms in parallel, whereas a 100 nanofarad part in the same circuit cannot be measured at all because the signal clips on the surrounding silicon.[1474] Raising the frequency makes matters worse rather than better for large values, since the signal level collapses: at 100 Hz a 220 microfarad capacitor reads correctly, at 1 kHz the signal is buried in common-mode noise, and at 10 kHz it is hopeless.[1474] Anything with tens of ohms around it is unmeasurable, and multiple capacitors charging in parallel will produce wandering readings.[1474][1649] Where a lead can be freed — by lifting it, or by pulling a socketed IC so one terminal goes nowhere — the ambiguity disappears.[777] Two capacitors in parallel read as their sum, which is worth buzzing out before drawing conclusions from a doubled value.[1527][763]

## Capacitors as circuit elements

Feeding a constant current into a capacitor produces a linear voltage ramp, the basis of the op-amp integrator and of the dual-slope integrating technique used in precision multimeters.[600][485] A diode and a capacitor make a peak detector, but the capacitor value is a genuine trade-off: too small and droop degrades the measurement, too large and the op amp's slew rate limits how fast it can charge, so speed and hold time pull in opposite directions.[490][490] In a 555, the timing capacitor charges through the resistor network to the two-thirds threshold and is dumped by the discharge pin, and the same part discharged through a second steering diode gives independent control of the PWM duty cycle.[555][392] The recommended 10 nF capacitor from pin five to ground is not optional decoration.[160]

Switched-capacitor circuits use capacitors as charge carriers rather than filters, producing voltage doublers and inverters from a microcontroller pin, with output ripple set by the capacitor values and switching frequency and the values chosen from the discharge rate of the load.[483][473] A charge pump inside an IC needs its external capacitor to function at all.[5v8qX9PrjnA] The Cockcroft-Walton multiplier stacks the same principle: because each stage sees only the input swing, the capacitors and diodes need only be rated for the input voltage rather than the multiplied output, though output voltage sags with load depending on capacitor value and switching frequency.[469] Energy harvesting stores what it scavenges in capacitors before delivering it in a usable burst.[664][MCyLO-1grEk] The same buffering keeps a solar calculator alive under 20 lux, at the cost of a turn-on delay proportional to how much capacitance is fitted.[1094]

At high frequency the capacitor need not be a component at all. In distributed-element filters, a pad on the board is the capacitor and the trace leading to it is the inductor, their values set by geometry, controlled impedance and the distance to the ground plane, producing an LC low-pass or band-pass structure with no discrete parts fitted.[391][823][470] Two pads facing each other across the board are a capacitor whether or not that was the intent, which is how a signal crosses a gap that appears unconnected.[470][1415]

Capacitors also serve as sensors and transducers. A pyroelectric infrared element is a capacitor whose ferroelectric dielectric produces a charge displacement, and hence a voltage, in response to thermal energy, feeding an RC network with a time constant of around one second.[275] A condenser microphone capsule is a polarised capacitor — 200 V for a measurement microphone, perhaps 60 to 90 V for a studio capsule — in which a pressure wave closes the plate spacing, raising the capacitance while the charge is preserved, so the voltage must fall.[609] A ceramic piezo bender is likewise just a capacitor, and can be driven to emit sound as a continuity test.[1400] Placing a capacitor in series is the whole of AC coupling, as in the AC-coupled input range that older analog meters labelled output.[1067] Series strings of capacitors are used for safety as well as ratings: a high-voltage differential probe uses several 6.8 pF 1 kV ceramics in series across a chain of 1 megohm resistors, so a single failure does not compromise the isolation.[932][932]

## Selection and design practice

Capacitor choice is usually a systems decision rather than a component one. Output capacitance stabilises a control loop and cleans a transient — replacing 22 microfarads with 100 nanofarads on a lab supply output turns a clean response into a badly degraded one, and removing it entirely is worse still — while a large-value output capacitor gives a smooth switch-on that a small one or none at all will overshoot.[224][225][577] Cheap supplies sometimes simply add output capacitance until the loop is stable.[1174]

Where a design has freedom, a resistor is generally the cheaper and more versatile way to set an RC constant than a high-value capacitor, so a 10 megohm resistor with a small capacitor is preferred over 100 kilohms with tens of microfarads.[471] Component consolidation is worth doing deliberately: substituting a value already in the bill of materials for a nearby one that is not — a 10 microfarad where 4.7 would do — reduces part count.[232][1307] A design carrying 26 different capacitor types consumes half the reel capacity of a pick-and-place machine that supports only 40, which is immaterial for a hundred boards and decisive for a hundred thousand.[1307] Higher temperature ratings are a genuine benefit where the parts are actually to specification rather than merely stamped with a number.[400]

Finally, notation. Values are written in nanofarads rather than as fractional microfarads because decimal points are lost in printouts — 10 nF rather than 0.01 µF — a convention that dates only from the nanofarad's adoption in schematics in the early to mid 1980s.[1129]
