# reverse engineering

Reverse engineering is the recovery of a design — a schematic, a pinout, a bus protocol, a firmware image — from a finished product, using only the physical article and whatever instruments are on the bench. It exists because the information usually is not published: manufacturers withhold schematics even from their own dealers,[1667][912] service manuals for a given model often simply cannot be found,[1602] and undocumented salvage hardware carries no documentation at all.[717][641] The practical payoff is repair without a schematic,[1602][1672] reuse of a working subassembly instead of redesigning it,[1491][1489] and the ability to say what a design actually does rather than what its marketing claims.[675][1667]

## Scope: partial by design

A complete, verified schematic is almost never the goal. The normal working result is a partial trace-out of the one subsection that answers the question — an oscilloscope's analog front end rather than its whole board,[675][1639] a multimeter's input path rather than its full measurement chain,[1667] a few subsections of an inverter rather than its entire topology.[1620] Such results are stated with their accuracy caveat attached: not 100 percent, but "good enough for Australia" for the purpose at hand,[1598] close enough for a specific experiment,[909][530] enough to find out the one thing worth finding out.[675]

The decision to stop is itself an engineering judgement. Work is abandoned when the signal paths run somewhere convoluted and the effort would balloon out of proportion to the question,[966] when getting at the circuit would require ripping the whole guts out of the unit,[1293][1294] or when the answer simply is not needed to proceed.[470][1639]

## Method

For a simple single- or double-sided board the traces can be followed by eye and held in memory, checking off what has been done.[714] For anything more complex, the standard aid is a photocopy of both sides, with the trace-out done on paper so that completed nets can be marked off as they are resolved.[714] A refinement is a printed transparent overlay registered against the board, or a fade between top-side and bottom-side images, with a handful of measurements taken to confirm the reading.[1415] Under any method, high-resolution photographs of both sides are sufficient raw material for a competent third party to reconstruct a front end without touching the hardware.[824][674][1038][976] A light box is standard bench equipment for the inspection work this requires.[Lrhee7hz53U]

Obstacles are physical. White and black solder masks make traces genuinely hard to see and slow the work down.[330][1460] Multilayer construction hides runs entirely, and where an X-ray machine is unavailable the buried connections must be inferred.[675] Traces that pass under components force desoldering of those parts before the net can be resolved.[1313] Vias that drop under a switch body end the visual trail outright.[1360]

Where the unit still works, probing beats tracing. An operational device can be powered up and its interface probed live to capture the data format directly, which is far quicker than deriving it from copper.[488][641] The same approach recovers bus traffic on a proprietary serial link.[297] It can be pushed further by cutting away the parts that are in the way — tearing out a transformer, wiring in bench power, and taking the output of the final stage amplifier directly.[660]

## Cost and error

The effort is routinely underestimated. A single scope front end represents many hours rather than an hour or two, consumed by red herrings and dead-end traps, and the work itself is tedious.[675] Getting a mains power supply schematic out of a board is not conceptually hard but is very time-consuming to do correctly.[1313]

Errors in the result are close to certain. The honest position is that a recovered schematic almost certainly contains a mistake or two; being sure would require checking it, having someone else check it independently, and then simulating it to confirm the configuration works and nothing was left out.[675] The characteristic failure is a single misread connection propagating into a wrong conclusion.[714] Familiarity with standard circuit building blocks is the main defence: when a recovered fragment does not make sense as a known topology, the goof is usually obvious, and without that knowledge it is much harder to spot.[1415]

Skipping the trace-out has a measurable cost. Identifying a blown resistor by colour band alone, without first establishing that it sat in a Zener dropper, produced a value wrong by at least an order of magnitude — basic reverse engineering would have placed the correct value in the tens of kilohms.[1423] Similarly, a discharge path that was missed on the first pass through a charger board was found immediately on a second, more careful look.[812]

## Representative results

The Rigol DS1054Z front end was traced to establish how bandwidth limiting was implemented; the same work showed the channel has no variable-gain amplifier, with gain control instead performed digitally inside the ADC chip.[675][1228] The Brymen BM2257 low-impedance mode was resolved to a 2.3 kΩ path, in contrast to the roughly 10 MΩ presented at the IC in the earlier BM235.[1667] A metal detector's drive circuit came down to a 10 nF capacitor directly across the coil, forming the LC tank needed to resonate.[714] A fast NiMH charger proved to use two N-channel MOSFETs across each AA holder, fed from a DC-DC constant-current source into the centre tap — a minimalistic and clever arrangement for per-cell charge and discharge.[811] An aircon control panel was found to encode its keypad as a resistor-divider read by the microcontroller's internal ADC, rather than the scanned matrix expected.[1360] An EPIRB's test-mode detection reduced to two transistors and an AC coupling capacitor off the antenna.[368]

Firmware is reverse engineered by the same logic at a different layer: a multimeter's binary was decompiled, understood, and modified to fix slow auto-ranging;[3MD88EYLdQQ] an ARM board's firmware was rewritten from scratch after the original was analysed;[1207] and an ASIC miner chip with no public datasheet was worked out well enough for an independent open-source operating system to be written for it.[0h7lKgIFFXA] Whole-product analyses of this kind run to hundreds of pages of detail.[1581] Salvaged display modules are a standing target, since a bare vacuum fluorescent panel without its controller requires the high-voltage drivers, the multiplex, and the AC filament supply to be worked out before anything can be shown at all.[1601][717]

## Countermeasures and their limits

Manufacturers obstruct the work by laser-marking part numbers off chips, rebranding standard parts under their own logo, potting assemblies, and designing enclosures to be effectively unopenable.[391][511][360][893][669] None of it holds long. A lasered-off part can usually be narrowed down in a few hours from its expected function in the block diagram, its package type, and its pin count.[391] Sanding the markings off a wideband RF detector front end leaves a circuit that is probably not hard to recover anyway.[956] A design that does not obfuscate its parts at all is simply traced from photographs.[674]

The measures that do work operate on cost rather than possibility. Firmware locked behind blown security fuses can still be extracted, but the difficulty is high enough that a copyist may as well take the idea and re-engineer it from scratch — which is the practical argument for firmware protection over patents as a defence.[106][500] Dedicated cryptographic chips carry hardware protections built into the die specifically against penetration.[1048] Attacking those requires purpose-built equipment for voltage-spiking parts and probing security fuses.[720]

Secrecy in a communications protocol is a weaker defence still. Where a product relies on an undisclosed protocol for security, a hostile party will reverse engineer it regardless, so withholding the specification from legitimate users buys nothing — the same lesson the lock industry learned, that mechanisms become more secure once their vulnerabilities are openly discussed.[1407][k4zrmTsZj_s]
