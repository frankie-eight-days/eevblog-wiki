# fft

The fast Fourier transform is the algorithm that converts a captured time-domain record into a frequency-domain display, and on an oscilloscope it appears as a math function that turns the instrument into an approximate spectrum analyser.[662][845] It rests on Fourier's theorem, which holds that any wave shape whatsoever is composed of sine waves, so a square wave is in fact a sum of sine waves at many different frequencies.[1417] Practically every digital storage oscilloscope on the market carries an FFT capability, alongside the other math operators — add, subtract, multiply, divide, differentiate, integrate, square, square root, absolute value, logarithms and low-pass filtering.[199][662]

## How it works in an instrument

A scope with software FFT captures the entire waveform record, which may extend well off the screen depending on the memory depth, processes it, and displays the resulting frequency domain.[199] The same architecture underlies dedicated dynamic signal analysers: front-end gain, a wide dynamic range analogue-to-digital converter, a signal source, and then digitising and FFT processing on the result — which is why so many of them are now simply a small box on a USB port driving a PC.[523] Inside a spectrum analyser the transform typically happens in the same FPGA that performs the Hilbert transform, filtering, video bandwidth, log and detector stages.[892][1109]

The processing load is real enough that instrument designers throw dedicated silicon at it. The Rigol DS1052E uses an Analog Devices Blackfin DSP because a DSP is far quicker at processing FFTs than a general-purpose processor.[37] An ultrasound machine's sample space processor handles FFTs along with Doppler colour imaging and sampling.[1314] Modern mid-range scopes lean on FPGAs: the Xilinx Zynq in the Siglent SDS1104X-E and SDS1202X-E is what makes a one-megapoint FFT possible at a $399–$499 price point.[1042][985] Keysight instead does it in the MegaZoom ASIC, where the 64K-point FFT runs entirely in real time in hardware alongside hardware protocol decoding.[976]

## Point count and frequency resolution

The number of points, equivalently the number of frequency bins, sets how finely the spectrum can be discriminated.[845] The spread across instruments is wide: 8K points on the Teledyne LeCroy WaveJet Touch 354, 64K on the Keysight InfiniiVision series, 128K on the Siglent SDS1104X-U, one megapoint on the GW Instek GDS-1104B, the Siglent SDS1104X-E, the Rigol MSO5000 and the Rigol DHO800, and two megapoints on the Siglent SDS5000X and the Tektronix MDO3000.[845][1188][1355][824][1042][1146][1566][1220] An 8K-point result is effectively unusable — no amount of tuning the settings recovers a legible spectrum from it.[845] Some instruments do not publish the figure at all, in which case the only clue is how slowly the display updates.[845][1218]

The same trade-off appears on dedicated analysers as a line count. On the HP 35660A dynamic signal analyser, a 102.4 kHz span divided across 400 lines of resolution means measurements can only be taken at those discrete frequency points, which is visible as sparse data at the low end of a log-scaled X axis.[528]

## Speed, and why it depends on the hardware

Point count and update speed pull against each other. The Tektronix MDO3000 computing two million points is markedly slow, while the GW Instek doing one million was more than twice as fast.[845] Keysight's 64K-point FFTs are dwarfed on paper by the two-megapoint Siglent and one-megapoint Rigol, but because they are hardware-based they are far quicker, which makes the Keysight the likely choice where fastest updating matters more than pure resolution.[1218]

Where the FFT runs decides what happens to the waveform update rate when it is switched on. On a scope that does the work in an ASIC, it does not matter what is enabled — FFTs, cursors, measurements, other processing — because the main processor never handles it.[1478] A Keysight instrument with statistics, histograms and FFT all on at once still holds a million waveform updates per second.[1638][383] The Tektronix MDO3000, lacking that hardware, is fast with nothing turned on but its update rate collapses once FFT or serial decoding is enabled.[701] On the MDO4000, turning on the math FFT causes the display to freeze repeatedly.[199] Even a low-end Rigol DS1054Z, though, stays reasonably responsive with measurements, statistics and FFT math running.[703][704]

The FFT's own refresh rate is a separate figure. The Rohde & Schwarz MXO4 computes on the order of 50,000 FFTs per second, and the practical consequence is that glitches invisible on the time-domain trace show up on the spectrum — a runt pulse can be seen disturbing the FFT display, where a Keysight at the same centre frequency and span captures nothing at all.[1529] Enabling peak detection on that display slows the update markedly and the glitches stop showing up.[1529]

## Setting it up

An FFT result is not independent of the acquisition: it depends on the sample memory depth, the sample rate and the time base setting.[845] Getting a usable spectrum therefore means working backwards from the desired span. At 5 ms per division with 65K points the required span is unavailable; dropping to 2 ms per division brings it back.[845] The procedure that yields the best obtainable result is to set the horizontal time base first, then set the centre frequency and span — at 5 MHz per division there is no resolution to be had at all.[845] On some instruments the horizontal controls continue to act on the time-domain signal while the FFT is displayed, forcing the operator onto separate multi-purpose knobs or out of math mode entirely to change the time base.[845][792]

One firmware-level distinction matters more than it appears: whether the transform is computed on the trace data — that is, the displayed data — or on the entire acquisition memory. Switching a Keysight from trace to memory transforms a mediocre result into a good one.[1188]

Window selection is a standard offering, typically rectangular, Blackman and Hann.[1723][797] Gated FFT, where a particular part of the waveform on screen is selected and the transform taken on only that portion, is a Keysight-specific feature.[1218]

## Interface details that matter in use

A dedicated FFT button that goes straight in is a real convenience, and the ability to run an FFT and another math function at the same time — rather than choosing between them — was a notable improvement in the Keysight 3000T over its predecessor.[845][701] The FFT can be taken from a different channel than the math function.[701] Being able to visually turn off the source channel is valuable because the time-domain trace obscures the spectrum, and not every scope allows it.[845] On the MXO4 the number of FFT points per display pixel is set by a ratio, default 1000 for one point per pixel across the capture window, adjustable up to 5933 or down to 100 for roughly ten pixels per frequency point and correspondingly greater discrimination.[1529]

Rough edges are common. The LeCroy WaveJet has a bug in which the trace vanishes while the waveform continues updating.[792] On the Rigol DHO800 the FFT keeps updating live while the display is moved, which is unusual and welcome, but the cursors have no FFT-specific behaviour and do not snap.[1566] On the Keysight MegaZoom V instrument, moving a fourth channel stops the FFT running on channel one.[1638] The standing engineering judgment across this class of instrument is blunt: "I generally hate FFT modes on scopes".[792]

## What a scope FFT is not

There is no substitute for a proper spectrum analyser.[143] The mixed-domain oscilloscope makes the distinction concrete: the MDO4000 is not merely an FFT function but carries a true RF spectrum analyser front end, capturing a genuine RF frequency spectrum at each point in time.[199] A regular DSO with FFT could not reach a 2.4 GHz carrier even with sufficient sample rate, because the sampling time required would be enormous.[199] On the MDO3000 the 100 MHz model includes at least a 100 MHz spectrum analyser front end, with better performance than the FFT built into a regular scope; an 8-bit converter yields only around 50 dB of dynamic range at best.[587] The MDO3000's RF path is still digital-sampling and still performs an FFT, but with specific hardware and software behind it, which is why it updates far faster than a sweep-based system despite the scope itself being slow.[845]

Some analysers offer both approaches: the Siglent SSA3021X can be placed in FFT mode or sweep mode or left in auto, where the Rigol DSA815 has no such choice.[891] In a calibration-grade phase noise system, using a different FFT gives better dynamic range than a normal D-to-A converter and hence a lower close-in noise floor.[1041]

FFT is also the wrong tool for some jobs it superficially fits. Obtaining a frequency response plot is not one of its applications — the conventional method needs only a multimeter and a function generator, sweeping the input and recording values at spot frequencies.[396]

## Applications

An FFT display turns a scope into a workable EMC troubleshooting setup when paired with a home-made near-field probe costing about $10, removing the need for a $1,000 to $1,500 spectrum analyser for live probing.[1188] Doing this well requires a fast FFT mode with a high point count and real-time updating; a slow, low-resolution implementation can be pressed into service but is far less pleasant.[1188]

Other uses run from noise measurement — reading an opamp's input noise voltage spectrum down to DC on a log X axis[528] — to acoustic work, where the spectrum of ambient lab noise reveals low-frequency content filtering in from outside and a ventilation fan adds a broad peak with content in the 500–600 Hz and 1400 Hz regions rather than any single tone.[1745] In a Bulova Accutron teardown the transform confirmed a 300 Hz tuning-fork component present beneath a dominant 100 Hz signal that digital filtering alone could not separate out.[920] At the other extreme of scale, SETI@home distributed the enormous FFT and frequency-bin processing load across tens of thousands of idle home computers.[871]

FFT results are also a diagnostic on the acquisition system itself. In an interleaved-clock sampling architecture, any jitter in the stagger clocks manifests in the sampling and produces errors in the FFT of the signal, which is why such systems demand very low jitter and accurately spaced clocks.[19]

## The low end

Below a certain price the function is nominal. The FNIRSI DPOX180H technically shows an FFT but nothing useful can be done with it.[1582] The Digilent OpenScope MZ's is rudimentary.[1056] The AlienTek DM40 gives a basic display with selectable windows and visible harmonics but no cursor functionality.[1723] The Uni-T UPO3000 offers windows and a split or full-screen view and little else, with no indication of point count.[1231] The Owon SDS is not worth the trouble.[480] Even a scope with 14 megabytes of sample memory can have an FFT that behaves almost like a toy.[845] The general lesson from comparing implementations across instruments is that entry-level FFT modes are largely toys — a carrier can be detected, but basic FM sidebands cannot be resolved.[845]

At the other end, the Rohde & Schwarz MXO4's FFT is streets ahead of most scopes and feels like a real spectrum analyser rather than a mode that kind of sort of works, which is unsurprising from a company whose traditional market is RF and spectrum analysis.[1529] The Digilent Analog Discovery, with its 14-bit ADC at 100 MS/s, does real-time FFTs and can take a transform of just the contents of a zoom window.[692] Reasonable FFT capability sits comfortably within the expected feature set of a general-purpose scope, alongside logic analyser channels and a couple of hundred megahertz of bandwidth.[ln_XJDPKJlc]
