# series connection

A series connection joins components end to end so that the same current flows through every element in the chain while their voltages add.[932][212] It is the standard way of building a useful voltage out of devices that individually produce or withstand only a small one — cells, solar cells, LEDs, capacitors, thermoelectric pellets — and equally the standard way of building an adjustable total from fixed steps, as in a decade resistance box.[212][1586] Its defining weakness follows directly from the shared current path: every element carries the whole current, so the chain is only as good as its worst member.[1217][1426]

## Adding voltages

Stacking cells is the most common application. Two lithium thionyl chloride cells at 3.6 V nominal give 7.2 V, comfortably more than the 6.5 V a meter needs.[WWMXJLhPVdA] A 9 V alkaline battery is internally a series stack of smaller cells spot welded together.[518] Model aircraft packs follow the same pattern, a three-cell pack being three cells in series for 11.1 V nominal.[397] Battery holders provide the series link mechanically, with a terminal plate joining adjacent cells.[292] Coin cells are stacked the same way where 3.3 V is insufficient and 5 V is wanted.[1286] Supercapacitors are stacked to reach a rail voltage: two 2.7 V parts must be in series to run anything above 2.7 V, such as a 3.3 V or 5 V rail.[1511][WFVfbu1Xz3A] Stacking supercaps to reach 3.2 V for a memory LCD and its microcontroller halves the available capacity, which can make the arrangement useless — 100 µAh at 2 µA is only 50 hours.[1242]

Photovoltaics are built series-first at every level. A silicon solar cell is effectively a diode producing about 0.6 V, and a typical residential panel strings 60 of them in a 6-by-10 arrangement to give roughly 36 to 40 V.[1426] Panels are then strung in series into an array: twelve panels produce a nominal 450 V DC, which is dangerous in its own right.[1426][1217] The cells within a panel are connected by a single silver strip zigzagging down and back so that the two terminals emerge at opposite corners.[1385] Solar shingles have traditionally been installed in the same series configuration.[938] Small amorphous cells behave identically at small scale, three in series giving 3.4 V.[48]

Thermoelectric modules are a special case: the bismuth telluride pellets are electrically in series but thermally in parallel, forming a long series chain of PN junctions across the metallized ceramic substrate via printed conductive traces.[664] A module may contain 50 or 100 pellets in series.[664] Thermoelectric harvesters can likewise be strung to raise output voltage, but stringing does not create energy that is not there: at roughly 2 µW per square centimetre, a handful of elements still yields only single-digit to ten microwatts.[1383]

LEDs are almost always driven in series rather than parallel. A series string needs a higher compliance voltage but a much lower current, so the wiring can be thinner and the board easier to manufacture — around 50 mA for a laptop backlight string rather than the far higher current a parallel arrangement would draw.[pKV_JiauAE4] The same logic drives DIY lighting: six 6 V LED strips in series need a 36 V supply.[1372][6ZH2KeplSrs] Where a panel is too large for one string, it is broken into parallel strings of twelve LEDs each, driven at constant current because constant voltage would be unworkable.[1343] A single indicator LED gets a series resistor in the lead, a 2k2 costing about 2 mA extra from the battery.[182]

Bench instruments are stacked the same way. Two 30 V power supply modules wired in series give a 60 V output, and three would give 90 V.[755] Two channels of a 30 V, 3 A supply in series reach 60 V, enough to test a 48 V input regulator.[895] A 0 to 100 V, 2 A per channel supply becomes 0 to 200 V at 2 A in series, or 0 to 100 V at 4 A in parallel.[1402] Not every instrument permits it — a battery emulator limited to a single solar-panel pole cannot have multiple ones put in series.[Y2rcx4vKxlc] Transformer primaries use the same trick in reverse: a split primary wired in parallel gives the 115 V configuration, and a jumper change putting the two coils in series gives 240 V, doubling the measured primary resistance from 45 Ω.[1189]

## Adding voltage ratings

Series-connected passives share the applied voltage, so their ratings add. Where a part above 1 kV is hard to obtain, three 1 kV capacitors in series give a 3 kV total, at the cost of a capacitance divided by three.[932] DMM input networks whack resistor arrays in series for extra standoff, each element contributing a couple of hundred volts up to a 1000 V rating.[1382] A defibrillator capacitor bank uses five 400 V working-voltage capacitors in series for a 2000 V maximum.[909] Zener diodes add likewise: a 30 V and a 20 V part in series give a total 50 V drop.[952] Series MOSFETs appear where standoff rather than current handling is the requirement, drain connecting to source along a P-channel pair and the same for the N-channel pair.[1005] The same principle extends to non-isolated converters: eight LLC stages in series produce an isolated 800 V assembly even though no individual stage is isolated between primary and secondary.[1737]

## The weakest-link problem

Because current is forced through every element, one bad member takes down the whole chain. If a single panel in a twelve-panel string fails, the string dies and the array produces nothing.[1217] Shading has the same effect without any failure: the string current is still forced through the shaded cell, and a very slight, diffuse shadow — from an antenna pole or even a guy wire — is enough to upset a string inverter's entire array.[1426][NoIjTK249D0] Panels therefore carry bypass diodes, typically three per panel, to route current around a shaded section.[1426][938]

Batteries in series fail in a nastier way. Cells from the same packet differ slightly in capacity and ESR, so discharging a long series string drives the weakest cell into reverse charge once it is exhausted.[hSkaZEgrZkY] Discharging thirteen packs of two cells at a constant 100 mA is attractive because it tests everything at once, but with unknown and mismatched capacities across brands the result is unreliable — a string expected to read about 26 V read zero while 44 mA still flowed.[1296][RFb3TwWzza0][XDjyY48u0PU] Discharging in small matched groups, two at a time, avoids the problem at the cost of time.[hSkaZEgrZkY] The same mismatch is why multi-cell packs need balancing: an unbalanced cell in a three-cell pack shortens the pack's life or ends it violently, so a charger buffers and taps each cell node individually.[397] Production packs apply this at both levels, four parallel strings in series for a 12.8 V pack with the management system tapping each string.[8P8Af5SR57U] Series supercapacitors get balance resistors across them for the same reason.[WFVfbu1Xz3A] Series capacitors carry a poor reputation in industry on these grounds, though they remain a legitimate engineering trade-off.[WFVfbu1Xz3A]

## Series as a construction technique

Decade resistance boxes are wired entirely in series, one decade cascading into the next, so the highest decade dominates and each lower switch trims the total in finer steps.[212][510][1586] Because the elements are in series, the physical order of the decades on the board is irrelevant.[212] Within a single decade using a dual-wipe switch, four resistors sit between contacts and a fifth returns to the common, snaking the whole set into one series path.[1586] Capacitance substitution boxes invert the arrangement, using parallel connection because capacitances add in parallel.[510]

Series connection also serves as a deliberate load or measurement technique. Putting the two filaments of a lamp in series across the outer terminals makes a convenient 24 V, four-and-a-half to five amp load.[gqzZHbEfWDU] A current-limiting circuit is inserted in series with an existing supply to measure its effect on output, against a 1630 lux benchmark.[392] With two supply channels in series, the input powers of both must be summed to get the true total.[895] A thermal cutout switch is placed in series with the load it protects.[1694] And where a device is a two-terminal series element regardless of pin count, as with a dual-anode part whose anodes are internally in series, the pinout distinction stops mattering.[1049]
