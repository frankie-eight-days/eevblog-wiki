# isopropyl alcohol

Isopropyl alcohol — also written isopropanol, and abbreviated IPA — is the default general-purpose cleaning solvent of the electronics bench, used to strip flux residue, finger grime, dried thermal compound, corrosion products and assorted crud from circuit boards, connectors and mechanical parts.[682][437][726] Its standing rests less on being the strongest solvent available than on being the least aggressive one that works: it is cheap, universally obtainable, and the first thing to reach for before escalating to a dedicated flux remover or a harsher cleaning compound.[682] It evaporates completely, so flooding a board with it carries no penalty from the liquid itself.[1497] Every lab should hold a stock of it, on the order of a litre or more at any time.[qsX7tEM6gFA]

## Grades and concentrations

Two grades dominate practice. The near-anhydrous product sold for electronics work runs 99.7% to 99.8% pure and is usually described simply as the 100% stuff.[433][788][qsX7tEM6gFA] The other is the 70% pre-mixed form, most familiar as the individually wrapped medical swab, each carrying a pre-moistened cloth pad.[788][qsX7tEM6gFA] Both are used routinely — the swabs are convenient enough for wiping heatsink compound and tidying second-hand gear that they get used in preference to the squirt bottle for casual jobs.[1280][qsX7tEM6gFA] Bulk 100% isopropanol is inexpensive, roughly fifteen dollars for five litres.[qsX7tEM6gFA]

Where residual water matters, the distinction becomes real rather than a matter of convenience. Cleaning a water-flooded mechanism calls for the 100% grade specifically, because the point of the alcohol is to displace and carry off water that would otherwise keep corroding the metal — a 70% mix defeats the purpose.[lEpmVxMu-PE] Microscope optics likewise call for the 100% grade.[992] For plain surface grime, 70% is entirely adequate.[Bh3gcBr0DKs]

## Circuit board and contact cleaning

On a board, isopropanol is the stock first pass: general contamination, waxes, oils, hydrocarbon deposits and flux residues all respond to it, and only when it fails is there cause to move to a purpose-made flux remover or a stronger solvent such as ultrasolve.[682][1564][437] Standard practice after any rework or repair is a visual inspection followed by a clean.[1418]

The solvent alone is rarely sufficient. Effective cleaning is mechanical as much as chemical, and the companion tool is a stiff-bristled brush — commercially available as conductive-bristle PCB brushes, whose stiffness lets the crud actually be scraped off while the alcohol carries it away.[682][1418][m3sQHx5aMmU] Heavy contamination is best attacked by flooding the area with 100% isopropanol and brushing, since the excess simply evaporates.[1497] Fine work on pads, IC legs, drive heads and connector fingers is done with a cotton bud or swab wetted with alcohol.[903][913][788][zNG_oNhKCBg]

A specific diagnostic use is contamination in multimeter input jacks. Grubby environments — a factory with mineral oil in the air, for instance — leave deposits that create a slightly low-impedance path across the terminals, which a meter's input-jack detection circuit reads as a probe being plugged in, producing a spurious alert or error.[1663][1692] Cleaning the jacks with isopropanol, removing the board and flushing it if necessary, has resolved the fault in practice.[1663] Moisture absorbed into the plastics can cause the same symptom, and a spell in a sealed bag with desiccant is the complementary remedy.[1692] Rotary range switches respond to the same treatment: dismantle, flood the contacts with isopropanol, scrub with a brush.[m3sQHx5aMmU] Suspected excess battery drain in a meter is also worth treating as contamination — a thorough alcohol clean followed by a thorough dry-out.[1449]

## Battery leakage repair

Alkaline leakage is a two-stage job in which isopropanol plays the second part. The potassium hydroxide electrolyte is alkaline and is neutralised first with a mild acid — white vinegar is the usual choice, with lemon juice as an alternative — the reaction being visibly complete when the bubbling stops.[1349][1687][2QZ8GWwzXXY] The vinegar must not be left on the board, so the neutralised area is then cleaned off with isopropanol and water and dried thoroughly.[1687] Where no vinegar is to hand, isopropanol alone will at least clean up the visible mess.[1349] Corroded terminals may still be salvageable if the leak is caught early enough; left alone, plating is eaten away and contacts rot through.[1274][j_eaXfmRB8Q][1497]

Leakage cleaning is one of the few contexts in which the alcohol's own mobility becomes a hazard. Flooding a leaking battery compartment sends solvent through vias and out the far side of the board, onto switch contacts and under the display, and residual water-and-isopropanol mixture beneath an LCD has itself created a low-impedance path and a fault.[1497][1670] The corrective is to spray deliberately rather than indiscriminately, and to dry the assembly out afterwards.[1670]

## Thermal compound and mechanical parts

Removing aged thermal paste from a CPU lid or heatsink is a standard isopropanol job, done either with a wipe or with liquid and a cloth, and it is the necessary preparation before fresh compound goes on.[726][GaMpbx8kMbU][yKflC-fmSX8][1098][1280] The same wipe cleans the printed markings on a de-lidded processor enough to read the part number.[882][rtQ2vTdYBn4][1280] Rubber keypads and elastomer buttons of the kind used on Agilent instruments accumulate finger grime badly; isopropanol wipes plus persistent rubbing will bring them back.[655] Dust caked onto laptop fan blades needs to be moistened with alcohol before it will come off.[q7VQfhkOLm8][zNG_oNhKCBg] Enclosures, screens and general second-hand or salvaged equipment clean up the same way.[463][1424][1425][1346]

## Water displacement

Because isopropanol is miscible with water and evaporates readily, it can be used to drive residual moisture out of a mechanism that has been flooded. The sequence after a water immersion is to rinse with filtered water to flush out the original contaminant, blow out as much of the water as possible with compressed air, then wash through with 100% isopropanol to carry off what remains, moving the mechanism continually so that the liquid reaches every internal cavity.[lEpmVxMu-PE][sxUnA5dn3AU] The alcohol left behind then evaporates on its own, optionally assisted by a desiccant bag.[lEpmVxMu-PE]

## Where it does damage

Isopropanol is a solvent, and the same action that lifts flux attacks other things:

- **Adhesives.** It dissolves glue. A calculator keyboard cleaned with alcohol had the bond between the keyboard and the main board dissolved, disabling it.[697] The same property is useful where the glue is the target, as when stripping a delaminating LCD polariser film.[afwqRu7W8V0]
- **Shellac and watch movements.** Watch jewels are commonly set with shellac, which isopropanol attacks, so the solvent can ruin jewel mounts.[lEpmVxMu-PE] Putting such a movement in an ultrasonic bath with IPA compounds the damage, the cavitation and the solvent working together on the mounts.[lEpmVxMu-PE]
- **Printed markings.** Wiping a part with alcohol can rub off silkscreened or printed numbering.[1546]
- **Mineral and machine oils.** Heavy oil contamination largely resists it — spraying isopropanol may displace some oil but is not a reliable route to removing it, and soaking an oil-soaked assembly is of doubtful value.[2G7Z9IIQoIs][1341] Adhesives holding heatsinks down likewise do not yield to it.[1289]

## Optical cleaning

Cleaning microscope optics is a graduated process rather than a single wipe, and recommendations vary considerably between authorities. Loose dust goes first, removed with a blower bulb so it is not dragged across the glass. Compacted dirt is addressed next with distilled water, and only then with solvents, of which isopropanol is one — in the 100% grade.[992] Zeiss's own recommendation is a different formulation: 85% petroleum ether mixed with 15% isopropanol.[992]

## Disinfectant use

The same bottle serves as a surface disinfectant, which is why it is stocked in hospitals in swab form.[qsX7tEM6gFA] Isopropyl alcohol and ethyl alcohol are effective against lipid-enveloped viruses — coronaviruses, influenza, herpes, hepatitis B — because the alcohol and water molecules together attack the outer lipid shell surrounding the viral proteins and break it down, a process termed denaturing, after which the particle is inactive.[qsX7tEM6gFA]

Concentration matters, and the optimum is not the strongest available. Effectiveness requires at least 60% alcohol, with 70% to 80% the recommended range — which is why medical swabs are almost invariably 70%.[qsX7tEM6gFA] Since the bulk lab product is typically 99.8%, a serviceable hand spray is made by diluting it roughly 70% isopropanol to 30% water in a small pump bottle; a little glycerine may be added to counter the drying effect on skin, since the alcohol evaporates fast and leaves hands very dry.[qsX7tEM6gFA] The commercial pre-mixed 70% product is the same thing.[qsX7tEM6gFA] A 70% mixture is also the practical way to disinfect a keyboard and mouse, which are among the surfaces where contamination accumulates.[qsX7tEM6gFA][1762] Surface disinfection reaches only surface contamination and does nothing about an airborne route.[qsX7tEM6gFA] Regular alcohol spraying keeps the top of a keyboard clean, but does nothing at all about the debris that accumulates underneath the keys.[1762]
