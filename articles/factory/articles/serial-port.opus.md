# serial port

A serial port is an interface that moves data one bit at a time over a small number of lines — in the simplest asynchronous case just transmit, receive and ground.[810][135] Because it costs so few pins and so little board area, it is the default way a processor talks to a peripheral chip, a second processor, a PC, or an engineer holding a terminal program.[853][810] Its practical importance today is less about connecting equipment than about access: a serial port is very often the only doorway into a piece of embedded gear that was never meant to be opened.[p-eLu1z7-cs][CK5nbC_dBWk]

## Serial versus parallel

The trade is bandwidth against pin count and complexity. A parallel interface transfers a byte per clock but demands eight or more data lines plus control, and debugging it means watching all of them at once — impractical with a two-channel scope and a nuisance even with a logic analyser, where probing sixteen channels is its own hassle.[8] A serial interface reduces the same job to one or two signals whose timing can be verified on a single trace.[8] Where a display or peripheral supports both modes and development speed matters more than transfer rate, serial mode is the better starting point: a stubborn bring-up problem on a 128 by 64 graphical LCD was resolved only after rewiring from 8-bit parallel to SPI, at which point the scope showed the timing was correct and the fault had to be in the driver code.[8] Some peripherals share a single pinout across both modes, with the same pin acting as serial input/output or, in parallel mode, as not-connected or reset.[1664]

The same cost applies at the interface level. JTAG boundary scan is powerful precisely because it can read live pin state, but it is a serially shifted system, so it is not fast and not real-time; on an FPGA with hundreds of I/O pins the entire chain has to be shifted through.[499]

## Chip-level serial interfaces

Most of the serial links in a modern instrument never leave the board. A digital step attenuator such as the Hittite HMC624LP4 takes its attenuation setting over a digital serial or parallel interface.[587] A 1.2 GHz PLL such as the Analog Devices ADF4117 is programmed by sending a serial command to select the local oscillator frequency.[571] A thermal camera core reads out over a serial interface and signals back over the same path when it decides a shutter compensation is due, leaving the host micro to agree and drive the shutter coil.[669][670] Vacuum fluorescent display panels are driven by serial-input, latched-output driver chips, ten outputs apiece, with a microcontroller behind them.[717] Even a fully digital pyroelectric sensor packages an ADC, a decimator and a serial interface to the host micro on one part.[275]

The pattern extends to instrument architecture. A multimeter front-end chip does only measurement — range switching, ADC, generators — and cannot drive a display; it presents a serial output to a secondary processor that handles the LCD and everything else.[853] Where the measurement section must be isolated, the serial link is the only thing crossing the barrier, through a reasonably high-speed optocoupler, which is why the count of connections over the boundary is so small.[1382] That isolation is not automatic: some supplies route both a D9 and a USB port to the same serial interface with no optical isolation anywhere on the board.[440] Purpose-built parts exist for the isolated case — the LT6820 carries a full SPI-compliant interface over a single galvanically isolated twisted pair, one device at each end.[1110]

Isolation matters most on instruments with floating outputs. A bench supply's rear connectors referenced to a PC, which is itself referenced to mains earth, can cause major problems, which is the argument for putting the serial interface on a separate board that can be built as plain 9-pin RS-232 or as an electrically isolated variant.[232] Serial timing tolerance is loose enough that a ceramic resonator, good to about half a percent, is more than adequate for RS-232.[232]

Microcontrollers integrate serial hardware, but never enough of it — bit-banging is inadvisable in most applications and is resorted to when there are not enough serial ports, or not the type wanted.[9hMsNOwY5AQ] Where a design needs many, the choice of part follows: a Rabbit 2000 was selected for a test system partly because it carried four serial ports on chip alongside a built-in real-time operating system.[1632][1306] Integrated peripherals also fail in silicon. A PIC24F errata, buried at the bottom of the document, stated that serial port two simply did not work and that the pins on serial port one had been swapped, with no fix — the cure was mod wires on an already-built board.[1288]

## Physical ports and level translation

Historically the connector was a D9 or D25.[1053][438] Machines that omitted the standard connector required a purchase: the IBM PC Jr used a proprietary serial connector and needed an adapter cable sold in its own box.[1053] RS-232 signalling levels require a transceiver — a MAX232 sitting next to a TV's service jack marks that jack as a serial service interface,[446] and a MAX202 performs the same job on a supply's D9 board.[440] Because the levels are true RS-232 rather than logic level, such a port cannot be driven directly from a 3.3 V TTL adapter; either a real serial port or a switchable adapter offering RS-232 or TTL levels at 5 V or 3.3 V is required.[485][977]

Modern hosts reach the same ports through USB-to-UART bridges — FTDI FT232 and FT230X parts, STMicro virtual COM drivers, Prolific PL2303 — all of which enumerate as a COM port.[440][592][776][1684] This is where most practical trouble lives. A driver may assign COM8 while the vendor's own software only supports up to COM6.[137] Automatic detection frequently fails, requiring the port to be typed in by hand after reading it out of Device Manager.[1684][115] A port that is visible may still refuse to open, either because the baud rate is unknown or because access is denied.[862][1331] Typical rates in the corpus run from 9600 8-N-1 on small micros up to 115200 on instruments.[1306][1042][1265]

On PCs the port survives as a lab convenience rather than a consumer feature: its presence dates a machine, and it is one of the reasons a dumpster-found office PC is worth taking home. "Nothing beats having a nice good old D9 serial port on there."[Lhmg1jUNxVc][1615][1342][1212]

## Instrument control

For older test equipment the serial port is the friendly option. HP-era gear predates Ethernet and USB and is normally controlled over HPIB/GPIB — IEEE 488 — so an instrument that happens to offer a serial port with a string command set is markedly easier to talk to.[1232] A string-based command set such as SCPI is human-readable, easy to extend, and requires only that a string be sent to the device over the serial interface, with serial libraries available for every platform.[GarToEo6ekQ] Instruments that document their serial command set can be driven from user-written software on Linux or any other serial-equipped machine, which is often preferable to the vendor application.[137][439]

Not every rear-panel serial connector is a control port. On one frequency counter the serial port is only for connecting a serial printer, not for data extraction.[647]

## Bringing up and reverse engineering hardware

On unfamiliar equipment, locating the serial port is the first move. There is no standard connector or pinout for it — unlike JTAG, which is often standardised — so the search is for a likely-looking header, commonly four pins carrying transmit, receive, ground and possibly power.[p-eLu1z7-cs] A row of test points on a production board is frequently serial comms or JTAG.[672] Once found, the boot sequence is the payload: a device that reaches a splash screen is almost certainly emitting boot code, and capturing it typically yields U-Boot output and system information.[CK5nbC_dBWk][1042][485][1101] Serial output labelled TTYS is itself the tell that a machine runs Linux.[458] The technique does not always pay: some devices emit nothing at all from the boot serial port,[1462] some emit boot text that carries no useful information and cannot be interrupted,[1154] and some stall in self-test and never print anything after power-up.[1061]

Consumer hardware exposes the same thing. A third-generation Kindle brought its serial interface out to four external contacts — TX, RX, ground and one further pin — and that access is what hackers used; the successor removed it.[135][205] Four external gold contacts on a tablet serve as a remote serial interface for programming, monitoring, debugging and factory firmware loading.[321] Digital calipers commonly bring a data port out to bare PCB pads, giving a serial output that can be read by a microcontroller.[304]

## Configuration, firmware and field use

Serial is the routine channel for setting up and repairing equipment. A reflow oven's controller exposes serial commands that make every profile parameter settable, and a terminal session comparing its reported temperature against a reference thermometer showed a reading of 36 °C where 28 °C was correct — a calibration error found only because the serial console existed.[558] A clock module's real-time clock was set over serial commands even while its display stayed blank, isolating the fault to a 4.1 V supply rail rather than the communications path.[688][689] A solar inverter's serial port, once the Wi-Fi dongle proved unusable, was cabled straight to a Raspberry Pi running monitoring software.[1628] A USB soldering station enumerates as a serial port for firmware updates and temperature setting, then retains the last temperature for use in the field without a computer.[1646] Where trimmer pots have been deleted from a meter's design, the addition of a receive channel alongside the existing transmit LED suggests calibration is now performed over the serial input.[1378]

Development boards follow the same convention. An Arduino identifies as a generic serial port, and choosing the right one is the only configuration step in the upload process.[45][WXJP_CNYt3o] A MicroPython interpreter running on the target is reached over the serial port from a terminal.[el6CB-h9Lo0] Some cheap 8051 variants need nothing more than two pins connected to a serial port and an IDE plug-in to be programmed.[1013]
