# rom

Read-only memory is non-volatile storage that holds the instructions a computer executes.[1205] In the classic microprocessor architecture it is one of three mandatory blocks — a processor, some RAM and some ROM — with everything else on the board being interface, glue logic and drivers.[641][1008][645][1097][621] The distinction between a microprocessor and a microcontroller rests on where that memory lives: a microprocessor requires external RAM and external program memory, whereas a microcontroller has both built in along with its I/O.[1111]

## Role in a system

On a bare processor board the ROM sits on the address and data bus close to the processor, and its position in the memory map determines where execution begins; on the RC2014 Z80 kit the base address of the ROM is set by jumper links, so that at power-up the processor reads instructions from a known address and drops into BASIC.[1205][1163] That kit runs Microsoft BASIC from ROM alongside 32 K of RAM and a 7.3 MHz clock.[1163] Layout follows the same logic: on the Amiga 500 the ROM is placed immediately adjacent to the processor, with data flowing out through buffer and latch stages towards the I/O.[438]

The dependency can be removed entirely in either direction. The Philips PCF80C31 is functionally an 8051 with no internal ROM, which forces a separate external ROM device onto the board.[967] At the other extreme the Gigatron TTL microcomputer has no microprocessor at all — only a ROM and a 62256 RAM, with every remaining function implemented in TTL logic.[1080] The Texas Instruments TMS1000, the first microcontroller, integrated RAM, ROM and I/O in one part costing a couple of dollars, which collapsed the bill of materials for handheld electronic games to a single chip plus LEDs and a case.[1111]

## Mask, one-time-programmable and erasable types

A mask ROM has its contents fixed at manufacture and is the cheapest and least serviceable option. The IBM PC Jr uses a mask ROM soldered directly to a multilayer board rather than socketed, carrying an 8th-week-1984 date code — before the machine's official March 1984 introduction — so any BIOS change requires desoldering the part; a second TMM23256 on the same board is likewise a mask ROM.[1053] Apple's Newton splits its firmware across a low ROM and a high ROM, both Apple-branded mask parts at version 1.30.[418]

One-time-programmable devices sit between mask and erasable parts. The Atmel AT27C256 is a 256 Kbit, 32 kilobyte OTP ROM; a PDA carrying two of them holds 64 K of ROM in total.[334] Program ROMs with no window on the package cannot be UV-erased and are therefore write-once, as in the Compucorp 322G calculator.[663] Genuinely erasable parts are identifiable by a clear quartz window and must be exposed to ultraviolet light to be cleared, which is precisely why in-circuit emulation was needed to examine or substitute program memory during development.[410] Field-programmable memory carried a price premium of roughly 20 to 25 percent over mask ROM, which was accepted for its time-to-market advantage and for low-volume or high-value products.[d1D3d3YIc9A]

## Capacity

ROM sizes track the era of the machine rather than any single trend. A 1970s 8085 design kit shipped with 2 K of ROM against 512 bytes of RAM once expanded.[1308] Early PDAs pair 32 K of ROM with 8 K of SRAM; mid-generation devices move to 4 megabytes of ROM with 512 K of SRAM; late devices reach 48 megabytes of ROM with 128 megabytes of SDRAM.[334] A 1985 pocket computer offered 32 K of ROM and 8 K of RAM.[936] Integrated parts follow the same pattern, with the TMS320C5504 DSP carrying 256 K of on-chip SRAM and 128 K of ROM.[271] The Compaq Portable's BIOS lives in a 64 Kbit device.[1348]

## Uses beyond program storage

Not every ROM holds processor instructions. A 1970s programmable calculator contains six different ROMs of which only some are program ROMs — the set also includes a keyboard mapping ROM that translates key positions, a character generator ROM, and a printer controller with a ROM of its own.[1153] The same lookup principle drives an electronic translator, where a typed word is mapped to a ROM entry and the mapped word displayed, with separate language ROMs — including an Italian one copyrighted 1980 — plugged in as cartridges.[1003]

ROM is also a substitute for computation. A cardiac simulator stores its waveforms in a one-megabit Atmel ROM and has the microcontroller do nothing but stream them out, which offloads the processor entirely and is effectively required if high-resolution signals are wanted rather than an algorithmically generated approximation.[1133] Test gear uses the same trick, with dedicated waveform ROMs on a generator board separate from the processor board's own ROMs.[1256]

Inside custom silicon, ROM appears as a control store. The Fluke 27's custom analog IC contains digital circuitry that runs a state machine over an A/D decoding ROM, which is what implements autoranging and the modified dual-slope conversion.[372] A Sony video effects processor uses an internal ROM alongside dividers, latches, rounding and square-root hardware within a single signal-processing block.[598] Where a sensor has a non-flat response, calibration constants characterised at the factory may be programmed into the instrument's ROMs so the firmware can compensate.[1366] Security material is a poor fit for ROM, since anything held there is readable by anyone who dumps the part; battery-backed SRAM is the appropriate place for secure keys.[1110]

## Servicing, dumping and preservation

Socketing decides how repairable a machine is. Where ROMs are socketed they can be reseated to cure intermittent contact, and simply pushing them home is worth doing before deeper diagnosis.[536][620] Where they are soldered — as in the Tandy 102, whose ROM carries a 1983 Microsoft copyright — they are not.[1376] Sockets are also the failure point: the Acorn Archimedes A3000 suffers corrosion on the ROM pins and in the sockets themselves, and its four ROMs are numbered so they can be returned to the correct positions.[507] Date codes on those ROMs read 1992 against a board otherwise dated to early 1990, which indicates the RISC OS 3 ROMs were fitted after manufacture.[507] Some designs anticipate the problem: the Cadex C7000 battery analyser places its ROM where it can be reached and swapped without disassembling the surrounding mechanics.[1434]

Reading the contents out before doing anything else is the correct first step with old equipment: dump the ROM, archive the image, and locate a reprogrammable replacement that can be fitted if the original fails.[615] A universal programmer such as the TL866 will read parts it does not formally list, because pinouts and algorithms are shared across manufacturers — the Compaq Portable ROM reads correctly despite its Mostek part number being absent from the device list.[1348][411] A dump alone is not always enough to change behaviour: the Prema 6047's 27C256 image contains no model-number strings at all, so distinguishing it from a related model would require disassembling the 65C02 binary rather than patching text.[615]

ROM and RAM are, by a wide margin, the parts most likely to go obsolete over a product's production life, which makes them the usual trigger for a redesign of a long-lived instrument.[1032]

## Physical and electrical behaviour

ROMs are among the device families most vulnerable to electrostatic discharge, alongside PROMs, CMOS and MOSFETs.[BUW6h88weXU] Thermally they are undemanding: on a running 8085 kit the ROM measures about 35 °C, the coolest device on the board, against 47 °C for the active RAM and close to 50 °C for the processor.[3zAWgNjx5Ew]

Electrically, the address and data lines into a ROM are among the noisiest nodes on a digital board. A near-field probe held directly over a ROM picks up some of the highest-amplitude broadband emissions anywhere on the assembly, well above what a general sweep of the board shows, and the ROM is a standard probing location when comparing two-layer against four-layer construction.[1188][1176] Despite that switching activity, stripping every bypass capacitor from the section of a board carrying the instruction register, the RAM and the ROM produced no functional failure in a low-clock-rate TTL machine.[1081]
