# waveform

A waveform is the graphical representation of an electrical signal's amplitude as it varies with time, conventionally plotted with voltage on the vertical axis and time on the horizontal axis.[UJjMt2-k99c][926] Displaying waveforms is the primary purpose of an oscilloscope, which is a time-domain instrument; a multimeter can only report a single numeric value such as a true RMS reading and cannot show what a signal is actually doing.[1661][926] Because modern digital electronics operates on fast-changing signals invisible to a multimeter, the waveform display is the window into the behaviour of a circuit.[UJjMt2-k99c]

## Waveform parameters

A waveform can be characterised by peak-to-peak voltage, average value, RMS value, frequency, and rise and fall times, all of which a modern digital oscilloscope measures automatically, with cursors available for precise manual time and amplitude measurements between arbitrary points.[926] Several distinct conventions exist for quoting the voltage of a single waveform — peak, peak-to-peak, average, and RMS among them — which is why the measurement basis must be stated when communicating a value.[1417] "The base of the waveform is the lower part of the waveform," a definition relevant to automatic amplitude measurements.[704]

The crest factor of a waveform is the ratio of peak voltage to RMS voltage; narrow, spiky waveforms can reach crest factors of five or more, which defeats the sampling hardware of true-RMS converter circuits and produces erroneous readings unless faster RMS conversion hardware is used.[1417] For a full-wave rectified sine wave, the average value is 2/π of the peak, approximately 0.637.[Ju19Rz8FcBg] Component datasheets also characterise signals graphically: op-amp input noise, for example, is typically specified as a peak-to-peak figure with a typical waveform captured over a bandwidth-limited 0.1 Hz to 10 Hz range.[528]

## Phase

The phase difference between two waveforms is "the difference in degrees between the two waveforms at the same reference location," where one full cycle corresponds to 360° and the reference location may be a zero crossing, a peak, or an edge.[1751] A waveform whose reference feature occurs earlier in time is said to be leading the other.[1751] Oscilloscope techniques for measuring phase include cursor delta-time measurement, automated phase measurements, Lissajous figures in X-Y mode, and computing the arcsine of the ratio of a channel's amplitude at a reference instant to its maximum amplitude.[1751]

## Common waveform shapes

Function and arbitrary waveform generators provide a standard set of shapes — sine, square, ramp, pulse, noise, and DC — with many instruments adding specialised shapes such as exponential rise and ECG, plus fully arbitrary user-defined waveforms.[1701][CMoBGGqojqs][323]

In switching converter design, the switching waveform is defined by its on-time and off-time, which sum to the period; for a 10 µs period with a calculated 2.7 µs off-time, the on-time is 7.3 µs.[110] In digital logic, timing diagrams depict the relationship between input and output waveforms, and physically feeding a gate with inputs of the drawn timing produces the drawn output waveform at real logic levels such as 5 V or 3.3 V and 0 V.[7bVnsXHO6Uw]

Complementary drive schemes produce paired inverse waveforms: the two coil drives of an electromagnetic toothbrush mechanism are inverse waveforms of just over 13 V peak-to-peak, with ringing visible on the rising and falling edges.[284] LCD segment and common pins are driven with mutually inverted waveforms that remain at standard 0–5 V logic levels relative to ground, the AC component across the segment arising purely from the phase opposition.[1045] In a Cockcroft-Walton multiplier, each stage carries a waveform of identical amplitude but with its reference point shifted upward, which is why the diodes and capacitors need not be rated for the full output voltage.[469]

## Waveform fidelity and measurement traps

A displayed waveform is not necessarily a faithful record of the signal. Digital oscilloscopes apply interpolation by default, so a smooth-looking zoomed-in trace can appear to contain detail that was never actually sampled; the displayed points must be checked against memory depth and sample rate before drawing conclusions.[1211] Insufficient acquisition memory produces gross artifacts: a complex amplitude-modulated pulse waveform rendered as garbage at 1,000 samples of memory depth only began to resemble the true signal at 100K–1M samples, and required 5–10 Mpts for equivalence with a scope that manages memory automatically.[617] Aliasing can similarly fold out-of-band frequencies back into range, a hazard of all sampling systems, not only oscilloscopes.[j49T1E4UEv4]

Signal integrity problems also distort waveforms at the source. With a mismatched coaxial load, individual harmonics reflect back with phase and group delay, adding to or subtracting from the forward signal; this produces pedestal artifacts on square waves and amplitude that varies with frequency at the generator output.[652] Probe grounding matters at high speed: a longer ground lead introduces visible extra undershoot and slower recovery compared with a short ground connection at the same point.[1718] Amplifiers driven near their gain-bandwidth limit begin to distort a clean input waveform, which is why gain-bandwidth margin is required.[572] In LCR measurement, an over-ranged drive signal clips the test waveform and produces wildly wrong readings, such as 470 pF reading far off value.[1474]

Deep acquisition memory is the enabling feature for fidelity over long captures: a 4 Mpts scope can record a long waveform and then be zoomed in to reveal detail such as a single anomalous spike or an odd decaying pulse that would otherwise be missed.[307] Display processing can also be used deliberately; an inverted intensity-grading mode makes infrequent glitch energy appear brighter than the main waveform, isolating intermittent anomalies.[601]

## Generating waveforms

On microcontrollers, the practical way to generate an arbitrary waveform such as a triangle wave at the maximum sample rate is to store the shape in a lookup table and have a DMA controller feed it to a DAC, optionally paced by a timer; this runs almost entirely in the background, leaving the CPU free, whereas an interrupt-per-sample approach would saturate the processor.[eUA2TRX1NTc] At the simplest level, bit banging generates a waveform by setting and clearing individual pins in sequence.[9hMsNOwY5AQ] Multiple coordinated digital waveforms — for example the clock, latch, and blanking signals needed by a CD4026-based frequency counter — can be generated on spare pins of a small microcontroller board when a multi-channel signal generator is unavailable.[NvIv-0-R6qQ] An audio file played through a sound card's left and right channels into an oscilloscope's X-Y mode can draw text, scrolling images, and rotating shapes, the two audio waveforms serving as deflection signals.[153]

## Waveforms in service documentation

Vintage service manuals annotated schematics and fold-out diagrams with expected oscilloscope waveforms, DC bias test-point voltages, and timing diagrams, supporting a structured troubleshooting sequence: verify power supply rails first, then DC bias levels at the marked points, then compare live scope captures against the printed waveforms.[863][612][407][381] The same compare-against-known-good principle underlies component-level signature tools, where probe-point waveforms from a known-good board are captured and published so that suspect boards can be checked point by point for a rough match.[-ON1AdmAMOY]

Modern digital oscilloscopes support this workflow directly with reference waveforms: a captured trace can be stored to a reference channel and overlaid in a different colour for comparison against later acquisitions.[1324] This technique has been used to distinguish correct from incorrect key-press sequences in a power-line analysis of an electronic safe lock, where stored reference captures revealed repeatable waveform differences between entry sequences,[771] and to compare probing techniques, where a stored reference trace exposed the extra overshoot and undershoot introduced by a longer probe ground lead.[1718] Fine vertical adjustment, decoupled from the calibrated Volts/division steps, is useful precisely for such waveform-to-waveform shape comparison.[1226] Some instruments can also transfer a captured waveform directly into an arbitrary generator's memory, automatically inheriting its measured amplitude and frequency, so a real-world signal can be replayed.[209]
