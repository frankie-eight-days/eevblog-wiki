# service manual

A service manual is the manufacturer-issued document that makes a product repairable by someone outside the factory: schematics, board overlays, test-point voltages, disassembly and alignment procedures, troubleshooting charts, and — in the best of them — a theory-of-operation chapter that explains how the circuit actually works.[116][1376][1410] Whether one exists, and whether it can be obtained, largely decides whether a fault is traced in an afternoon or has to be reverse-engineered from a bare board with no silkscreen designators.[1602][1527][905] For second-hand and vintage equipment the manual is frequently the most valuable thing about a given model, and its availability is a legitimate criterion for choosing what to buy.[502][756]

## What a comprehensive manual contains

The full-fat form covers specifications, disassembly instructions, theory of operation, block diagrams, timing diagrams, troubleshooting flow charts, alignment procedures, complete schematics, PCB layout, board overlays and a bill of materials.[116][1376][1662][1410] A 1985 camcorder manual carries hand-drawn diagrams showing the removal of every nut and screw, internal photographs identifying individual parts with the boards flipped open, exploded views, and foldout schematics that annotate not only the external circuit but the internal operation of the chips, with test-point voltages marked around them and the signal flow traced across multiple pages.[381] Documents of that density predate desktop publishing and represent a very large drafting effort.[381]

Bulk varies enormously with the equipment. A Fluke Combiscope manual dated 2000 runs 478 pages and indexes itself by section rather than page number.[1452] An Apple IIc manual runs about 560 pages and includes the ROM dump listing.[788] A Sony compact camera is covered by two documents totalling roughly 180 pages, split between a service document and a second volume holding the parts list, block diagrams, frame schematics and schematics.[1429] An A3 colour photocopier manual runs to roughly 1,500 pages.[1629]

Some manufacturers put the same material in the ordinary user manual instead of a separate service document: a Fluke 8842A bench meter ships full schematics in the regular manual, and a Keithley electrometer includes the schematics and the complete theory of operation there as well.[1012][1017]

## Finding test points and unmarked parts

The recurring practical need is identification. Instrument boards frequently carry no silkscreen component designators and no marked voltage test points, so the overlay and pin charts in the manual are the only way to know what is being probed.[905][903][536][1203] On an HP dynamic signal analyser the test points are labelled only as bare numbers such as TP240, and the manual supplies the chart that maps pins to rails — measuring +18.3 V on the nominal +18 V supply and −19.3 V on the −18 V supply.[536][538] On a Tektronix 2465B every voltage test point turns out to sit on a single 14-pin DIP socket, J119, discoverable only by reading the manual, and the unlabelled capacitors require the board overlay to identify.[1203] Calibration work has the same dependency: adjusting the vertical gain on a Tektronix 2225 means finding pot R145, which is not on the board one would expect and is located only from the adjustment-location diagrams.[208]

Manuals also carry the diagnostic procedures. An HP analyser manual includes a 70-page troubleshooting guide and documents the meaning of an ADC gateway error, which resolves to the instrument's source output rather than the ADC ASIC named in the message.[538][540] A receiver manual gives the expected voltage at a test point, against which a measured value can be compared — the first rule of troubleshooting being that thou shalt test voltages.[379] Reset and service procedures appear too, such as holding a dip switch on for two seconds to reset an intercom's firmware.[710]

Mechanical procedure is a category in its own right. Removing the front panel of a Tektronix TDS3054 requires starting at the handle, taking a cap off and pulling a hidden pin out about 5 mm; a Keysight bench multimeter requires prying a specific black plastic clip before anything else moves; Sony amplifier manuals supply disassembly flow diagrams that remove all guesswork from getting the chassis apart.[564][485][1602] Large modular equipment such as a mixing console gets full disassembly procedures for extracting individual boards.[840] Without such a document, a laser fax or similar assembly has to be opened by working the sequence out by trial.[1587]

## Errors, omissions and partial coverage

Manuals are not automatically correct or complete. A Tektronix 2465B manual has two capacitors swapped relative to the hardware, a discrepancy independently found by forum contributor Howard Long, and following it leads to condemning a good part.[1203] Coverage frequently stops at the boundary of what the manufacturer built: an HP analyser manual carries no schematics at all for the CRT unit, only for the HP-designed circuitry up to the input.[523] A Fluke PSU manual has schematics for almost all boards but not for the mains power supply nor the switch-mode base board.[755] Another HP manual supplies a full schematic and PCB overlay but no BOM cross-reference, so the front-end op-amp cannot be identified from the paperwork alone.[529]

Below that, manuals shade into being service documents without circuit information: a Keithley multimeter manual has block diagrams of the analog input circuitry and full calibration data but no schematics; an ion laser PSU manual has theory of operation and no schematics; an Olivetti Programma 101 manual, produced by Alberto Rubenelli, covers only the mechanical side.[478][1381][1705] A Toshiba laptop represents the floor: no schematic, no block diagram, and a service manual that does not even list typical test voltages or test procedures.[1527] Undocumented behaviour also occurs, such as a speaker amplifier chip reporting a fault code through a flashing LED that appears in no manual.[1322]

Where the exact model is not covered, a nearby model often is — an HP LCR meter's B-model manual stands in for the A model, a Sony amplifier is worked on using the manual for a similar chassis, and a Compaq Portable has only a manual for the 286 version whose diagrams do not match.[757][1602][1348] A very large manual is no guarantee either: the power supply section of the 1,500-page photocopier manual could not readily be located within it.[1629]

## Obtaining manuals

Many are free and official: HP and Agilent instruments can be downloaded complete with schematics from the manufacturer, and an Acorn Archimedes manual is available as original digital files rather than scans.[523][507][502][667] Others circulate informally — uploaded to document-sharing sites, photographed from an eBay listing and posted to a forum, or located by a forum member on request.[1672][777][1705] Searching the manufacturer's internal model number printed on a board can turn up a full manual for equipment sold under a different brand.[272] Paid services exist but are unreliable, one having taken payment and then returned a file-not-found error, while a camera manual bought for five dollars turned out to be for the wrong hardware revision.[1246][1429] The Sams Photofact series, published from the 1960s onward, produced standard-notation schematics with circuit traces for consumer equipment, CB and amateur radios.[1364]

For a substantial amount of equipment nothing exists in the public domain at all: high-power PSUs, in-circuit emulators, calibrators, generators and unbranded instruments where no user manual, specification, schematic or service manual can be found.[814][410][544][576][452][1126]

## Manufacturer practice

Sony is the strongest consistent example, producing manuals through its quality engineering department for even inexpensive consumer gear — a 1998 boombox, a portable CD player, a compact camera — that begin with specifications and disassembly and continue into annotated schematics and test procedures.[1243][863][1429][1602] Stanford Research Systems manuals are similarly comprehensive with full schematics, matching hardware that is largely through-hole and built from off-the-shelf parts.[450][358] Tandy manuals for the Model 100 and Tandy 102 include complete theory of operation, schematics, bill of materials and PCB layout, written by people with direct knowledge of the design.[116][1376] Tesla published a complete Roadster service manual including theory of operation and circuits.[1581]

The countervailing manufacturer argument is support cost and liability: a company selling into nearly every country must author the service manuals, translate them into multiple languages, and then staff the phones to answer questions about the schematics it released.[1032] Intellectual-property policy is the other constraint, and the trend among instrument makers has been away from publishing schematics even where older products in the same range had them.[358]

## Restricted access

Withholding service information is a central mechanism of repair restriction. Apple issued copyright threats to anyone posting its service manuals online, removing that information from the internet; Kyle Wiens, who had used those manuals at an Apple authorised service centre, responded by writing replacement documentation, which became iFixit.[1407] A site sharing service manuals in Australia received a takedown notice from Toshiba on the grounds that the manuals were copyright and available only to authorised technicians, and restriction has since extended beyond documents to parts supply and diagnostic software.[1407] Service documents themselves are often marked as intended only for training and service purposes.[1154] Products have shipped to customers with no service manual or technical information at all, leaving field service unable to fix them.[1209]

Regulation has begun to move the other way. France's repairability score, rated 1 to 10, factors in the availability of both spare parts and service information, and Samsung has released service manuals for many flagship smartphones in French while not releasing them in other markets.[1407]
