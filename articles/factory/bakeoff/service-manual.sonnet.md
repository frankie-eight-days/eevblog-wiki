# service manual

A service manual is manufacturer- or third-party-produced documentation for troubleshooting, repairing, calibrating, and disassembling a specific product, distinct from the ordinary operating manual that ships with a device. Where an operating manual explains how to use a product, a service manual explains how it works internally and how to fix it when it doesn't, typically through schematics, board overlays, test-point voltage charts, troubleshooting guides, and disassembly procedures.[523][536][905] Its availability is often the deciding factor in whether a piece of gear is worth buying or attempting to repair at all.[502][756]

## Contents

A comprehensive service manual combines several distinct kinds of information: a theory of operation explaining the circuit's function block by block, a troubleshooting guide, block and timing diagrams, and full schematics.[1662][1169] Where a board lacks silkscreen component designators, the manual's component overlay is often the only way to identify a given chip or transistor.[905][903][536] Manuals also carry test-point voltage charts for boards that are otherwise unlabelled,[538][536] adjustment and calibration pot locations for boards with many similar-looking trimmers,[208] disassembly and complex teardown procedures,[840][485][1757] and, for older gear, a full bill of materials alongside the schematics.[116][788]

## Use in troubleshooting and repair

Downloading the service manual is often the first practical step in a repair, ahead of any measurement.[536] Test-point charts let a technician read off expected supply voltages directly rather than guess at unlabelled rails,[538] and a manual's block diagram can be used to trace a fault code, such as an ADC gateway error, back to the specific subsystem responsible.[540] A troubleshooting guide is not always followed literally, however: faced with a 70-page troubleshooting guide for a known-fault-prone design, a more efficient approach is to reason from what is most likely to have failed rather than chase every step and every red herring in turn.[538]

Manuals are not infallible. On one Tektronix 2465B, the schematic's test-point labelling for two capacitors was swapped, leading to an apparently bad component that in fact measured fine — a fault later found to be a known, previously documented manual error rather than a defect in the unit.[1203] Manuals can also be wrong or misleading enough that reading them is itself the diagnostic step: locating an unlabelled test-point socket on the 2465B required reading the manual directly, since nothing on the board's silkscreen identified it.[1203]

## Variation in comprehensiveness by manufacturer

Quality and availability vary sharply by manufacturer. Sony's service manuals are considered exceptionally thorough, covering everything from full schematics to detailed disassembly flow diagrams, across products from a 1979 Walkman to 2013-era amplifiers and 2015 compact cameras.[752][863][1243][1602][1429] That reputation does not guarantee coverage of every model: when a manual could not be found for one Sony amplifier, the manual for a related model was used instead on the assumption that the power circuitry would be shared.[1602] Stanford Research Systems' gear is likewise associated with comprehensive service manuals and full schematics for older instruments,[358] though the downloadable manual for at least one model gives only a high-level circuit description without schematics.[452] Tektronix manuals are described as ridiculously comprehensive even by the standard of the rest of the field.[1203] Agilent has continued to host full service manuals, complete with schematics, for instruments it has long since discontinued.[523]

Other manufacturers withhold more. One Toshiba laptop's service manual contains no schematic, no block diagram, and lacks the typical test voltages or test procedures found in a comprehensive manual.[1527] For at least one Advantest signal generator, no user manual or service manual could be located at all, only a datasheet.[576] The manual for the Olivetti Programma 101 focuses on the mechanical side of the machine and contains no schematics.[1705] Independent, manufacturer-unaffiliated manuals have also filled gaps in the historical record: Sams Photofact manuals, published by Howard W. Sams & Co., covered CB radios, amateur radios, and other consumer equipment outside any single manufacturer's own documentation.[1364]

## Manufacturer support and access

From the manufacturer's side, producing and maintaining service manuals is bound up with supporting a product across every market it is sold in — Keysight has historically created and translated its service manuals into multiple languages to support customer questions worldwide.[1032]

Access to that documentation has also been a point of conflict. iFixit's founder was denied access to the same Apple service manuals he had used while working at an Apple-authorized service center once he left it, after Apple sent copyright threats to sites hosting its manuals and had that information wiped from the internet; he responded by writing his own manual to replace what had been taken offline.[1407] A similar copyright takedown was issued against a site in Australia that had been sharing service manuals with the public, and manufacturer restriction extends beyond the manuals themselves — Apple has declined to sell replacement batteries to consumers, and Samsung has kept diagnostic software for its phones restricted to channels outside independent access.[1407] Regulatory pressure has begun to change this in some markets: France's repairability scoring system factors in the availability of service information, and Samsung has released service manuals for several of its flagship smartphones in French as a result, without yet doing the same for American or Australian markets.[1407]
